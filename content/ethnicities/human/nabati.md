# Nabati

> **Race:** [[Humans]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Frithre]] (formerly [[Nabatia]])

## Physical Traits
Brown skin, ginger hair, blue or green eyes. Human anatomy is otherwise consistent with the standard, median height range of 5'4"–5'9".

## Element Attunement
Varies.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Also called Nahatians in many languages, due to a historical translation error shifting 'b' to 'h' during a period of rapid Human expansion across the continent
- Primarily reside in and around [[Nabat]] in [[Minalsche Oblast]]
- Associated with the former nation of [[Nabatia]]
