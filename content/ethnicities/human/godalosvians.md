# Godalosvians

> **Race:** [[Humans]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Frithre]] (formerly [[Godalosvia]])

## Physical Traits
White-toned skin, brown, blue, or gray eyes, blonde or light-brown hair. Human anatomy is otherwise consistent with the standard, median height range of 5'4"–5'9", no unique anatomical features distinguishing them from the other Human ethnicities beyond coloring.

## Element Attunement
Most commonly [[Water]], but many other elements are present and regularly passed down within the population.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- The most populous Human ethnicity and the most geographically distributed, living across major cities and towns of [[Frithre]] rather than being concentrated in a single tribal region
- Historically associated with [[Godalosvia]], the first major trade hub on the continent
