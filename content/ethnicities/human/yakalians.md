# Ya'kalians

> **Race:** [[Humans]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Frithre]]

## Physical Traits
Light skin, blue eyes, brown or light-brown hair. Human anatomy is otherwise consistent with the standard, median height range of 5'4"–5'9".

## Element Attunement
[[Water]] is the most common element, though others exist.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Primarily reside in [[Ya'kali]], a tribal village in the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] in [[Zovamnev Oblast]]
- One of the settlements historically controlled by the Almanian Empire before liberation in the third year of the Bygone Era
