# Navaki

> **Race:** [[Humans]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Frithre]] (formerly [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]])

## Physical Traits
Dark skin, black hair, dark brown or black eyes. Human anatomy is otherwise consistent with the standard, median height range of 5'4"–5'9".

## Element Attunement
Varies.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Primarily reside in and around [[Navakeve]] in [[Lomensk Oblast]]
- Associated with the former [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]], centered around the northern [[Orbev Mountains]]
- By Book II, [[Navakeve]] is reported nearly destroyed in the Huozhese advance; a Navaki shepherd named Arkady is encountered on the road near [[Lovyov Forest]], one of the few survivors
