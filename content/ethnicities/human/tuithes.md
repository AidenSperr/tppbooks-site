# Tuithes

> **Race:** [[Humans]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Frithre]]

## Physical Traits
Tan skin, brown hair, light brown or gray eyes. Human anatomy is otherwise consistent with the standard, median height range of 5'4"–5'9".

## Element Attunement
Typically [[Magnetism]], though other elements are also common.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- The most populous of the four tribal Human ethnicities
- Primarily reside in [[Tuithe'la'nat]] on the [[Endall Peninsula]] in [[Mavisktev Oblast]]
- A traditional pilgrimage rite of passage involves crossing the [[Tuithe Islands]] chain to the Grand Shrine of [[Seville]] on the outermost island
- [[Tahlii A'Trentao]], the Magnetism Gemwielder, is a Tuithe
