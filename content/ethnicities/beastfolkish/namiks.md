# Namiks

> **Race:** [[Beastfolk]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Birumiaka]]

## Physical Traits
Resemble deer. Descended from the Third Faunan King, [[Nuveriami]], a buck with long and complex antlers. Namiks inherited antlers growing from the sides of their heads, a small tail of around four inches usually hidden by clothing, and two ears placed just in front of the antlers that are typically halfway downturned. Hair is normally light or dark brown; eyes are normally dark brown.

Traditional clothing for men is a one-piece outfit dyed in desaturated colors, red, orange, green, or light gray and brown, with elastic bands at the ankles and wrists bearing stitched designs, supplemented by beaded bracelets and necklaces and a stitched belt. Women wear a long brown and white skirt with geometric embroidering and a sleeveless top with highly detailed white and black embroidered designs of vines, flowers, and geometric patterns. Shoes have one thick strap spanning the top of the foot.
