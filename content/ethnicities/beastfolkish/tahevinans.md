# Tahevinans

> **Race:** [[Beastfolk]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Birumiaka]]

## Physical Traits
Resemble canines. Descended from the Second Faunan King, [[Wotavi]], a large coyote with four ears. Tahevinans have four ears, two large and two small, positioned on top of the head. They have sharp teeth lining the entirety of the mouth and a short, fluffed tail attached at the tailbone. Hair is normally light gray; eyes are typically slate gray or black.

Traditional clothing is stark in palette. Men wear silver and gold shirts with a large V-shaped gap in the front, beaded in gold and forest green, tucked into black and white striped pants. Women wear blue, white, and black dresses with many metallic piercings on the ears, lips, nose, or eyelids, and occasionally large feathery hats on an animal-hide band. Shoes are open-toed with straps over the top of the foot.
