# Satsawans

> **Race:** [[Beastfolk]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Birumiaka]]

## Physical Traits
Resemble rhinoceroses. Descended from the Sixth Faunan King, [[Tagelndi]]. Satsawans have gray, somewhat rough skin, a long and thick horn growing from the forehead, near-ovular ears on the top-side of the head, and a small tail ending in a small bushel of prickly black hair. Hair is black; eyes are dark brown.

Traditional clothing exposes the midriff for both genders, who wear nearly identical tops: an underarm torso wrap tied at the back and a draped, thick cloth sitting on the shoulders made of a continuous circle of fabric. Shoes are thin, flat, open-toed sandals with a strap over the top of the foot and a strap tied above the ankle, in dark brown, black, or tan. Women wear short, vibrantly striped skirts ending just above the kneecap in reds, yellows, greens, blues, black, and white; men wear similarly patterned pants ending above the shoe leg strap.
