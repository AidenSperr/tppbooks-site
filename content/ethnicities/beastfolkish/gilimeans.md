# Gilimeans

> **Race:** [[Beastfolk]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Birumiaka]]

## Physical Traits
Resemble foxes. Descended from the First Faunan King, [[Shrova]], a large fox with four wings and eight tails. Gilimeans inherited two tails attached at the tailbone, which split into two parts at their ends: long, with a tight, thick, pointed curl. They have two fox-like ears atop their heads, the tallest of the six tribes at a normal height of six inches. Hair is normally orange or light gray; eyes are usually dark brown or orange.

Traditional clothing features extremely colorful, unique jewelry. Men wear a large, baggy light-brown or red shirt and an angled skirt with horizontal stripes in red, orange, yellow, white, black, and blue, with beads wrapping around the ears and arcing down the face. Women wear a slimmer version with an ankle-length skirt and more elaborate facial bead arrangements. Shoes are closed-toed brown or red, worn mainly outdoors.
