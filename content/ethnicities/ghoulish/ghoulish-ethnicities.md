# Ghoulish Ethnicities

> **Race:** [[Ghouls]]
> **Nation(s):** [[A'Amaer]]

## Physical Traits
There are no distinct Ghoulish ethnicities. Ghouls are individuals reborn through the [[Risen]] process after death, restored as their former selves without solid form. They retain the physical appearance, ethnicity, and traits they had while alive, meaning the Ghoulish population reflects the full range of Alatrya's racial diversity across all eight races.

Ghouls have no solid form, but can interact with physical objects through volition; they can touch things, but things cannot touch them.

## Element Attunement
Varies; determined by whatever attunement the individual had in life.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None (by definition)

## Notes
- The sibilant emphasis common in the A'Amaerian language is linked to speech patterns that developed within the Ghoulish population
- For ethnic identity purposes, a Ghoul is classified by their pre-death race and ethnicity
