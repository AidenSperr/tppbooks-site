# Dusk Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Individuals of exactly 50% [[Sun Elves|Sun Elf]] and 50% [[Moon Elves|Moon Elf]] heritage. Physical traits are a blend of both parent groups, varying by individual, coloring, ear structure, and attunement tendency all fall somewhere between the two lines rather than expressing either cleanly.

## Element Attunement
Varies; may lean toward either [[Light]] or [[Shadow]] depending on parentage balance.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Considered a minor ethnicity rather than a fully distinct grouping; the result of inter-ethnic Elven relationships
- [[Dakkai Eletha]], proprietor of the [[Aevatre Ajjael]], is a Dusk Elf
