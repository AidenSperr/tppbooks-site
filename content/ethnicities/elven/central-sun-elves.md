# Central Sun Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Typically have blonde or light-brown hair, blue eyes across a wide spectrum of blue tones, and pale or white-toned skin. Commonly freckled, especially on the face. Inherit the Sun Elf ear structure: thicker top fold, thinner bottom fold, distinctive earlobe, small valley near the corner, large valley near the cavity, and a more rounded end corner.

[[Light]] attunement is the most common element, consistent with all [[Sun Elves]].

## Element Attunement
[[Light]] is the common racial attunement.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None
