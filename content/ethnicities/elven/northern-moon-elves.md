# Northern Moon Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Pale skin, red or orange eyes, jet-black hair that is either straight or slightly wavy. Clear of freckles or blemishes. Inherit the full Moon Elf ear structure: two equal-thickness folds, small thin earlobes, a small valley and large valley placed further apart than the Sun Elf ear, a cliff between the two ear halves, and sharply pointed end corners.

[[Shadow]] attunement is the common element, consistent with all [[Moon Elves]].

## Element Attunement
[[Shadow]] is the common racial attunement.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- More prevalent in the northern regions of [[Othaala]]; mixed in the central regions
- [[Anue Beidaka]] is a Northern Moon Elf: pale skin, red eyes, jet-black hair
- [[Torra Beidaka]] resembles her mother [[Valaeria Beidaka]], who is Northern Moon Elf
