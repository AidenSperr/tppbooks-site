# Sun Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Sun Elves are on the shorter end of the Elven height range, with a median of 6'2"–6'6". Their pointed ears are shorter in length than those of the [[Moon Elves]], with a thicker top fold and a thinner bottom fold, a distinctive earlobe just beneath both folds, and two internal valleys. The small valley is a semi-circular sunken section of cartilage near the ear's corner; the large valley is a sunken section near the ear cavity. The end corner of the ear is more rounded and less pointed than that of the Moon Elves.

## Element Attunement
[[Light]] is the common racial attunement.
