# Southern Moon Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Darker skin, brown or hazel eyes, dark brown or black hair that is either finely curled or has small waves. Clear of freckles or blemishes. Inherit the full Moon Elf ear structure: two equal-thickness folds, small thin earlobes, a small valley and large valley placed further apart than the Sun Elf ear, a cliff between the two ear halves, and sharply pointed end corners.

[[Shadow]] attunement is the common element, consistent with all [[Moon Elves]].

## Element Attunement
[[Shadow]] is the common racial attunement.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- More prevalent in the southern regions of [[Othaala]]; mixed in the central regions
- Distinguished from [[Northern Moon Elves]] primarily by coloring and hair texture
