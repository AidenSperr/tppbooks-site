# Moon Elves

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Othaala]]

## Physical Traits
Moon Elves are a few inches taller than [[Sun Elves]], with a median height range of 6'5"–6'9". Their pointed ears are longer than those of the Sun Elves. They have two folds of equal thickness, earlobes that are much smaller and thinner than those of Sun Elves, and the same two internal valleys (small and large), though placed further apart. They also have a cliff, a point where the two halves of the ear differ in altitude, which Sun Elves lack. The end corners of Moon Elf ears are notably more pointed than those of Sun Elves.

[[Shadow]] attunement is the common racial element, though it is generally less dominantly expressed than that of Sun Elves.

## Element Attunement
[[Shadow]] is the common racial attunement.
