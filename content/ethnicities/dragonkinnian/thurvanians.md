# Thurvanians

> **Race:** [[Dragonkin]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Almania]]

## Physical Traits
The fourth-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity. Descended from the Old Dragon of Water, [[Thurva]]. The only Dragonkinnian ethnicity with an additional anatomical feature: small fin-like webs on the sides of the head, positioned in front of the ear hole. Have light blue to light-turquoise skin, thin scales mostly concentrated on the tail and the sides of the face, a thin and long tail with a wavy tailfin along its top, and blue or indigo eyes.

All Dragonkin share vertical slit pupils, ears that are slight recesses in the side of the head rather than formed cartilage, a median height range of 5'8"–6'0", and black or dark-brown hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Water]] is the associated element of the Thurvanian bloodline, tied to the nature of [[Thurva]].
