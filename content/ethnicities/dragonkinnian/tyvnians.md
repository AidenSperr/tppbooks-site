# Tyvnians

> **Race:** [[Dragonkin]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Almania]]

## Physical Traits
The most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity. Descended from the Old Dragon of Earth, [[Tyv]]. Have green skin in the darker end of the spectrum, many pointed scales across the body, a thin and long tail with a small barb on the end, and yellow-orange or orange eyes.

All Dragonkin share scales in patches or large portions over the skin, vertical slit pupils, ears that are slight recesses in the side of the head rather than formed cartilage, a median height range of 5'8"–6'0", and black or dark-brown hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Earth]] is the associated element of the Tyvnian bloodline, tied to the nature of [[Tyv]].
