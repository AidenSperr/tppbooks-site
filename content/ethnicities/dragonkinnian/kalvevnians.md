# Kalvevnians

> **Race:** [[Dragonkin]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Almania]]

## Physical Traits
The third-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity. Descended from the Old Dragon of Wind, [[Kalvev]]. One of two Dragonkinnian ethnicities with horns, short and rounded. Have dark-brown skin with very little in the way of scales, a thin and short tail, and light-blue or light-gray eyes.

All Dragonkin share vertical slit pupils, ears that are slight recesses in the side of the head rather than formed cartilage, a median height range of 5'8"–6'0", and black or dark-brown hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Wind]] is the associated element of the Kalvevnian bloodline, tied to the nature of [[Kalvev]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Third-most populous of the five Dragonkinnian ethnic groups
- Descended from [[Kalvev]], the Old Dragon of Wind
- One of two ethnicities with horns (the other being [[Shvafnians]])
