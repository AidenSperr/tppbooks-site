# Valevyrians

> **Race:** [[Dragonkin]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Almania]]

## Physical Traits
The second-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity. Descended from the Old Dragon of Flame, [[Valevyr]]. Have desaturated red skin, a thick and medium-length tail, sharp triangular scales, and yellow-orange or reddish eyes.

All Dragonkin share scales in patches or large portions over the skin, vertical slit pupils, ears that are slight recesses in the side of the head rather than formed cartilage, a median height range of 5'8"–6'0", and black or dark-brown hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Fire]] is the associated element of the Valevyrian bloodline, tied to the nature of [[Valevyr]].

- None
