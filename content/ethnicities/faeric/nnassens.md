# Nnassens

> **Race:** [[Fairies]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Qauta]]

## Physical Traits
Top wings are identical to the Alktans'. Bottom wings differ significantly. They have wingspines that favor the upper half, with thinner and more erratic extensions in the upper part and extensions more comparable to the other ethnicities in the lower. Forehead rune is a double-pinwheel shape where the longer wheel appears in front and the shorter, wider wheel appears behind it, with an eight-pronged star in the center.

Nnassens tend to be somewhat taller than the other two Faeric ethnicities, though still within the general 5'4"–5'7" range. All Fairies share brown, blonde, or near-white hair and jade green, turquoise, or hazel eyes.

## Element Attunement
[[Electricity]] is the most common attunement among [[Fairies]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Named for the ancient Faeric nation of [[Nnasse]] and the [[Nnasse River]]
- Tend slightly taller than the Alktans and Tasemans
- The Nnassen wing is the second most dominant wing trait in mixed heritage
