# Tasemans

> **Race:** [[Fairies]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Qauta]]

## Physical Traits
Wings are similar in shape to the Alktans but more compact, with the bottom wings angled up slightly more, wingspines split in the middle and less visible, and a much rougher edge overall. Forehead rune is a symbol characterized by eight diamonds and a cross in the middle.

All Fairies share a median height range of 5'4"–5'7", with hair that is brown, blonde, or close to white, and eyes that are jade green, turquoise, or hazel.

## Element Attunement
[[Electricity]] is the most common attunement among [[Fairies]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Named for the ancient Faeric nation of [[Tasema]] and the [[Tasema River]]
- The Taseman rune is the most genetically dominant rune trait, commonly inherited alongside Alktan wings in mixed heritage
