# Balinians

> **Race:** [[Angels]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Herat]]

## Physical Traits
Two feathered wings attached at the scapulae, rising slightly from that point and draping down to the back of the knee. Pupillary shape is plus-shaped, and iris color is more intense near the pupil. Irises are normally gray, light blue, or light turquoise. Hair is stark white and straight.

All Angels share light or white-toned skin, a defined halo hovering above the head that emits golden or white light (a manifestation of concentrated light and mana, appearing only since the Magician's Era), and exceptionally long eyelashes (sometimes reaching two and a half inches) in the same color as the hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Light]] and [[Earth]] are the most common attunements among all Angels.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Most populous in [[Excemia]] and [[Bovalna]] in western [[Herat]]
- Named for the city of [[Balina]]
