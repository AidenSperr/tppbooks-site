# Atemians

> **Race:** [[Angels]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Herat]]

## Physical Traits
Two wings attached at the scapulae, foldable into two halves, coming down to the top of the glutes. Pupillary shape is a cross with a vertical line and three horizontal slashes. Eye color is normally light yellow or deep gold. Hair is extremely light blonde, nearly white but not quite as stark as the Balinians.

All Angels share light or white-toned skin, a defined halo hovering above the head emitting golden or white light, and exceptionally long eyelashes in the same color as the hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Light]] and [[Earth]] are the most common attunements among all Angels.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Most populous in [[Kalavam]], [[Centrem]], and [[Divinava]]
- Named for the capital city of [[Atema]]
