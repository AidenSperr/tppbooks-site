# Tavirians

> **Race:** [[Angels]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Herat]]

## Physical Traits
Four wings total. The top two are longer and attached at the superiore scapulae, hanging further from the back and covering the bottom two, which are attached at the inferiore scapulae at mid back. The bottom wings are roughly half the length of the upper pair, but fold similarly. Pupillary shape is compass rose-like: four larger cardinal-direction points and four smaller median-direction points. Eye color is normally light gray or light desaturated green. Hair is either light blonde or middle-blonde.

All Angels share light or white-toned skin, a defined halo hovering above the head emitting golden or white light, and exceptionally long eyelashes in the same color as the hair.

## Element Attunement
[[Light]] and [[Earth]] are the most common attunements among all Angels.

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Most populous in [[Colvescum]], [[Novernia]], and parts of [[Divinava]]
- Named for the city of [[Tavira]] in [[Kalavam]]
