# Chaipon

> **Race:** [[Demons]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Huozhi]], [[Othaala]] ([[Baodia]])

## Physical Traits
Backwardly curled horns with concave segments growing on the top sides of the head. White sclera. Skin is usually dark brown; hair is normally scarlet or dark red; eyes are normally the same color as the hair, ranging red to scarlet.

All Demons share a median height range of 5'4"–5'9", human-like ears with a pointed top, and slit-shaped pupils.

## Element Attunement
[[Fire]] is the common element attunement of [[Demons]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Mostly concentrated in the south of [[Huozhi]], near [[Anjing]] in [[Jofiena]], and in [[Baodia]]
- [[Chuntian Zhenfung]]'s father was a Chaipon Demon from [[Anjing]]; her horns did not grow to their full curve due to her half-Elven heritage
