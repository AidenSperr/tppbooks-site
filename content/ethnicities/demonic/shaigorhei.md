# Shaigorhei

> **Race:** [[Demons]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Huozhi]]

## Physical Traits
Slightly backwardly curved horns growing on either side of the top of the forehead, in either one or two segments. Black sclera. Skin is brown-toned; hair is dark brown or red-brown; eyes are red-brown or gold.

All Demons share a median height range of 5'4"–5'9", human-like ears with a pointed top, and slit-shaped pupils.

## Element Attunement
[[Fire]] is the common element attunement of [[Demons]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- The sparsest Demonic population, concentrated in cities near the [[Northern Flamelands]], mainly in the north and northeast
- A quarter-Shaigorheien or Yujeiyan Demon living in [[Kaedoa]] appears briefly in Book I, notable for having only dark horn stubs on either side of the hairline
