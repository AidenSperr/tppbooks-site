# Yujeiya

> **Race:** [[Demons]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Huozhi]]

## Physical Traits
Slightly backwardly curved horns on either side of the top of the forehead, in one or two segments, the same horn shape as the Shaigorhei. White sclera. Skin is normally dark (similar to the Chaipon and Zhumei); eyes are usually bright red or dark magenta; hair is normally dark red or light brown.

All Demons share a median height range of 5'4"–5'9", human-like ears with a pointed top, and slit-shaped pupils.

## Element Attunement
[[Fire]] is the common element attunement of [[Demons]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None

## Notes
- Most prominent in [[Kashteni]], particularly around [[Ranshao]] and [[Piaofuzhen]]
- [[Huolong]] is Yujeiya; the guard encountered at Yufengu in Book II is also Yujeiya, with ash-brown skin, yellow eyes, and two short horns jutting back from above the temples
