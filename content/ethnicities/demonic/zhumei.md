# Zhumei

> **Race:** [[Demons]]
> **Nation(s):** [[Huozhi]]

## Physical Traits
Backwardly curled horns with concave segments similar to the Chaipon, but with black sclera instead of white. Skin tone is around the same as the Chaipon; hair is either maroon or mahogany-colored; eyes are usually red-orange or yellow-orange.

All Demons share a median height range of 5'4"–5'9", human-like ears with a pointed top, and slit-shaped pupils.

## Element Attunement
[[Fire]] is the common element attunement of [[Demons]].

## Sub-Ethnicities
- None
