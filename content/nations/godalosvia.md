# Godalosvia

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Godascher]]
> **Government:** Monarchy
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (former palace along the [[Tuskiev River]] in [[Godascher]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Humans]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Godalosvia was an early Human nation of the Conception Era, one of three Human kingdoms that eventually consolidated into the modern nation of [[Frithre]]. It is historically notable as the site of the continent's first major trade hub and the location of the first stone bridge built at scale. Godalosvia also controlled the Ghoulish territory of [[A'Amaer]] before Ghoulish independence was achieved during the Wyvernal Era through [[Ghoulish Independence]].

## History
One of three original Human nations — alongside the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]] and [[Nabatia]] — that consolidated into [[Frithre]]. The [[Godalosvians]] remain the most populous Human ethnicity, distributed throughout the major cities of Frithre.
