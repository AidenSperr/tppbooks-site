# Alkta

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Mmenazh]]

> **Ruler:** King or Queen (estate outside of [[Mmenazh]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Fairies]]

## Overview
Alkta was an early Faeric nation, one of three that preceded the formation of the modern nation of [[Qauta]]. The [[Alkta River]] and [[Alkta Bay]] bear its name.

## History
One of three early Faeric nations — alongside [[Nnasse]] and [[Tasema]] — that eventually consolidated into [[Qauta]].
