# Kingdom of Navakeveia

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Navakeve]]
> **Government:** Kingdom
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (ruling hall in [[Orbev Mountains]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Humans]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
The Kingdom of Navakeveia was an old Human kingdom centered around the northern [[Orbev Mountains]], eventually consolidating into the modern nation of [[Frithre]] alongside [[Godalosvia]] and [[Nabatia]]. Its capital, [[Navakeve]], persists as a city in northern Frithre today.

## History
One of three original Human nations that merged to form [[Frithre]]. The [[Navaki]] are the ethnic group associated with Navakeveia.

## Notes
- The [[Navaki]] — dark skin, black hair, dark brown or black eyes — are the ethnicity linked to this kingdom
