# Othaala

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Lumen]]
> **Government:** Constitutional Monarchy with National Senate
> **Ruler:** [[Queen Namerethe]]
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Elves]] (and [[Demons]] in [[Baodia]])
> **Favored Deity:** [[The Twins]] ([[Ulmanae]] and [[Arveshia]]), [[Lavi]]

## Overview
Othaala is the nation of the [[Elves]], occupying the westernmost portion of [[Alatrya]]. It is the most culturally and academically developed nation on the continent, with a strong tradition of music, law, and scholarship. The capital, [[Lumen]], sits atop the [[Yavathre Plateau]] in the [[Yavathre Sector]] and is governed by [[Queen Namerethe]]. Othaala built the first democratic constitutional monarchy on the continent, balancing royal authority with the [[National Senate]] since the Conception Era. The nation is home to the [[Baodia]] Sector, the only racial refuge for [[Demons]] within an Elven nation, established following the [[Second Elven-Demonic War]]. The twin deities [[Ulmanae]] and [[Arveshia]] are especially revered here.

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Yavathre Plateau]] dominates the central-eastern interior. The [[Bhahare Mountains]] in the north mark the Elves' earliest settlement sites. Major rivers include the [[Sovithiena River]], [[Alavi River]], [[Plamauthe River]], [[Shobrae River]], and [[Collavajaen River]]. The [[Lovante Peninsula]] and [[Sohbra Peninsula]] extend into the southern sea. Notable forests include [[Pfapohrrv Forest]], [[Daegrakce Forest]], and [[Alavi Woods]]. The [[Slovaera Mountains]] rise in the central-eastern Yavathre area.

## History
The Elven people first settled the [[Bhahare Mountains]] in the early Conception Era, eventually unifying into the nation of [[Otanlla]] before expanding into what is now Othaala. The [[Second Elven-Demonic War]] against [[Huozhi]] resulted in Othaala annexing the [[Baodia]] region from Huozhi, opening it to Demonic refugees. By the time of Book II, Huolong's invasion forces are pushing into Othaala from the east, and the [[Almaeji River]] corridor becomes a key strategic zone.
