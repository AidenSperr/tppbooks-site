# Otanlla

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Knaema]]
> **Government:** Constitutional Monarchy with National Senate
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (palace on the coast of [[Knaema]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Elves]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[The Twins]] ([[Ulmanae]] and [[Arveshia]])

## Overview
Otanlla was the first nation of the [[Elves]], established after the early Elven settlements in the [[Bhahare Mountains]] grew into unified governance during the Conception Era. It eventually expanded into what is now [[Othaala]], and its political framework, including the earliest form of the constitutional monarchy and the [[National Senate]], laid the foundation for the modern Elven state.

## History
The Elven people built their first mountain dwellings along the [[Bhahare Mountains]] in the early Conception Era. As settlements grew and governance took shape, Otanlla emerged as a unified nation before eventually evolving into [[Othaala]].
