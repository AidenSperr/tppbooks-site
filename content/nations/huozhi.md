# Huozhi

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Ranshao]]
> **Government:** Dictatorship, Constitutional Monarchy
> **Ruler:** [[Huolong]]
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Demons]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Kashtet]]

## Overview
Huozhi is the nation of the [[Demons]], occupying the southeastern corner of [[Alatrya]]. Under the rule of the dictator [[Huolong]], bearer of the Gem of [[Kashtet]], Huozhi has launched a continent-threatening military campaign against [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]] during the events of Book II. The capital, [[Ranshao]], sits in the northern [[Kashteni]] region and is the location of the [[Castle Tianshaobieng]], Huolong's seat of power. Huozhi is deliberately insulated from continental norms, with a culture and government largely closed off from the wider Alatryan community. The [[Fire]] element is strongly attuned among the Demonic people, and [[Kashtet's Hearth]], an active volcano in the [[Northern Flamelands]], is a marker of his presence in the land.

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Chomien Mountains]] run through the [[Kashteni]] region. The [[Northern Flamelands]] are a harsh volcanic region where Lavastones are mined, including by slave labor under Huolong's rule, as well as being the location of [[Kashtet's Hearth]]. The [[Dhozhein Tundra]] lies in the north. The [[Baodian Sea]] and [[Bay of Zhouxe]] mark the western coast. The [[Geimang Forest]] lies within [[Kashteni]], and the [[Zhoulon Hills]] sit in the [[Sonueng]] region.

## History
Huozhi previously controlled the territory that is now the [[Baodia]] Sector of [[Othaala]] until the [[Second Elven-Demonic War]] resulted in its annexation by the Elven nation. Under Huolong, Huozhi expanded its military capacity dramatically, eventually launching an invasion of Othaala that forms the backdrop of Book II. Huolong operates under the influence of [[The Low]], who manipulates him as a tool to destabilize the continent's divine order.
