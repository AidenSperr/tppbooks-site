# Frithre

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Godascher]]
> **Government:** Royal-democratic monarchy; Oblasts governed by dukes and duchesses, with the Godascherian Parliament holding a check on royal power; Cities governed by family houses whose business has historically played the greatest role in building said city
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (palace sat on the sea in [[Godascher]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Humans]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Faemelia]], [[Seville]]

## Overview
Frithre is the nation of the [[Humans]], occupying the central-western portion of [[Alatrya]]. It is a significant continental power with deep historical roots, consolidated from three earlier Human kingdoms — [[Godalosvia]], the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]], and [[Nabatia]] — into a single unified nation. Its capital, [[Godascher]], sits at the confluence of the [[Tuskiev River]] and [[Kroviv River]] along the [[Sea of Hornaskia]], extending into the edge of the [[Maviv Forest]] and along the coast, and is home to the largest [[Polaenian Church]] on the continent, the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]].

## Geography (Notable)
Frithre spans a broad and geographically varied stretch of central-western Alatrya. The [[Sea of Hornaskia]] defines much of its northern coast, with rivers such as the [[Tuskiev River|Tuskiev]], [[Kroviv River|Kroviv]], and [[Slobeska River|Slobeska]] running through its interior. The [[Orbev Mountains]] mark the northern edge, and [[Maviv Forest]] runs through the central-northern territory. The [[Yatasha Plains]] and [[Sosseviv Grasslands]] make up much of the agricultural heartland.

## History
Frithre's predecessor states — [[Godalosvia]], the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]], and [[Nabatia]] — were three distinct Human kingdoms of the Conception Era that eventually consolidated into the modern unified nation. Godalosvia became the continent's first major trade hub and the site of the first stone bridge of scale. Human society was among the first to codify labor rights and formalize urban legal systems. The consolidation of the three kingdoms brought together the diverse Human ethnicities under one national identity.
