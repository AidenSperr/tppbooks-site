## Overview
Nnasse was an early Faeric nation, one of three that preceded the formation of the modern nation of [[Qauta]]. The [[Nnasse River]] bears its name.

## History
One of three early Faeric nations — alongside [[Alkta]] and [[Tasema]] — that eventually consolidated into [[Qauta]].
