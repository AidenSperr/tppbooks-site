# Eight Alatryan Nations

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]

## Overview
A collective term for the eight sovereign nations of the [[Alatrya|Alatryan]] surface, each home to one of the eight mortal races. Together they constitute the primary political landscape of the continent.

## Nations
- [[Othaala]] — nation of the [[Elves]]
- [[Frithre]] — nation of the [[Humans]]
- [[Huozhi]] — nation of the [[Demons]]
- [[Qauta]] — nation of the [[Fairies]]
- [[Herat]] — nation of the [[Angels]]
- [[A'Amaer]] — nation of the [[Ghouls]]
- [[Almania]] — nation of the [[Dragonkin]]
- [[Birumiaka]] — nation of the [[Beastfolk]]

## Notes
- The [[Lowworld]] (Prylkya) exists beneath the surface and is not counted among the eight
- [[Ghouls]] are a transformed state rather than a race in the biological sense; A'Amaer's population is composed of individuals reborn through the [[Risen]] process
