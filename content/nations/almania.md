## Overview
Almania is the nation of the [[Dragonkin]], situated in the eastern portion of [[Alatrya]]. Its capital, [[Caimania]], is an extremely large city along the [[Tovlekan River]] in the [[Viva]] region. The Dragonkin people descend from the five Wyverns defeated during the [[Wyvernal Wars]], with each of the five Dragonkinnian ethnicities tracing their lineage back to a different fallen Wyvern. Almania encompasses a wide range of geography, from the coastal [[Island States]] and the [[Sea of Novobriskia]] in the west to the inland mountains and marshes of the north and east.

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Avalva River]] and [[Farilvana River]] flow through the western regions. The [[Ossolovav Mountains]] rise in the northwest, with the [[Katav Lake]] in the [[Katav]] region. The [[Samina Swamp]] sits at the northern edge, and the [[Sea of Novobriskia]] borders the western coast. The [[Kalavian Islands]], [[Tavakav Islands]], and [[Tavervakian Islands]] form the Island States offshore. [[Mount Valemvakia]] is the tallest mountain on the continent, within the [[Valemvakia Mountains]].

## History
Almania came into existence following the [[Wyvernal Wars]], when the five primordial Wyverns were defeated by the combined armies of [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]]. Each Wyvern's death gave rise to a distinct Dragonkinnian ethnicity: the [[Tyvnians]], [[Valevyrians]], [[Kalvevnians]], [[Thurvanians]], and [[Shvafnians]].
