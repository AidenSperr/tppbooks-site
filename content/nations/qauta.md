# Qauta

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Rrroma]]
> **Government:** Parliamentary Monarchy
> **Ruler:** King/Queen of Qauta (palace located in the center of [[Rrroma]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Fairies]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Gyoledt]], [[Vathem]]

## Overview
Qauta is the nation of the [[Fairies]], situated in the northern portion of [[Alatrya]]. Its capital, [[Rrroma]], is an extremely large city in the [[Obbestra]] region and seat of the Qautan monarchy. Qauta is a cold northern nation bordered by the [[Shoneng Mountains]] and [[Orbev Mountains]], with frozen landscapes including the [[Follicce Snowdunes]] and the [[Great Ice Crystal Forest]] near the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]]. Three early Faeric nations — [[Alkta]], [[Nnasse]], and [[Tasema]] — preceded Qauta, consolidating in the early Golden Era.

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Shoneng Mountains]] and [[Orbev Mountains]] define the southern border. The [[Slobeska River]], [[Nnasse River]], and [[Alkta River]] flow through the interior. The [[Follicce Snowdunes]] and [[Rungon Snowdunes]] characterize the northern landscape. Forests include the [[Ssolli Forest]], [[Slobeska Forest]], [[Dalesce Forest]], and [[Tranast Forest]]. The [[Great Ice Crystal Forest]] is a notable geographic feature near [[Qantua]].

## History
Three earlier Faeric nations — [[Alkta]], [[Nnasse]], and [[Tasema]] — preceded and eventually consolidated into Qauta after the Faeric people gained independence from Demonic occupation in the early Bygone Era. The celebration of this independence, the Cauntalli:re Porava, remains the nation's most culturally significant holiday. The rivers [[Nnasse River]] and [[Alkta River]] are named for two of the founding nations.

## Notes
- Coronation Day of the current monarch is a national celebration; towns hold parades or displays of dancing and theatre followed by a district-wide feast held in open plains outside city limits
- Cauntalli:re Porava ("The Day of Founding") is held on the 25th of Czenilevr; it coincides with the national finals of Asyulani, a Faeric aerial sport where Wind-attuned players pass a ball while flying and attempt to throw it through a large ovular goal ring. Teams compete from Qantua, Mmenazh, Sfalncha, Zlanqin, Rrroma, and Amerrnikt
- [[Qirai Alvetavjh]] of [[Nneminik]] is a Church official secretly allied with hostile forces, referenced in Book II
- Faeric government has historically encouraged same-ethnicity reproduction to preserve ethnic wing and rune traits
