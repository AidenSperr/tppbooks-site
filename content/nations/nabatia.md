# Nabatia

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Minalshe]]
> **Government:** Monarchy
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (palace in the center of [[Nabat]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Humans]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Nabatia was an early Human nation and the ancestral home of the [[Nabati]] ethnicity. It was one of three Human kingdoms — alongside [[Godalosvia]] and the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]] — that consolidated into the modern nation of [[Frithre]]. Its capital, [[Minalshe]], remains a city in the central part of modern Frithre.

## History
One of three original Human nations that merged to form [[Frithre]]. The [[Nabati]] — also called Nahatians in some languages due to a historic translation shift from 'b' to 'h' — are the ethnic group associated with Nabatia.

## Notes
- The dual name Nabati/Nahatian stems from a translation error during a period of rapid Human expansion across the continent
