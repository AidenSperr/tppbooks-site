# Birumiaka

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Samaiku]]
> **Government:** High Leadership and Military Democracy
> **Ruler:** A High Leader (palace in [[Samaiku]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Beastfolk]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Erimevia]], [[Lavi]]

## Overview
Birumiaka is the nation of the [[Beastfolk]], organized into nine prefectures each associated with a distinct Beastfolkish ethnicity. Its capital, [[Samaiku]], is an extremely large city in the central [[Obemiva Prefecture]] on the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]]. The Beastfolk are distinguished from other Alatryan races by their animal-characteristic anatomical traits, which differ by ethnicity and regional lineage. Birumiaka occupies the northeastern portion of Alatrya, with significant coastal access along the [[Sea of Samaiku]] and [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]].

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Barumisu Mountains]] form the northern border of [[Tahevina Prefecture]], with the [[Vane Forest]] and [[Battellyakev Grasslands]] to the south. The central [[Obemiva Prefecture]] features the [[Shiona Plains]], [[Missast Marsh]], and coastal [[Eikalive Beaches]]. Rivers include the [[Skosva River]] and [[Dossaes River]]. Offshore, the [[Juvawe Peninsula]] and [[Juvawe Islands]] extend from the eastern coast.

## History
The nine prefectures of Birumiaka correspond to the nine main Beastfolkish ethnicities, each developing regional identities tied to their geographic territory and ancestral lineages.
