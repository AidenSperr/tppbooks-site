# Herat

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Atema]]
> **Government:** Presidential Republic with Council
> **Ruler:** Elected President (Presidential House near the [[Great Ateman Waterfall]] in [[Atema]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Angels]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Dvanithe]]

## Overview
Herat is the nation of the [[Angels]], founded by Angelic peoples in the earliest eras of [[Alatrya]]. Its capital, [[Atema]], sits in the central region of [[Centrem]] near the [[Great Ateman Waterfall]]. Herat occupies a varied landscape ranging from the coastal plains of [[Excemia]] and [[Bovalna]] in the north and southwest to the highland territory of [[Novernia]] and [[Colvescum]] in the east, defined by the towering [[Villeva Mountains]], [[Taseman Mountains]], and [[Thaetrachese Mountains]] that run through the nation's interior. The [[Polaenian Church]] maintains a strong presence throughout Herat, and the values within the Church make up the country's laws and policy.

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Thaetrachese Mountains]] run through the interior of Herat, with the [[Endall Mountains]] marking the eastern edge. Rivers include the [[Ateman River]], [[Solavev River]], [[Colvescum River]], and [[Tasema River]]. The [[Taseman Peninsula]] extends northward into the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]]. Notable water bodies include the [[Excemian Sea]], [[Tavirian Sea]], [[Bay of Balina]], and [[Gaelime Lake]]. The [[Great Ateman Waterfall]] is the most visited and upheld natural formations in the country, with its capital city based around it.

## History
Founded by the [[Angels]] in early Alatryan history. The nation's deep association with [[Polaena]] and the Angelic people has made it one of the more traditionally religious nations on the continent.
