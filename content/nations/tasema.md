# Tasema

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Rrroma]]
> **Government:** Monarchy
> **Ruler:** King or Queen (palace in the center of [[Rrroma]])
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Fairies]]
> **Favored Deity:** 

## Overview
Tasema was an early Faeric nation, one of three that preceded the formation of the modern nation of [[Qauta]]. The [[Tasema River]], [[Taseman Mountains]], and [[Taseman Peninsula]] all bear its name.

## History
One of three early Faeric nations — alongside [[Alkta]] and [[Nnasse]] — that eventually consolidated into [[Qauta]].
