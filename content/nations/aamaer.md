# A'Amaer

> **Continent:** [[Alatrya]]
> **Capital:** [[Spola]]
> **Government:** Communal Boards
> **Ruler:** A board consisting of each family or neighborhood in a city
> **Primary Race(s):** [[Ghouls]]
> **Favored Deity:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
A'Amaer is the nation of the [[Ghouls]], existing specifically to house those who have undergone the [[Risen]] process — individuals deemed unworthy of entering [[Polaena]]'s realm upon death, reborn in Ghoulish form for a second chance at [[Redemption]]. Its capital, [[Spola]], sits on and around [[Lake Spola]] in the central [[Sa'amas]] region. Ghouls have no solid form; things can be touched by them, but cannot touch them in return. The A'Amaerian language, with its heavy emphasis on sibilant sounds, is thought to have developed partly from speech patterns within the Ghoulish population. A'Amaer gained independence from [[Godalosvia]] during the [[Ghoulish Independence]] event of the Wyvernal Era, with the assistance of [[King Colchebushev]].

## Geography (Notable)
The [[Missast Marsh]] and [[Lake Spola]] are central geographic features. The [[Funiketa Mountains]] rise in the south. The [[Allsyenntsk Spring Mountains]] lie to the north of [[E'elsavest]], with the [[Allsyenntsk Springs]] and related spring waters. The [[Assimisian River]], [[Skosva River]], and [[Dossaes River]] flow through the interior. Forests include the [[Alsvama Forest]], [[Hestaha Woods]], and [[Solem Grove]]. The [[Marshes of Rising]] lie within [[Sa'amas]].

## History
A'Amaer was subjugated under the Human nation of [[Godalosvia]] before regaining sovereignty during the Wyvernal Era in the event known as [[Ghoulish Independence]], facilitated by [[King Colchebushev]]. The nation's existence is theologically ordained — it serves as the ground for Polaena's Redemption process.

## Notes
- The Redemption process is ongoing rather than a historical event; A'Amaer's entire social and national function is built around housing the Ghoulish population seeking Redemption
- The sibilant character of the A'Amaerian language is linked to the speech patterns of Ghouls
