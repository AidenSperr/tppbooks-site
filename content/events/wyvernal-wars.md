# Wyvernal Wars

> **Era:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Participants:** [[Othaala]], [[Frithre]], [[Five Wyverns]], [[Ghouls]], [[Humans]], [[Elves]]

## Overview
A series of battles led by the combined armies of [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]] to defeat the [[Five Wyverns]], who had been attacking and destroying Ghoulish, Human, and Elven cities across [[Alatrya]].

## Causes
The [[Five Wyverns]], the primordial dragons of the Wyvernal Era, were wreaking havoc on the continent's settlements.

## Outcome
The Wyverns were defeated. Their deaths gave rise to the five Dragonkinnian ethnicities, each descended from one of the fallen dragons' bloodlines.

## Aftermath
The Dragonkin came into existence as a people, the five ethnic groups ([[Tyvnians]], [[Valevyrians]], [[Kalvevnians]], [[Thurvanians]], [[Shvafnians]]) being the legacy of each defeated Wyvern. The Wyvernal Era drew to a close, leading into the Bygone Era.

## Notes
- The wars eventually led to the instantiation of [[Ghoulish Independence]]
