# Redemption

> **Participants:** [[Polaena]], [[Ghouls]]

## Overview
A holy process created and ordained by [[Polaena]]. Those deemed unworthy of entering Her realm upon death are instead cast into [[Ghouls|Ghoulish]] form and sent to the swamps of [[A'Amaer]], which houses the Ghoulish population. This is a second chance at proving themselves worthy.

## Outcome
If a person redeems themselves in Polaena's eyes during their Ghoulish life, they are "redeemed" and admitted to Her realm upon their second death. If they fail a second time, they are cast to the [[Lowworld]] for an era.

## Notes
- Considered an ongoing, living process rather than a historical event. Redemption is the perpetual mechanism governing the Ghoulish afterlife
- The nation of [[A'Amaer]] exists specifically to house and facilitate this process
- See also: [[Risen]]
