# Second Elven-Demonic War

> **Era:** [[Timeline of the Bygone Era|Bygone Era]]
> **Participants:** [[Othaala]], [[Huozhi]], [[Elves]], [[Demons]]

## Overview
A war fought between the Demons of [[Huozhi]] and the Elves of [[Othaala]] over mining rights in the hills surrounding the [[Almaeji River]]. The conflict resulted in [[Othaala]] taking control of the [[Baodia]] region, which had previously been part of Huozhi's territory.

## Causes
Competing claims over mining operations in the hills near the [[Almaeji River]] along the border of [[Othaala]] and [[Huozhi]].

## Outcome
[[Othaala]] overtook and annexed the [[Baodia]] region from [[Huozhi]], establishing the [[Baodia|Baodia Sector]] and opening the region to a Demonic refugee population under Elven rule.

## Aftermath
The Baodia Sector became the only racial refuge for [[Demons]] within an Elven nation, containing the cities of [[Yanjiang]] and [[Godeng]].

## Notes
- Referenced as context for the Baodia region's cultural character
- The [[Almaeji River]] later becomes a key strategic battleground again in Book 2 during Huolong's invasion of Othaala
