# Electricity

> **Associated Deity:** [[Gyoledt]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Gyoledt]], son of [[Polaena]]. Electricity mana can be gathered, filtered, and stored in citrine stones via Mineral Infusion, a technique pioneered in [[Herat]] that led to inventions such as electric fans and early artificial lighting. One of the more technologically applicable elements.

## Known Users (Notable)
- Vakviva — dual Lightning/Water user
- [[Alvayr Vahamenr]] — a Dragonkinnian father who wields the Electricity Gem

## Notes
- Mineral Infusion: Electricity mana is gathered via a Mana Bank-like filtering technique, forced into a citrine stone, then set in a wooden basin with conducting metal wires — one of the only elements with widespread non-combat technological application c. Book 2
