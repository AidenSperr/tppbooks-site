# Healing

> **Associated Deity:** [[Erimevia]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Erimevia]], daughter of [[Polaena]]. Healing magic works by channeling neutral and Healing mana into a patient's bloodstream to suppress, redirect, or neutralize problematic mana buildup and physical damage. Advanced Healing requires the practitioner to open a mana channel between their own Bank and the patient's, drawing excess or damaged mana out at personal cost.

## Known Abilities
- Closing wounds, clearing infections, and general medical applications
- Numbing painful mana buildup by introducing neutral mana as a buffer
- Channeling mana between Banks to relieve dangerous elemental overload

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Maresi Gratalza]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Erimevia]]; primary healer of the Gemwielder group
- [[Lynety Laythrya]] — skilled non-Gemwielder Healer; treats the Demonic woman Zhalie in Book 2

## Notes
- Healing magic is mana-expensive and physically draining; the healer cannot assist others for the rest of the day following a major procedure
- Water mana replenisher vials exist as a medical supplement but are dangerous without supervision
