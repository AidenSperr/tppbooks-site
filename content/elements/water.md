# Water

> **Associated Deity:** [[Faemelia]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Faemelia]], daughter of [[Polaena]]. Water mana is fluid and versatile, commonly used for both combat and utility. Mana replenisher vials, a recently developed and expensive medical technology, are Water-based.

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]] — bearer of the stolen Gem of [[Faemelia]]
- Vakviva — dual Lightning/Water user
- Shimae — secondary Water attunement, kept hidden in [[Huozhi]]

## Notes
- The Water Gem was originally gifted to [[Lynaria Praczaya]], a servant of the Szakuvya Family Estate, before she was murdered by [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]], who then absorbed the power.
