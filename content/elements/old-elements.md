# Old Elements

## Overview
The five elements that predate the full ten-element system of the Magician's Era. These are the elements that existed before [[Polaena]]'s children granted the full spectrum of magical attunement to the mortal races of Alatrya.

## Elements
- [[Water]]
- [[Fire]]
- [[Earth]]
- [[Wind]]
- [[Electricity]]

## Notes
- The remaining five elements — [[Shadow]], [[Light]], [[Nature]], [[Magnetism]], and [[Healing]] — were added within entities during the Wyvernal Era, however, the children were not born until during the Golden Era.
