One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by Arveshia, daughter of Polaena. Shadow magic is highly versatile, capable of forming solid constructs, transportation, mist-based concealment, binding restraints, temperature reduction, and planar storage.

## Notable Abilities
- **Shadow Transport** — teleportation up to ~750m for a skilled user; range scales with mana cost
- **Shadow Box** — extradimensional storage space; time passes extremely slowly inside
- **Shadow Mist** — disorienting concealment cloud
- **Solid Shadow** — hardened constructs usable as bindings or barriers

## Known Users
- **Anue Beidaka** — bearer of the Gem of Arveshia; primary attunement, extraordinarily powerful
- **Moon Elves** — Shadow is the common racial attunement

## Notes
- Considered "the worst of the Elements" by some prejudiced characters in Frithre
