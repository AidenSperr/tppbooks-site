# Shadow

> **Associated Deity:** [[Arveshia]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Arveshia]], daughter of [[Polaena]]. Shadow magic is highly versatile, capable of forming solid constructs, transportation (Shadow Transport), mist-based concealment, binding restraints, temperature reduction, and planar storage (the Shadow Box). Considered by some to be the "worst" element due to cultural stigma, particularly in [[Frithre]].

## Notable Abilities
- Shadow Transport — teleportation up to ~750m for a skilled user; range scales with mana cost
- Shadow Box — extradimensional storage space; only costs mana to create, not maintain; time passes extremely slowly inside, preserving food and spoilables
- Shadow Mist / Fog — disorienting concealment cloud
- Solid Shadow — hardened Shadow constructs usable as bindings, ropes, or barriers
- Realm-binding — high-level spell attempted by cultists to trap targets in a Voidrealm

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Arveshia]]; primary attunement, extraordinarily powerful
- Moon Elves — Shadow is the common racial attunement, though less dominant than Sun Elves' Light

## Notes
- Shadow attunement is associated with [[Moon Elves]] racially
- Considered "the worst of the Elements" by some prejudiced characters in [[Frithre]]
