# Fire

> **Associated Deity:** [[Kashtet]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Kashtet]], son of [[Polaena]]. Fire mana has a naturally outward, aggressive quality, described as always wanting out when stored in the Mana Bank. Its mana type runs red-orange in visible form and is among the more commonly attuned elements, particularly in [[Huozhi]].

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Huolong]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Kashtet]]; extraordinarily powerful Fire user
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — secondary attunement, far weaker than her Shadow
- Shimae — dual Fire/Water user, Fire being her dominant and registered element
- Jilua — former servant of Huolong

## Notes
- A ruby stone is commonly used in magistry to boost the effectiveness of Fire spells
- Fire mana left untreated in a person's blood can cause severe damage, requiring Healing intervention to clear
- Lavastones, mined by slaves in the [[Northern Flamelands]], are able to be made into a catalyst injectable which greatly amplifies the manatic power within an individual, at the cost of their health
