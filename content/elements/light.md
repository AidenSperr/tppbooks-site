# Light

> **Associated Deity:** [[Ulmanae]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Ulmanae]], son of [[Polaena]] and twin of [[Arveshia]]. Light magic manifests as high-velocity projectiles, explosive force, and radiant energy. Particularly suited to ranged and precision combat. Deeply tied to religious practice throughout [[Othaala]].

## Known Abilities
- High-speed light projectiles capable of boring holes through targets and felling trees
- Explosive impact force at range
- Can be channeled into specialized weapons such as the Tylnamus and Altenamius

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Lycheseia Athalnus]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Ulmanae]]; channels Light through her self-made weapons
- Sun Elves — Light attunement is the most common and dominant racial element

## Notes
- Light attunement is associated with [[Sun Elves]] racially
- The Tylnamus (rifle) and Altenamius (pistol) are weapons Lycheseia engineered specifically around Light magic
