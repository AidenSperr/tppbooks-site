# Wind

> **Associated Deity:** [[Vathem]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Vathem]], son of [[Polaena]]. Wind magic allows for flight, offensive cutting blasts, utility tasks like drying clothes or blowing debris, and levitation of objects and people. One of the more visually versatile elements in combat.

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Chuntian Zhenfung]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Vathem]]; extremely powerful Wind user
- Daskana and Lakcivia — Wind-attuned soldiers in Raomi's forces

## Notes
- Chuntian regularly uses Wind magic to fly, levitate the cart, and conduct powerful combat sweeps
- Over-reliance on a distorted Gem caused temporary Wind attunement disruption in Book 1
