# Magnetism

> **Associated Deity:** [[Seville]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Seville]], daughter of [[Polaena]]. Magnetism magic allows for the rapid propulsion and retrieval of metallic objects, particularly weapons, at high speed and with precision. Useful both in combat and for ranged utility.

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Tahlii A'Trentao]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Seville]]; uses Magnetism to throw and retrieve throwing knives at extreme velocity

## Notes
- Tahlii was pre-destined by [[Seville]] herself to bear the Gem; he did not have to fight for it
