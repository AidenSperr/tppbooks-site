# Earth

> **Associated Deity:** [[Dvanithe]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Dvanithe]], son of [[Polaena]]. Earth magic manifests as control over stone and soil, capable of raising walls, pillars, and barriers from the ground.

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Mataki Naleva]] — primary attunement; raises stone walls and barriers in combat

## Notes
- Used practically for construction and repair; the tall towers of [[Rrroma]] are maintained with Wind and Earth magic
