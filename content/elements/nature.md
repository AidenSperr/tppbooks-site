# Nature

> **Associated Deity:** [[Lavi]]

## Overview
One of the ten elements of Alatrya, governed by [[Lavi]], daughter of [[Polaena]]. Nature magic allows for the growth, control, and weaponization of plant life: roots, blooms, vines, and vegetation. Also useful for passive, non-combat applications such as causing flowers to bloom.

## Known Users (Notable)
- [[Nara Zamolvga]] — bearer of the Gem of [[Lavi]]; uses roots explosively in combat and casually causes plants to bloom at will

## Notes
- [[Nara Zamolvga]] received her Gem from [[Lavi]] directly after fleeing through a forest
