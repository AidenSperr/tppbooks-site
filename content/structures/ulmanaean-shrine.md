# Ulmanaean Shrine

> **Type:** Shrine
> **City/Location:** Various
> **Dedicated To:** [[Ulmanae]]

## Overview
A shrine dedicated to [[Ulmanae]], the God of Light and one of [[Polaena]]'s ten elemental children. [[Lycheseia Athalnus]] is Ulmanae's Gemwielder and primary mortal devotee during the events of the books, which may lend these shrines additional significance to those following her story.

## History
No specific historical events are recorded here at this time.

## Notes
- Pronunciation of Ulmanae: *UHL-muh-nay*
- Ulmanae is described as reserved, direct, and evaluative, traits that are often reflected in the aesthetic of his shrines.
