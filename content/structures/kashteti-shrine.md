# Kashteti Shrine

> **Type:** Shrine
> **City/Location:** Various
> **Dedicated To:** [[Kashtet]]

## Overview
A shrine dedicated to [[Kashtet]], the God of Fire and one of [[Polaena]]'s ten elemental children. As with most shrines to the individual gods, it exists within the broader umbrella of the Polaenian faith, serving worshipers who feel a particular devotion to Kashtet or who wield the element of Fire.
