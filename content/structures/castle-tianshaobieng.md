# Castle Tianshaobieng

> **Type:** Castle / Imperial Palace
> **City/Location:** [[Ranshao]], [[Huozhi]]
> **Dedicated To:** —

## Overview
The grand castle and seat of imperial power at the center of [[Ranshao]], capital of [[Huozhi]]. Home to [[Huolong]] and the imperial court. Also referred to as *Palace Tianshaobieng* in certain contexts. It is the most fortified and politically significant structure in Huozhi, and likely in all of Alatrya during the events of Book II.

## History
Serves as the nerve center of [[Huolong]]'s campaign of expansion. Chapter XXIV of Book II is set here, making it the site of the story's closest approach to Huolong himself.
