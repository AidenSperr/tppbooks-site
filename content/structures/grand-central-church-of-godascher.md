# Grand Central Church of Godascher

> **Type:** Church
> **City/Location:** [[Godascher]], [[Frithre]]
> **Dedicated To:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
The largest [[Polaenian Church]] on the continent of Alatrya. A landmark of the Human capital and a center of religious authority in the west. Originally led by [[Reverend Bhulytaszi]], whose tenure and eventual fate remain significant to the church's modern standing.

## History
No specific historical events are recorded in full at this time beyond Bhulytaszi's original leadership.

## Notes
- The church's size distinguishes it from all other Polaenian institutions on Alatrya.
- Its location in the Human capital gives it considerable political as well as religious weight.
