# Aevatre Ajjael

> **Type:** Café
> **City/Location:** [[Lumen]], [[Othaala]]
> **Dedicated To:** —

## Overview
A small, beloved café located in the [[Yavathre District]] of [[Lumen]]. Owned and operated by [[Dakkai Eletha]], a Dusk Elf known for his many piercings, large spacers, and personal warmth. The interior is cozy and nature-esque, with potted plants filling wooden shelves that line the walls and jade-colored paint throughout. A regular haunt of [[Anue Beidaka]] and [[Emaera Yuhava]], who came often on Erimevidae evenings or after school.

## History
Dakkai built close relationships with the young Elves who frequented the café, sitting them at a specific table along the right wall and memorizing their usual orders: a blonde coffee for Anue, Elatahan tea for Emaera. He was something of a makeshift therapist for them, asking after school, friendships, and home. One of the last places Anue visited before leaving Lumen.

## Notes
- The name is spelled "Aevatre Ajjael" on the door sign in Book I.
