# Church of the Low

> **Type:** Temple / Cult Shrine
> **City/Location:** Various; one ruined example found in a forest in [[Othaala]]
> **Dedicated To:** [[The Low]]

## Overview
A place of worship used by the [[Pyantyadha Eltyr]] to venerate [[The Low]]. These structures were long believed to be myth, stories told by elders to frighten children into attending the Polaenian Church. The discovery of a ruined example in a [[Umerr Forest]] in Othaala by [[Anue Beidaka]] confirmed their existence. The altar within that ruin was a pyramid shape, inverted and braced, and bore an inscription in Othaani despite the apparent age of the surrounding building.

## History
The ruined church Anue found had no roof left; its stone walls were crumbled, its stained-glass windows cracked and faded, and the floorboards long decomposed. Only the altar had survived intact, as if it had been preserved deliberately. It read:

> *Halalt vrikam shtomalv.*
> *Umerr vrikam thafva zaeran.*
> *Da Gakca arrapathelt!*
> *Elorvathe meinale prollet ulhawa prramelve!*
>
> *The living will perish. The dark will consume all. The Low prevails! Bring forth the smell of death!*

More active cells exist. By Book II, the Pyantyadha Eltyr had moved their operations from [[Daegrakce Forest]] and [[Umerr Forest]] to [[Kkallestiae Forest]] and [[Asslvave Woods]], before being largely cleared out of those areas by Othaalan defense forces.

## Notes
- These temples are illegal and deliberately hidden. Their existence is considered alarming intelligence.
- The smell of rot and death is associated with the presence of The Low and these shrines.
