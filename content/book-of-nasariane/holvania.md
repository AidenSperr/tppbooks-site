# Holvania

> **Author:** A gathering of leaders from the three early Faeric nations: [[Alkta]], [[Tasema]], and [[Nnasse]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** History, culture, and early governance of the [[Fairies|Faeric]] peoples

## Summary / Known Contents
The fifth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Unique among the compilation in that it was authored collectively by leaders representing all three of the early Faeric nations — [[Alkta]], [[Tasema]], and [[Nnasse]] — rather than by a single individual. Documents Faeric history, cultural traditions, and the political landscape before the consolidation of the three nations into [[Qauta]].

## Significance
The only collectively authored book in the [[Book of Nasariane]], reflecting the Faeric tradition of governance by council rather than singular authority. Its inclusion marks the formal representation of the Faeric peoples in the compiled sacred text.
