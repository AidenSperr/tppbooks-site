# Frithranis

> **Author:** [[Savel Godarev of Lapentsk]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]] (266 c.e.)
> **Subject Matter:** Early history of the [[Humans|Human]] nation of [[Godalosvia]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The sixth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by [[Savel Godarev of Lapentsk]] in 266 c.e., it documents the history of [[Godalosvia]] — the first major Human trade hub and the site of the first stone bridge built at scale on the continent. Published quickly after Delmervas, Vichanera, and Elemelfasa and widely distributed.

## Significance
The primary historical record of early Human civilization. Its rapid publication and wide distribution made it one of the most accessible texts in the eventual Book of Nasariane compilation.
