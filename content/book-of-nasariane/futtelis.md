# Futtelis

> **Author:** [[Futtel Ffranich]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Golden Era|Golden Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The establishment of the [[Eight Alatryan Nations]] as distinct sovereign states

## Summary / Known Contents
The twenty-second book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by the Faeric historian [[Futtel Ffranich]], it documents the formal establishment of the Eight Alatryan Nations, the point at which the continent's political map became the one broadly recognizable in the current era. Covers the consolidation of peoples, the drawing of national borders, and the recognition of each nation's distinct racial and cultural identity.

## Significance
The canonical record of the formation of the Eight Nations. Marks the transition in the compiled text from race-specific histories to continental political history.
