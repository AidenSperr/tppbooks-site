# Estalitana

> **Author:** [[Ginev]] (the [[Prophet]])
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The establishment and rules of the [[Polaenian Church]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The twelfth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by the Prophet [[Ginev]], it documents the formal founding of the [[Polaenian Church]]: its structure, hierarchy, rules, and the principles governing its operation. The institutional foundation document of the Church.

## Significance
The text by which the [[Polaenian Church]] legitimizes its own existence and governance structure. All formal Church hierarchy and policy traces back to Estalitana.
