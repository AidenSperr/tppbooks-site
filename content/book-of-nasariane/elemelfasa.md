# Elemelfasa

> **Author:** [[Theraniel of Lumen]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]] (265 c.e.)
> **Subject Matter:** Early history of the [[Elves]], the rise of [[Otanlla]], and its cultural and political development

## Summary / Known Contents
The fourth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by [[Theraniel of Lumen]] in 265 c.e., it documents Elven history from the earliest mountain settlements in the [[Bhahare Mountains]] through the formation and cultural achievements of [[Otanlla]], including a detailed account of its enduring political complexities. One of the most thorough early Elven historical records.

## Significance
The primary source text for Elven Conception Era history. Its documentation of [[Otanlla]]'s political framework informed later scholarship on the development of [[Othaala]]'s constitutional monarchy.
