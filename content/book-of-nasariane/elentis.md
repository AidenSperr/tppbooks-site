# Elentis

> **Author:** [[Elent]] (the [[Prophet]])
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** Proverbs originally written by [[Elent]] for the [[Church of Columil]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The eighteenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. A collection of proverbs by the Prophet [[Elent]], originally composed for the congregation of the [[Church of Columil]] in [[Frithre]]. The specific context of their origin, written for a local congregation, distinguishes them from the more universally directed proverbs of [[Ginevis]].

## Significance
One of two proverb collections in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Its inclusion connects the sacred canon to the [[Church of Columil]], one of the noted architectural and religious landmarks of [[Frithre]].
