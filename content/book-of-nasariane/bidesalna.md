# Bidesalna

> **Author:** [[Polaena]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Golden Era|Golden Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** Guidelines for the establishment and keeping of good government

## Summary / Known Contents
The twenty-fourth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]] and the second of the three Golden Era Polaenian books. A prescriptive text outlining what [[Polaena]] considers just and righteous governance, the principles by which mortal nations should be organized and led. Sits between the warnings of [[Waresis]] and the prophecies of [[Nasalis]].

## Significance
The primary divine text on governance, complementing the legal framework of [[Leneles]] with a more expansive political theology. Its placement near the end of the canon makes it one of the least-read texts despite its practical relevance.
