# Ronaela

> **Author:** [[Ronaela Astarise]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]] (commissioned by [[Marien I]] following her return to [[Knaema]])
> **Subject Matter:** Ronaela's solo southern expedition and the discovery of the [[Five Wyverns]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The fourteenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Originally titled *A Story on the Birth of Wyvernal Studies*, it was written by [[Ronaela Astarise]] at the commission of King [[Marien I]] following her return to [[Knaema]]. It documents her solo departure from the main [[ESEA]] body in 392 c.e., her crossing of the [[Farilvana River]] into what would become [[Almania]], her multi-decade mapping of the southern continent, and her discovery of all five Wyverns. Her relationship with [[Thurva]] in particular, the only positive contact established with any Wyvern, is detailed at length.

## Significance
The founding document of the field of Wyvernal Studies and the primary record of the first Elven contact with the Wyverns. Its companion text is [[Divisaccis]].
