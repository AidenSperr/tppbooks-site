# Nalekati

> **Author:** [[Taylyas Szenitsky]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The birth of the [[Beastfolk]] and their descent from the [[Six Faunan Kings]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The twenty-first book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. The second text by [[Taylyas Szenitsky]], companion to [[Davmvva]]. Documents the emergence of the six Beastfolkish ethnic groups from the [[Six Faunan Kings]], paralleling the Dragonkin origin story of [[Davmvva]].

## Significance
The primary origin record of the [[Beastfolk]] and their connection to the [[Six Faunan Kings]].
