# Bhulytaszis

> **Author:** [[Reverend Bhulytaszi]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The life and teachings of [[Reverend Bhulytaszi]], a prominent early religious figure

## Summary / Known Contents
The thirteenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by [[Reverend Bhulytaszi]], an early figure of significant standing in the [[Polaenian Church]]. The contents document either his life, his teachings, or both, situating his example as a model of religious conduct within the canon.

## Significance
One of the few books authored by a mortal who was not a head of state, an explorer, or a collectively recognized group. Its inclusion reflects the degree of Bhulytaszi's spiritual influence.
