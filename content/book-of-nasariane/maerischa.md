# Maerischa

> **Author:** Elder Ghouls of [[A'Amaer]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** History of [[A'Amaer]] and the [[Ghouls]], written primarily for those newly arrived in the nation

## Summary / Known Contents
The seventh book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written collectively by elder Ghouls, its primary purpose is to orient those newly reborn through the [[Risen]] process, explaining the nature of [[A'Amaer]], the theology of [[Redemption]], and the history of how the nation came to exist. It functions as both a historical record and a practical guide for newly risen Ghouls.

## Significance
The only book in the [[Book of Nasariane]] written from the perspective of the [[Ghouls]] themselves, and the only one directed primarily at a specific audience rather than the general reader.
