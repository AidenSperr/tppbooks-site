# Waresis

> **Author:** [[Polaena]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Golden Era|Golden Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** Warnings of coming catastrophic and prophetic events; the first of the three Polaenian Golden Era books

## Summary / Known Contents
The twenty-third book in the [[Book of Nasariane]] and the first of three Golden Era texts written by [[Polaena]]. Focused on warnings — signs to watch for, dangers to prepare against, and the shape of catastrophes to come. Written after the nations had been seeded and the lands fully blessed and lived-in, but before [[Polaena]]'s long silence.

## Significance
The first of the prophetic late-book texts that most believers never reach. Sets the tone of the final section of the [[Book of Nasariane]] as one of warning and preparation rather than law or history.
