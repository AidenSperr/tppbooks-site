# Colchebushev II

> **Author:** [[King Colchebushev]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The facilitation of [[Ghoulish Independence]] and the political trials Colchebushev faced in pursuing it

## Summary / Known Contents
The seventeenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. The second of [[King Colchebushev]]'s two contributions, it documents his efforts to achieve [[Ghoulish Independence]], freeing [[A'Amaer]] from Godalosvian control, and the opposition he faced within his own government and populace in doing so. A frank political memoir as much as a historical document.

## Significance
The primary account of [[Ghoulish Independence]] from the perspective of its principal facilitator. Its candor about internal opposition makes it unusually personal for a canonical religious text.
