# Vichanera

> **Author:** [[Khoren of Xeingang]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]] (264 c.e.)
> **Subject Matter:** History of the [[Demons|Demonic]] peoples from the [[Sharaczala Mountains]] to the northern plains

## Summary / Known Contents
The third book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by the [[Bone-Singer]] elder [[Khoren of Xeingang]] in 264 c.e., it documents the scattered histories of the Demonic peoples across [[Huozhi]] and surrounding territories. Compiled following the success of [[Delmervas]], it was shared cautiously beyond Demonic lands. The oral Bone-Singer tradition that informed its content predates the written text by generations.

## Significance
One of the few surviving records of early Demonic history from a Demonic perspective. The Bone-Singer tradition of oral historiography gives it a distinct voice compared to the other Conception Era histories.
