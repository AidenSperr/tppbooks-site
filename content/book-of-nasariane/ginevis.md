# Ginevis

> **Author:** [[Ginev]] (the [[Prophet]])
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** Proverbs of the Prophet [[Ginev]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The eleventh book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. A collection of proverbs written by [[Ginev]], a Human Prophet of [[Polaena]]. Short, aphoristic in form, meant for repeated reference rather than linear reading.

## Significance
One of two books authored by [[Ginev]] in the compilation; the other is [[Estalitana]]. Together they represent his major contribution to the sacred canon.
