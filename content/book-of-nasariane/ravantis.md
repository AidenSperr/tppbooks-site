# Ravantis

> **Author:** [[Nasati Aerthuma]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The [[Wyvernal Wars]] and the discovery of the [[Six Faunan Kings]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The nineteenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by [[Nasati Aerthuma]], an Othaalan field officer and explorer. Documents both the [[Wyvernal Wars]], in which the five Wyverns were defeated by the combined armies of [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]], and Nasati's post-war discovery of the [[Six Faunan Kings]] in their then-ancient and nearly perished state.

## Significance
The primary record of the [[Wyvernal Wars]] and the discovery of the [[Six Faunan Kings]]. Without Ravantis, the Faunan Kings and their connection to the [[Beastfolk]] would have no surviving first-hand documentation.
