# Ponashi

> **Author:** [[Polaena]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The importance of worship and how it is to be conducted

## Summary / Known Contents
The ninth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by [[Polaena]], it explains the theology of worship: why it matters, what it accomplishes, and the correct manner in which it should be practiced. Serves as the liturgical guide underlying [[Polaenian Church]] ceremony.

## Significance
The foundational text for [[Polaenian Church]] practice. All formal worship ceremonies are informed by its contents.
