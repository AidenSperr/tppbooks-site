# Davmvva

> **Author:** [[Taylyas Szenitsky]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The birth of the [[Dragonkin]] from the fallen Wyverns and the introduction of the [[Five New Gods]]

## Summary / Known Contents
The twentieth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. Written by the Frithren historian [[Taylyas Szenitsky]], it documents two watershed events of the post-Wyvernal Wars period: the emergence of the five Dragonkinnian ethnic groups from the defeated Wyverns' bloodlines, and the arrival of the [[Five New Gods]], [[Seville]], [[Arveshia]], [[Ulmanae]], [[Lavi]], and [[Erimevia]], who were added to the divine order during this period.

## Significance
The primary theological and historical record of the Five New Gods' introduction. Its companion text is [[Nalekati]].
