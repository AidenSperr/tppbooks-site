# Divisaccis

> **Author:** [[Ronaela Astarise]]
> **Era Written:** [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]]
> **Subject Matter:** The beginning of the Wyvernal Era and the origins of the five old elements tied to the Wyverns

## Summary / Known Contents
The fifteenth book in the [[Book of Nasariane]]. The companion text to [[Ronaela]], also written by [[Ronaela Astarise]]. Where Ronaela focuses on the personal account of her expedition, Divisaccis takes a broader historical lens, documenting the elemental significance of the five Wyverns and contextualizing the start of the [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]] within the continent's magical and theological history.

## Significance
The primary historical source for the Wyvernal Era's opening period. Referenced by name in Book II when the Covana priest turns through the compiled volume.
