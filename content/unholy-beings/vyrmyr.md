# Vyrmyr

> **Tier:** Half-God

## Overview
Third son of [[The Low]] and ruler of [[Cytana]], the capital of [[Prylkya]]. Smaller in stature than his brothers but unnervingly still in presence. Wears dark, restrained robes with tight-fitting gloves. His mouth twitches faintly at the corners, as though hearing something no one else can. Eyes are paler than his brothers'. Speaks with condescension and sardonic cruelty.

## Abilities
Capable of pulling mortals into a personal Voidrealm, or a near-perfect copy of their surroundings, where he manipulates the mind to coerce surrender of their Gem and life. Skilled in physical combat; strong, fast, and carries a dagger with a green-black sheen. Bears a scar across his upper chest from his first encounter with [[Anue Beidaka]] in Dajaerve.

## Role in Story
First encountered in Book 1 in [[Dajaerve]], where he dragged Anue into his Voidrealm and severely wounded her. Killed in Book 2 by Anue during a second Voidrealm encounter while attempting the same on [[Lycheseia Athalnus]] in [[Rrroma]]. Decapitated by Anue using the Druva. His death was felt by [[The Low]] immediately.
