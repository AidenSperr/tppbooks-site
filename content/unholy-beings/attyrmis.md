# Attyrmis

> **Tier:** Half-God

## Overview
First and eldest son of [[The Low]], serving as his personal bodyguard, a role he has never yet been called upon to fulfill in earnest. Of medium height but broad, armored stature. Wears black hardened armor adorned with spikes, its trim gold and crimson. Large black wings with exposed bones sprout in folded fashion from his back. His face is concealed behind an ornate helmet, and he carries a great sword held point-down before him like a knight at rest. Shares the white-eyed gaze of his brothers.

His voice is an incredibly deep grumble. Unbeaten in combat and feared by all who see him.

## Abilities
Strength believed to be comparable to that of [[Arveshia]], [[Ulmanae]], or even [[Vathem]], well beyond that of his younger brothers.

## Connections
- [[The Low]] — father
- Hrstattymm — second brother, ruler of [[Prylkya]] beyond [[Cytana]]
- [[Vyrmyr]] — third and youngest brother

## History
Present alongside his brothers when [[The Low]] debriefed them following his meeting with [[Huolong]] at the close of Book 2. Was the first to speak, asking whether The Low had secured Huolong's commitment.
