# Hrstattymm

> **Tier:** Half-God

## Overview
Second son of [[The Low]] and ruler of all places within [[Prylkya]] beyond the capital city of [[Cytana]]. Slightly shorter than [[Attyrmis]] but still of muscular build. Leans with a casual, careless air, draped in heavy fabric wrapped loosely but with perfect balance. His gaze bears down with wordless scrutiny, described as stone made animate. His presence is cold and heavy. Shares the white-eyed gaze of his brothers.

His voice is described as a low rumble.

## Abilities
Stronger than [[Vyrmyr]], though his full capabilities have not yet been demonstrated.

## Connections
- [[The Low]] — father
- [[Attyrmis]] — eldest brother, The Low's bodyguard
- [[Vyrmyr]] — youngest brother

## History
Present alongside his brothers when [[The Low]] debriefed them following his meeting with [[Huolong]]. Upon hearing that Huolong had fully committed himself, remarked "Then he is ours," which The Low gently corrected.

- Pronunciation: Herr-STAHT-tihm
