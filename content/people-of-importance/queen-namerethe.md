# Queen Namerethe

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Ethnicity:** [[Moon Elves]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Description
The reigning Queen of [[Othaala]], governing from the capital [[Lumen]] on the [[Yavathre Plateau]]. A [[Light]] element user with a direct line of communication to [[Ulmanae]]. Her bearing is composed and confident; she speaks with measured authority and backs her military decisions with strategic conviction, even when warned otherwise by allies.

## Role in Story
A major political figure in Book II. She is managing [[Othaala]]'s response to [[Huolong]]'s invasion, overseeing the defense lines at the [[Almaeji River]] under First General [[Raomi Allamaera]], and coordinating with foreign leaders like Queen [[Lynety Laythrya]] of [[Frithre]] on the broader war effort. She is also responsible for having largely cleared the [[Pyantyadha Eltyr]] from [[Umerr Forest]] and [[Daegrakce Forest]] through the Lovante Defense Companies.

## Relationships
- [[Ulmanae]] — communicates with him frequently; unusual for a non-Gemwielder
- [[Raomi Allamaera]] — First General; she places full confidence in him
- Queen [[Lynety Laythrya]] — ally and diplomatic contact in [[Frithre]]

## History
Details of her ascension are not yet recorded. She rules during the most significant military crisis in modern Alatryan history, the Huozhese invasion of the continent's western lands.
