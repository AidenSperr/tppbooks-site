A tall Moon Elf from the Yavathre District of Lumen, with pale skin, red eyes, and jet-black hair typical of Northern Moon Elves. Wields both Shadow and Fire elements, the latter being rare alongside the Shadow Gem.

## Role in Story
The primary protagonist of the first two books and Gemwielder of Shadow. Her journey begins when she leaves Lumen and gradually assembles the other Gemwielders across Book I, eventually leading the group toward Ranshao in Book II.

## Relationships
- **Arveshia** — patron goddess; close personal relationship, described as familial
- **Chuntian Zhenfung** — closest companion
- **Dakkai Eletha** — close friend and mentor figure

## Notes
- Has a large scar on her stomach from Vyrmyr's dagger wound
