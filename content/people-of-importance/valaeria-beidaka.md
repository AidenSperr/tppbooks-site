# Valaeria Beidaka

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Ethnicity:** [[Moon Elves]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Description
Formerly Valaeria Havaera. Wife of [[Tavu Beidaka]] and mother of [[Anue Beidaka]] and [[Torra Beidaka]]. Described as brash and unapologetically cruel, with a tendency toward control and emotional domination. She does not love Anue so much as she loves to preside over her.

## Role in Story
The abusive home environment Valaeria created is the foundational wound that shapes Anue's self-reliance, emotional guarding, and eventual departure from Lumen. She appears directly in the opening of Book I when Anue leaves, screaming at her daughter as she runs out and regretting it not out of love but out of losing her subject to control.

## Relationships
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — eldest daughter; estranged
- [[Torra Beidaka]] — younger child
- [[Tavu Beidaka]] — husband; described as kind and unlike her, making the attraction unclear

## History
Married Tavu Beidaka approximately twenty years before the start of Book I. The specifics of their courtship are described as unknown to the narrator. Her relationship with Anue was abusive throughout Anue's childhood and young adulthood.

## Notes
- Pronunciation of Valaeria: *Vah-LAYR-ree-yah*
