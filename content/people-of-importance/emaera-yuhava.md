# Emaera Yuhava

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Ethnicity:** [[Moon Elves]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Description
Longtime boyfriend of [[Anue Beidaka]] and son of [[Mallia Yuhava]]. A frequent companion of Anue's at [[Aevatre Ajjael]], where he and Anue shared regular evenings drinking tea and coffee with [[Dakkai Eletha]]. His usual order was Elatahan tea.

## Role in Story
Emaera's suicide is the event that breaks Anue's remaining reason to stay in [[Lumen]] and sets the entire story in motion. His absence shapes her grief, her sense of purpose, and her emotional reticence throughout both books.

## Relationships
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — girlfriend
- [[Mallia Yuhava]] — mother
- [[Dakkai Eletha]] — close family friend and café owner

## History
Lived in Lumen in the [[Yavathre District]]. Took his own life prior to the start of Book I.
