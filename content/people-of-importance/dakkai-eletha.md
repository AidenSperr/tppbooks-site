# Dakkai Eletha

> **Race:** [[Elves]]
> **Ethnicity:** [[Dusk Elves]] (50% Moon, 50% Sun; medium-dark skin)
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Description
Owner of [[Aevatre Ajjael]] in the [[Yavathre District]] of [[Lumen]]. A kind man of medium-dark Dusk Elf complexion with many piercings, large spacers along his ears, and a short kept beard. He favors baggy clothes and streetwear. An easy, warm presence who serves, cleans, and converses in equal measure.

## Role in Story
A steadying mentor and makeshift therapist for [[Anue Beidaka]] and [[Emaera Yuhava]] throughout their youth. He memorized their regular orders, seated them at the same table along the right wall whenever they came in, and genuinely cared about their lives at school and at home. After Emaera's death and Anue's departure, the café he built together with them lingers as one of the last places Anue pauses before leaving Lumen.

## Relationships
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — close friend; she views him as a cool uncle figure
- [[Emaera Yuhava]] — close friend; same dynamic

## History
Built and operates [[Aevatre Ajjael]] in Lumen.
