# Gem

> **Origin:** Created by [[Polaena]] and her gemsmith [[Lovitavo]]
> **Associated With:** [[Gemwielders]], [[Polaenian Church]]

## Overview
The ten holy catalysts placed across [[Alatrya]] at the beginning of the Magician's Era. Each Gem is tied to one of the ten elemental gods and their respective element, and functions as a permanent broadcast of that element's mana across the entire continent, enabling mortal attunement. Without the Gems, widespread elemental magic would not be possible.

Each Gem is small, a precious stone of a rich color corresponding to its element, with mana held inside in a visible flow-state. Even when bonded to a bearer, they emanate a detectable field of Natural Mana Density (NMD) that can be located with a Manometer.

## Properties / Abilities
- Passively broadcasts its element's mana across all of [[Alatrya]], enabling attunement in the population
- When bonded to a bearer, the Gem is absorbed into the body and stored within the Mana Bank; the bearer can summon its physical form at will, usually manifesting from the palm
- Bonding occurs on first contact: the Gem surges its power into the holder's blood and Bank in an intensely painful burst
- Amplifies the bearer's elemental power significantly beyond what an ordinary person can achieve
- If a bearer dies alone, the Gem's power dies with them. If the Gem is forcibly taken from a living bearer, it bonds to the one who takes it, transferring all power to the new holder
- A Gem that is distorted or neglected by its corresponding deity can cause the bearer's spells to weaken and distort

## History
Conceived when [[Vathem]] wished to gift his power to his betrothed, [[Trovia]]. After deliberation among the Eleven, [[Polaena]] directed her gemsmith [[Lovitavo]], a Dragonkin, to craft ten stones, one per element. These were placed in locations across [[Alatrya]] and their power was bound into them, broadcasting mana to the world. The ten elemental gods then personally taught the mortals how to use their respective elements. The Gems have remained active ever since, and their bearers are chosen by the gods themselves.

## Notes
- The Gems are among the most closely guarded secrets on [[Alatrya]]; knowledge of them is restricted to organizations like [[Cavant A-Meftua]] and the [[Polaenian Church]]
- Fire mana from [[Kashtet]]'s Gem emits a faint reddish glow when buried; it was located by [[Huolong]] using a Manometer to track NMD
- A Gem in distress (when a god is withholding communication from their bearer) manifests visually as a distorted, fluid shape rather than its normal organized octagonal form (except for the Shadow Gem, which is shaped fluidly by default)
