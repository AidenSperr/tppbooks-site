# Erimevia

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Healing]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Erimevia is the Goddess of Healing and the tenth of Polaena's ten children. She is blonde with wavy hair that falls to her chest, and wears simple, tasteful dresses; in her first appearance she wore a white knee-length dress reminiscent of Alktan Fairywomen's traditional wear. Her room smells of Alfri flowers and spices. Her voice is soft and gentle but carries power at the same time, and she has an easy warmth: she calls Anue "sweetie," asks about her scar with genuine care, and makes light of tense moments. She is also quick-tongued and sarcastic with Arveshia in a fond way.

She healed [[Anue Beidaka]]'s severe stab wound from [[Vyrmyr]] within seconds, closing the injury entirely and leaving only a scar. Her Gemwielder is [[Maresi Gratalza]], whom she met face-to-face in Book II. She sized him up quickly, told him "you'll be a good one," and moved on.
