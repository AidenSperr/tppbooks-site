# Lavi

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Nature]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Lavi is the Goddess of Nature and the sixth of Polaena's ten children. She is described as "really adorable" by [[Nara Zamolvga]], who says she felt an urge to squeeze her. She has appeared to Zamolvga at least three times. In Book II, she accompanies Faemelia and Arveshia to deliver war news to the Gemwielders, arriving with her arms crossed and face tight with worry. When [[Ulmanae]] fired an experimental shot into a grove of trees in her presence, she was displeased, though he was unconcerned.

[[Kaedoa]] is the city most associated with her worship, being built beneath the [[Grand Olbaerd Tree]] in a valley described as practically a living shrine to Nature, celebrating the miracle of the Tree and diversity of all races.
