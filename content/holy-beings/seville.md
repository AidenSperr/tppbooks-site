# Seville

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Magnetism]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Seville is the Goddess of Magnetism and the ninth of Polaena's ten children. Her Gemwielder is [[Tahlii A'Trentao]]. Arveshia places her strength in a tier below the twins ([[Arveshia]] and [[Ulmanae]]) and above Erimevia, noting Attyrmis's power approaches or matches that range.

The closest peoples to her are the [[Tuithes]], who reside mainly in [[Tuithe'la'nat]]. Once every year, as a coming-of-age ceremony and general religious pilgrimage for elders, the invitees walk across the [[Tuithe Islands]]. On the final island, on the cliffside, is the largest [[Sevillean Shrine]] in Alatrya, covering most of the island. On the backhand cliffside is a small and dangerous path which leads to a small cave, where Seville gifted [[Tahlii A'Trentao]] the Gem of [[Magnetism]].
