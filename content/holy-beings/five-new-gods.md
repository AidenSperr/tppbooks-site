# Five New Gods

> **Tier:** God (grouping)
> **Element:** Nature, Shadow, Light, Magnetism, Healing
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
A collective term for the second five of [[Polaena]]'s ten children: [[Lavi]] (Nature), [[Arveshia]] (Shadow), [[Ulmanae]] (Light), [[Seville]] (Magnetism), and [[Erimevia]] (Healing). The distinction reflects the order of their origin among the Eleven, with the Five New Gods coming after the [[Five Old Gods]].

## Notes
- The Twins, [[Arveshia]] and [[Ulmanae]], are both New Gods
