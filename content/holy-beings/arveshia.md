Goddess of Shadow and the seventh of Polaena's ten children, twin to Ulmanae, the God of Light. Among the most personally present of the gods in the story, developing a close and informal friendship with Anue Beidaka and Chuntian Zhenfung.

## Worship & Church
- Polaenian Church generally
- Arveshian Shrine in Vahhlve — the largest in Othaala
- Various smaller shrines throughout the continent

## Notes
- Twin to Ulmanae; she calls him "baby brother" and teases him freely
- Favors Othaala most of any nation
- Prefers close followers, in keeping with the shadowed, intimate nature of her element
