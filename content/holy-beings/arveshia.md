# Arveshia

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Shadow]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Arveshia is the Goddess of Shadow and the seventh of Polaena's ten children, twin to [[Ulmanae]], the God of Light. She is among the most personally present of the gods in the story, developing a close and informal friendship with [[Anue Beidaka]] and [[Chuntian Zhenfung]] over the course of Book I and Book II. Dark-skinned with thick, curly hair and an aura of contained, non-malicious power, she appears in a black dress with silver woven designs. Her speech shifts between formal and casual depending on her mood and company, and she has stated a preference for treating her Gemwielder as a familiar rather than a subordinate. Chuntian described sensing her mana as infinite, kind, and reverent.

She is the one who placed the Gem of Shadow within [[Anue Beidaka]], making Anue her extension. The Gem is described as intrinsically linked to Arveshia herself: if the Gem were taken, Anue would die and Arveshia would fall to [[The Low]].
