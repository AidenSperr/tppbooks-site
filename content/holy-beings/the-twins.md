# The Twins

> **Tier:** God (grouping)
> **Element:** [[Shadow]] and [[Light]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
A collective term for [[Arveshia]] and [[Ulmanae]], twin siblings and the seventh and eighth children of [[Polaena]]. Shadow and Light as paired elements reflect their twinhood. They are the patron gods most favored by [[Othaala]], which is noted as their preferred nation. Their combined power is noted as among the highest of the [[Five New Gods]] children, with Arveshia placing their strength above most of the younger siblings.

## Notes
- [[Othaala]] is described as being "most favored" by the Twins
