# Five Old Gods

> **Tier:** God (grouping)
> **Element:** Water, Fire, Earth, Wind, Electricity
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
A collective term for the first five of [[Polaena]]'s ten children: [[Faemelia]] (Water), [[Kashtet]] (Fire), [[Dvanithe]] (Earth), [[Vathem]] (Wind), and [[Gyoledt]] (Electricity). The distinction between Old and New Gods reflects the order of their origin among the Eleven, with the Five Old Gods predating the [[Five New Gods]].

## Notes
- Contrasted with the [[Five New Gods]]: [[Lavi]], [[Arveshia]], [[Ulmanae]], [[Seville]], and [[Erimevia]]
