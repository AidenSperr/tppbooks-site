# Gyoledt

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Electricity]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Gyoledt is the God of Electricity and the fifth of Polaena's ten children. [[Alvayr Vahamenr]] has seen him once and describes him warmly as "a good 'ol fellow; great beard." His Gemwielder is Alvayr, with whom he watched develop into a great father and husband, as well as a protective and wise axeman.
