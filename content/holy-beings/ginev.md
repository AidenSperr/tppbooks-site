# Ginev

> **Tier:** Prophet
> **Element:** None
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Ginev is a Prophet of [[Polaena]] and the founder of the [[Polaenian Church]], the dominant religious institution of Alatrya. Beginning around 351 c.e. of the Conception Era, he drafted a collection of proverbs in the larger cities around the [[Sea of Hornaskia]], which became known as *[[Ginevis]]*. He followed this with *[[Estalitana]]*, a treatise of church order completed in 353 c.e. that formally established the Church as an organization. His work was grounded in the earlier sacred texts Leneles and Ponashi, which he used as the doctrinal foundation for the institution.

## Worship & Church
- [[Polaenian Church]] — founded by Ginev

## Associated Shrines/Structures
- [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]] — first seat of the Church, blessed in 354 c.e.

## Notes
- Author of *[[Ginevis]]* and *[[Estalitana]]*
- His founding of the Church in Godascher in 354 c.e. is one of the most significant events of the late Conception Era
