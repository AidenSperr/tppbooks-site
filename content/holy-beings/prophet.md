# Prophet

> **Tier:** Prophet
> **Element:** None
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
A Prophet is a mortal given direct permission by [[Polaena]] to spread her word across Alatrya. Unlike the Gemwielders, who are extensions of the individual gods, Prophets are specifically tasked by Polaena herself and carry a mandate for religious teaching and the founding or expansion of her faith. Their writings are typically elevated to scripture status and incorporated into the [[Book of Nasariane]].
