# Ulmanae

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Light]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Ulmanae is the God of Light and the eighth of Polaena's ten children, twin to [[Arveshia]]. Dark-skinned and of medium height, his hair hangs in thick dreads, and his dark brown eyes command attention from anyone he looks at. He dresses sharply: black leather pants and overcoat with black-on-black woven designs, a high-collared white dress shirt with golden buttons, diamond bud earrings, and a tattoo of his rune in Othaani on his chest. His voice is described as deep and full. He is reserved and evaluative, speaking little but with precision. He side-eyes Arveshia's teasing and rarely rises to it.

His Gemwielder is [[Lycheseia Athalnus]], whom he sought out and tested directly, examining her Altenamius pistol and assessing her abilities. His first response to her craftsmanship was to fire it into a stand of trees without warning.
