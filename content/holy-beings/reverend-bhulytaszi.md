# Reverend Bhulytaszi

> **Tier:** Prophet
> **Element:** None
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Reverend Bhulytaszi was the first reverend of the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]], ordained when the Church's first seat was blessed in 354 c.e. He is considered a Prophet of [[Polaena]]. His sermons, homilies, and letters were later copied down and collected into a volume called *[[Bhulytaszis]]*, which gained holy-book status almost immediately in 355 c.e. and was incorporated into the [[Book of Nasariane]].
