# Dvanithe

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Earth]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Dvanithe is the God of Earth and the third of Polaena's ten children. He is described by Arveshia as one of the siblings who does not particularly like talking to mortals. He and [[Faemelia]] were personally tasked by [[Polaena]] to deliver her message to [[Vakviva Evvuniav]] at the founding of the [[Cavant A-Meftua]] refuge, making him one of the few gods to act as a direct divine messenger in the founding era of the Gemwielder lineage.

He chose [[Mataki Naleva]] as his Gemwielder, who he thought carried great determination, loyalty, and judgement, in addition to being a daughter in a historically military family and a strong and proven warrior.
