# Kashtet

> **Tier:** God (One of the Eleven)
> **Element:** [[Fire]]
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]]

## Overview
Kashtet is the God of Fire and the second of Polaena's ten children. The [[Kashteni]] region of [[Huozhi]] bears his name, highlighting the historical relationship between the Demonic people and his worship. The region is notable in Book II as a source of merchant activity and trade contacts within Huolong's court.

In addition to this, the village of [[Piaofuzhen]] is noted as the holiest place to worship Kashtet, and is the birthplace of the dictator [[Huolong]]. The volcano, [[Kashtet's Hearth]], which is an often-active volcano in the [[Northern Flamelands]], is an additional marker of his power around the Huozhese people.
