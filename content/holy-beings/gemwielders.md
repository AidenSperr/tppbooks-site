# Gemwielders

> **Tier:** Gemwielder
> **Element:** Varies
> **Parent/Origin:** [[Polaena]] (via the Eleven)

## Overview
The ten mortals chosen by each of Polaena's ten children to wield the elemental Gems. Each Gemwielder is the mortal extension of their patron god; the Gem itself is described as being intrinsically the god's own power, meaning the god and the Gemwielder are bound together. If a Gem is taken or destroyed, the Gemwielder dies and the god falls to [[The Low]]. Gemwielders have greatly expanded mana capacity and receive guidance, visions, and direct contact from their patron god.

The ten current Gemwielders are:
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — Shadow ([[Arveshia]])
- [[Chuntian Zhenfung]] — Wind ([[Vathem]])
- [[Lycheseia Athalnus]] — Light ([[Ulmanae]])
- [[Maresi Gratalza]] — Healing ([[Erimevia]])
- [[Alvayr Vahamenr]] — Electricity ([[Gyoledt]])
- [[Nara Zamolvga]] — Nature ([[Lavi]])
- [[Tahlii A'Trentao]] — Magnetism ([[Seville]])
- [[Mataki Naleva]] — Earth ([[Dvanithe]])
- [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]] — Water ([[Faemelia]]) (traitor, original wielder: [[Lynaria Praczaya]])
- [[Huolong]] — Fire ([[Kashtet]]) (hostile)

## Worship & Church
- No formal institution; the Gemwielders operate outside and alongside the [[Polaenian Church]]

## Associated Shrines/Structures
- [[Cavant A-Meftua]] refuge (founding base of operations)
