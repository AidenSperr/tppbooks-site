```timeline-labeled
[line-3, body-4]
date: 1 Schloraft, 1 w.e.
title: Wyvernal Era Begins
content: The second recorded era begins. This was considered, at least before the Forsaken Era, to be the time of most suffering and difficulty between all nations. In this era, many cities were ravaged by the Five Wyverns, and great names of leaders were created. In addition to this, the introduction of the first five of Polaena's children were established, and their communication to one of the final prophets to contribute to the Book of Nasariane was given

date: 14 Trovevivi, 1 w.e.
title: [[Ronaela Astarise]] Discovers [[Valevyr]]
content: Similar to her encounter with [[Tyv]], she narrowly escapes her contact with [[Valevyr]], suffering from severe burns from cast fire. She notes down the seemingly unique abilities of both of these entities she calls Wyverns or Dragons.

date: 26 Czenilevr, 1 w.e.
title: Encounter With [[Kalvev]]
content: Though slightly less dangerous yet much quicker, she encounters [[Kalvev]] who is less aggressive and more territorial. She is hit with light wind magic, floating her off of a mountain ledge and allowing her to escape through the forest below

date: 10 Uyathirnd, 1 w.e.
title: [[Thurva]] Is Found Back At Base Camp
content: After returning to her camp along the shores of the [[Sea of Valvaka]], she spots a large serpentine creature far off in the distance, spewing water and creating incredible watery displays, seemingly playing. She notes the encounter down and continues to watch the spot

date: 37 Szanirerf, 1 w.e.
title: [[Shvafni]] Spotted During a Violent Thunderstorm
content: As an extreme thunderstorm raged on along the southern beaches, a Wyvern seemingly able to wield lightning flies overhead of [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela]]. Though in her tent, she hears the great wings flapping and peeks out to document the sight. After this, she found no new Wyverns, and concluded after a time that there were five, and named them all

date: 4 Porletivi, 2 w.e.
title: Positive Contact Is Made With [[Thurva]]
content: After multiple spottings, both ways, [[Thurva]] approaches the shoreline near to [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela's]] campsite, which had then grown to a small cabin. Ronaela is able to touch the Wyvern, who willingly accepts her hand. The two exchange gifts, highlighting the seemingly great intelligence of the creature, who was conclusively much kinder than her siblings

date: 40 Agataft, 4 w.e.
title: [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela]] Leaves Future [[Almania]] For the Elven Palace
content: Upon gathering much documentation on her encounters with the [[Five Wyverns]], especially that of [[Thurva]], she makes her way back to the Elven Palace near [[Knaema]]. Her intent is to educate the crown on her findings, having the respect of the old [[Marien I]]

date: 21 Uyathrind, 4 w.e.
title: [[Marien I]] Heeds Warnings and Learns of the [[Five Wyverns]]
content: Upon reading and hearing from [[Ronaela Astarise]] about the [[Five Wyverns]], the king takes note of the creatures and instructs her to formally write about her discoveries and publish the work under the crown's name. She obeys, and begins work on what would become the book [[Ronaela]], eventually added into the [[Book of Nasariane]]

date: 25 Uyathirnd, 4 w.e.
title: [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela]] Begins Writing the Book [[Ronaela]]
content: Originally titled *A Story on the Birth of Wyvernal Studies*, the book's creation begins, compiling in a more consumable format the events which had taken place in the mysterious southern lands regarding the [[Five Wyverns]]

date: 32 Asheverf, 4 w.e.
title: [[Ronaela]] Completed and Published
content: She finishes the book, and it is spread throughout all countries, first to the leaders of each nation, then to their advisors, then to the peoples. Many regard the book as sacred, holding the existence, though dangerous existence, of the [[Five Wyverns]] to be a trophy of [[Polaena|Polaena's]] power

date: 6 Trovevivi, 6 w.e.
title: [[ESEA]] Higher-Ups Return to [[Sea of Valvaka]]
content: Led by [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela]], a larger group of [[ESEA]] leaders make their way back to her camp on the shores. They build more cabins, a small dock, a field of native grain, and a scientific research house where experiments and documention was to be centralized. Among this group, besides Ronaela, were five botanists, seven biologists, four physical scientists, two mathematicians, and eight expeditionaries

date: 29 Inyutevr, 6 w.e.
title: The [[27WI|27 Wyvernal Investigators]] Meet [[Thurva]]
content: The group, which dubbed themsevles the [[27WI|27 Wyvernal Investigators]], or [[27WI]], finally encounter their first Wyvern, thankfully, being [[Thurva]]. Upon seeing more companions of [[Ronaela Astarise|Ronaela]], the Wyvern seems to become overjoyed and brings them enough fish to feed to whole group for a week, including some rarer species which were examined and documented

date: 14 Agataft, 7 w.e.
title: Magical Studies Begin
content: After many demonstrations of the conjuring of water from [[Thurva]], multiple members of the group begin to theorize on the capabilities of the creature, and come to a similarly-based conclusion as Ronaela: that the creatures are able to use things called [[Old Elements|Elements]] which are granted to them especially by [[Polaena]]

date: 39 Porletivi, 8 w.e.
title: [[Marien I]] Dies
content: Elven King [[Marien I]] dies, and his grandson, [[Marien III]], takes the throne. As his first decree, he allows for the expansion southward, the first settlement being [[Dvaelemnev]]

date: 2 Uyathirnd, 10 w.e.
title: [[Ronaela Astarise]] Dies From Wyvernal Attack
content: After a short expedition to attempt and observe the behavior of [[Valevyr]] in the mountains to the north of the camp, the small group is attacked after being sighted by the Wyvern, and Ronaela suffers more severe burns, too severe for her to survive. She gives the reins over to her second-in-command, [[Tsella Aanistre]], who originated from [[Suvrra]]

date: 33 Asheverf, 10 w.e.
title: [[27WI]] Returns to [[Knaema]]
content: Following another attack on the small encampment, the group decides to return to [[Knaema]] with their findings. They bring their multitude of documents to the University of Knaema and their research is continued to be built upon and many maps of the south-central lands of the continent are researched

date: 9 Schloraft, 12 w.e.
title: [[ESEA]] Disbands
content: Following the death of [[Ronaela Astarise]] and an ultimate distaste for the lack of inspiration to continue exploring the south of the continent from the majority of the members, [[Tsella Aanistre]] calls for the organization to disband, and it is confirmed via a board vote

date: 16 Inyutevr, 14 w.e.
title: [[House Steskar of Lapentsk]] Falls
content: After a wave of disease passes through the cities of [[Godascher]] and [[Lapentsk]], the majority of House Steskar dies, and no suitable heirs remain. The crown is then passed to [[King Elestryve]], who quickly gains a reputation for being strict regarding civil law

date: 4 Uyathirnd, 15 w.e.
title: [[HEO|Human Exploration Organization (HEO)]] Is Founded
content: Inspired by [[ESEA]], [[Nabatia]] and [[Godalosvia]] create a joint governmental organization to do similar exploration, mapping, and faunal/floral research south of their respective territories

date: 17 Szanirerf, 15 w.e.
title: [[HEO]] Begins First Mission
content: Starting from [[Minalshe]], the group begins to map west toward the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]]. Though the majority of the exploration stayed on the same longitudinal range as the existing territories of [[Nabatia]], the overall success and believed importance of the mission is considered by both participatory nations to be a great deal
```
