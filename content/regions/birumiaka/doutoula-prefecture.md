# Doutoula Prefecture

> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Administrative Type:** Prefecture

## Overview
A southwestern prefecture of [[Birumiaka]] bordering [[Almania]] along the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] and the [[Sapala Sea]].
