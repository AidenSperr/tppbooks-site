# Tahevina Prefecture

> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Administrative Type:** Prefecture

## Overview
A northeastern prefecture of [[Birumiaka]], predominantly home to the [[Tahevinans]], a canine-characteristic Beastfolk ethnicity descended from the Second Faunan King, [[Wotavi]].
