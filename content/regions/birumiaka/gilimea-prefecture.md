# Gilimea Prefecture

> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Administrative Type:** Prefecture

## Overview
A northeastern prefecture of [[Birumiaka]], predominantly home to the [[Gilimeans]], a fox-characteristic Beastfolk ethnicity descended from the First Faunan King, [[Shrova]].
