# Obemiva Prefecture

> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Administrative Type:** Prefecture

## Overview
The central prefecture of [[Birumiaka]] and home to the national capital, [[Samaiku]], one of the largest cities on the continent. Predominantly home to the [[Obemevians]].

## Cities
- [[Samaiku]] — capital of [[Birumiaka]]
- [[Mavana]], [[Taibasa]], [[Eikalive]], [[Kavesana]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Shiona Plains]], [[Missast Marsh]], [[Skosva River]], [[Dossaes River]], [[Juvawe Peninsula]], [[Juvawe Islands]], [[Eikalive Beaches]], [[Sea of Samaiku]]

## Notes
- Each prefecture is predominantly home to a specific Beastfolkish ethnicity; Obemiva is home to the [[Obemevians]]
