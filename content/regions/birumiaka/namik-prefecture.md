# Namik Prefecture

> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Administrative Type:** Prefecture

## Overview
An east-central prefecture of [[Birumiaka]], predominantly home to the [[Namiks]], a deer-characteristic Beastfolk ethnicity descended from the Third Faunan King, [[Nuveriami]].
