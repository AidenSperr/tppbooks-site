# Ttuventk

> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A region of [[Qauta]] in the southern interior, defined by the [[Shoneng Mountains]] along its southern border. Part of the Huozhese assault vector into [[Frithre]] passes near this region in Book II.
