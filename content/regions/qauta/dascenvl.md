# Dascenvl

> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A southern region of [[Qauta]] bordering the [[Orbev Mountains]] and [[Frithre]]. Contains [[Zlanqin]], one of the Asyulani team cities and a potential military target in the Huozhese campaign.

## Cities
- [[Zlanqin]], [[Llomnej]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Elsfacca Plains]], [[Dalesce Forest]], [[Orbev Mountains]], [[Slobeska River]]

## Notes
- [[Zlanqin]] is identified as a likely secondary target if Huozhese forces advance beyond [[Robosse]] Oblast
