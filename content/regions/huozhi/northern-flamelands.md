# Northern Flamelands

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A mostly uninhabited volcanic wasteland in the far north of [[Huozhi]]. Home to [[Kashtet's Hearth]], an active volcano considered a sacred marker of [[Kashtet]]'s presence. Lavastones are mined here, including by slave labor under [[Huolong]]'s rule.

## Cities
- None

## Geographic Features
- [[Chomien Mountains]] (northern portion), [[Kashtet's Hearth]], [[Dhozhein Tundra]]

## Notes
- Lavastones mined here can be refined into injectable mana catalysts that amplify power at severe cost to health
- Political prisoners and those deemed undesirable under Huolong's regime were historically sent here to mine
