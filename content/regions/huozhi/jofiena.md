# Jofiena

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A western border region of [[Huozhi]] abutting [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]], and a key zone in the Huozhese military advance during Book II. The [[Voemna Pass]] and [[Almaeji River]] corridor lie just across the border in [[Othaala]]'s [[Amekia Sector]].

## Cities
- [[Yufengu]], [[Muyunfa]], [[Anjing]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Baodian Sea]], [[Xhouten Forest]], [[Shoneng Mountains]], [[Alkta River]], [[Huegieng Plains]], [[Almaeji River]], [[Orbev Mountains]], [[Chensk River]]

## Notes
- Serves as a staging ground for Huozhese military movement westward into [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]] in Book II
