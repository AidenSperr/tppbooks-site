# Vinzhin

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A coastal region of [[Huozhi]] along the [[Baodian Sea]] and [[Bay of Zhouxe]], containing [[Xeingang]], historical home of [[Khoren of Xeingang]].

## Cities
- [[Xeingang]], [[Songbein]], [[Piaochu Xin]], [[Dongfeng]], [[Peitao]]
