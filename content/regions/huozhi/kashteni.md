# Kashteni

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
The primary region of [[Huozhi]] and home to the national capital, [[Ranshao]], seat of [[Huolong]]'s dictatorship and the location of [[Castle Tianshaobieng]]. Also contains [[Piaofuzhen]], the holiest site of [[Kashtet]] worship and [[Huolong]]'s birthplace.

## Cities
- [[Ranshao]] — capital of [[Huozhi]]; location of [[Castle Tianshaobieng]]
- [[Piaofuzhen]], [[Lansecheng]], [[Lojing]], [[Tonguen]], [[Penjung]], [[Taningang]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Chomien Mountains]], [[Geimang Forest]], [[Dhozhein Tundra]]
