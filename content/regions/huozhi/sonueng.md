# Sonueng

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A western coastal region of [[Huozhi]] along the [[Baodian Sea]] and [[Bay of Zhouxe]], bordering the former Huozhese territory of [[Baodia]] now held by [[Othaala]].
