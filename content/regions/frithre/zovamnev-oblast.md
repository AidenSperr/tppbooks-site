# Zovamnev Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
A southern oblast of [[Frithre]] bordering [[Almania]] and [[Birumiaka]]. Contains [[Ya'kali]], home of the [[Ya'kalians]], one of the five Human ethnic groups.

## Cities
- [[Zovamnev]], [[Itenatha]], [[Dahanal]], [[Ya'kali]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Shalatsav Flatlands]], [[Kahati Forest]], [[Kahati Plains]], [[Nazav Woods]], [[Nossalmi Mountains]], [[Ossolovav Mountains]], [[Farilvana River]], [[Skapyaviv Hills]], [[Vinami Prairie]], [[Offantev Forest]], [[Massakev Savanna]], [[Kanteramiv Mountains]]

## Notes
- [[Ya'kali]] and [[Dahanal]] were temporarily under Godalosvia/Almanian Empire control before being liberated in the third year of the Bygone Era
