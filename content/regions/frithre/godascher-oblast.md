# Godascher Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
The central-northern oblast of [[Frithre]] and home to the national capital, [[Godascher]], situated at the confluence of the [[Tuskiev River]] and [[Kroviv River]] along the [[Sea of Hornaskia]]. Also home to the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]], the largest [[Polaenian Church]] on the continent.
