# Lomensk Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
A western oblast of [[Frithre]] defined by the [[Orbev Mountains]] and [[Orbev Lake]] to the north. Contains [[Navakeve]], historical capital of the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]].

## Cities
- [[Navakeve]], [[Lomensk]], [[Aleski]]
