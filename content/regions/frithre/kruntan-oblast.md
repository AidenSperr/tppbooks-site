# Kruntan Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
An eastern oblast of [[Frithre]] along the [[Sea of Hornaskia]], home to [[Porve Yatasha]], a large coastal city led by House Szakuvya.

- [[Porve Yatasha]], [[Sana Berveva]], [[Porve Tviv]], [[Kruntan]], [[Sana Kohlev]]
