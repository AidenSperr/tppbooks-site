# Minalsche Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
A northern oblast of [[Frithre]] largely flat and agricultural, historically associated with the old nation of [[Nabatia]]. Contains [[Nabat]], home of the [[Nabati]] ethnicity.

## Cities
- [[Nabat]], [[Bezharmav]], [[Minalshe]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Sharaczala Mountains]], [[Habet Prairie]], [[Tasema River]], [[Solavev River]], [[Gaelime Lake]], [[Hemiv Forest]], [[Navalnitachia Flatlands]], [[Bivalva Woods]], [[Sczamiv Mudplains]], [[Avivischi Floodplains]], [[Sosseviv Grasslands]], [[Bosharyav Forest]], [[Gasaleviv Flatlands]], [[Ibhouth Woods]], [[Thrumniavasch Hills]]

## Notes
- Formerly associated with [[Nabatia]] before consolidation into [[Frithre]]
