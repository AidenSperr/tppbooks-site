# Robosse Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
A central-northern oblast of [[Frithre]] that becomes a key strategic zone during the Huozhese invasion in Book II. The city of [[Robosse]] is a primary target in Huolong's assault on [[Frithre]].
