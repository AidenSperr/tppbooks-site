# Columil Oblast

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Administrative Type:** Oblast

## Overview
A southwestern oblast of [[Frithre]] bordering [[Almania]] and [[Birumiaka]] along the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] and [[Farilvana River]]. Home to the [[Church of Columil]].

## Cities
- [[Columil]], [[Mavinev]], [[Dhavaenev]]
