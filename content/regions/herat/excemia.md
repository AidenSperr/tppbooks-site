# Excemia

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A northwestern region of [[Herat]] with significant coastal access and home to the ancient city of [[Balina]]. The [[Taseman Peninsula]] extends from this region northward into the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]].

## Cities
- [[Balina]], [[Tallota]]

- [[Taseman Peninsula]], [[Bay of Balina]], [[Excemian Sea]], [[Taseman Mountains]], [[Falmane Hills]], [[Tranast Forest]], [[Tasema River]], [[Thaetrachese Mountains]], [[Ateman River]]
