# Bovalna

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A southwestern region of [[Herat]], home to [[Covana]], a large city with a notable [[Polaenian Church]] that features in Book I.

## Cities
- [[Covana]], [[Menacali]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Tasema River]], [[Falmane Hills]], [[Habet Prairie]], [[Slobeska Forest]]

## Notes
- [[Covana]]'s [[Polaenian Church]] is visited by the group in Book II; an Angelic priest there remarks that many followers stop reading the [[Book of Nasariane]] before its later books
