# E'elsavest

> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
A northern region of [[A'Amaer]], bordered by the [[Allsyenntsk Spring Mountains]] to the north. Home to the large city of [[Waveshe]], situated within the [[Missast Marsh]].

## Cities
- [[Waveshe]] — large city in the [[Missast Marsh]]
- [[Chasaspa]] — medium-sized village in the west-central portion of the region

## Geographic Features
- [[Allsyenntsk Spring Mountains]], [[Hestaha Woods]], [[Missast Marsh]], [[Skosva River]]
