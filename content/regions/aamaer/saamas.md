# Sa'amas

> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]]
> **Administrative Type:** Region

## Overview
The central region of [[A'Amaer]] and seat of the national capital. Home to [[Spola]], which sits on and around [[Lake Spola]]. The [[Marshes of Rising]] lie within this region, central to the Ghoulish [[Redemption]] process.

## Cities
- [[Spola]] — capital of [[A'Amaer]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Lake Spola]], [[Missast Marsh]], [[Marshes of Rising]], [[Alsvama Forest]], [[Assimisian River]], [[Dossaes River]], [[Skosva River]], [[Shiona Plains]]
