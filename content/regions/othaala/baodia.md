# Baodia

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
An eastern sector of [[Othaala]], formerly controlled by [[Huozhi]] until annexed following the [[Second Elven-Demonic War]]. Now functions as the only racial refuge for [[Demons]] within an Elven nation, home to a significant Demonic refugee community in [[Yanjiang]] and [[Godeng]].

## Cities
- [[Yanjiang]], [[Godeng]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Bhei Forest]], [[Yumang River]], [[Bay of Amekia]], [[Dhieba Mountains]], [[Baodian Sea]]

## History
Originally Huozhese territory. Annexed by [[Othaala]] during the [[Second Elven-Demonic War]], after which it was opened as a refuge for Demonic peoples fleeing persecution under Huozhese rule.

## Notes
- The only region in [[Othaala]] where [[Demons]] are permitted permanent residency, besides Kaedoa
