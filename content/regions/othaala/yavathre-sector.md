# Yavathre Sector

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
The central sector of [[Othaala]] and home to the national capital, [[Lumen]], situated atop the [[Yavathre Plateau]]. Also contains [[Kaedoa]], known as the City of Light and situated under the [[Grand Olbaerd Tree]], and [[Vahhlve]], home of the continent's largest [[Arveshian Shrine]].
