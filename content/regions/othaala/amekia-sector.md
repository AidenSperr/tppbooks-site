# Amekia Sector

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
An eastern border sector of [[Othaala]], the primary front line of the Huozhese invasion in Book II. Contains the [[Voemna Pass]], the key defensive chokepoint where the combined [[Othaala|Othaalan]] and [[Birumiaka|Birumiakese]] armies make their stand, and [[Almaej]], a major city that falls early in the conflict.

## Cities
- [[Macaraalo]], [[Silvakca]], [[Almaej]], [[Tuusalche]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Almaeji River]], [[Bay of Amekia]], [[Baodian Sea]], [[Flokcek Mountains]], [[Chokke Grasslands]], [[Sclove Mountains]], [[Paelotsha Forest]], [[Hot Streams of Olbaerd]], [[Aamarre Gap]], [[Voemna Pass]], [[Chokke Gap]]

## Notes
- [[Almaej]] is the first major city to fall in the Huozhese invasion; [[Tuusalche]] is evacuated shortly after
- The [[Voemna Pass]] is the site of the major defensive battle in Book II
