# Kulavren Sector

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
A southeastern sector of [[Othaala]] with extensive coastal access along the [[Sea of Novobriskia]], containing [[Plamauthe]], a large coastal city, and [[Elataha]], home to a major port military base.
