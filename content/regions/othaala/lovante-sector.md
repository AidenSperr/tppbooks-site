# Lovante Sector

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
A western sector of [[Othaala]] containing [[Knaema]], a major center of law and scholarship, along a densely populated coastline on the [[West Takromavian Ocean]]. The [[Lovante Peninsula]] extends southward from this region.
