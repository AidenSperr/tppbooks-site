# Warethoan Sector

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Administrative Type:** Sector

## Overview
A southwestern sector of [[Othaala]] extending across the [[Sohbra Peninsula]] and [[Akcta Peninsula]], with significant coastal access and several evacuation-route cities relevant to Book II.

## Cities
- [[Waretho]], [[Sivrrakta]], [[Yawebesa]], [[Hhevkce]], [[Nolaykwe]], [[Llakte]], [[Shegahle]], [[Maevete]], [[Dvaelemnev]], [[Alametre]]

## Geographic Features
- [[Sohbra Peninsula]], [[Akcta Peninsula]], [[Akcta Islands]], [[Nolaykian Bay]], [[Lake Gohna]], [[Shobrae River]], [[Alavi River]], [[Alavi Woods]], [[Gohna Swamps]], [[Merrana Grasslands]], [[Akkemitaene Forest]], [[Sovithiena River]], [[Bosiahha River]], [[Abbrrohhaella Floodplains]]

## Notes
- [[Yawebesa]] and [[Nolaykwe]] serve as departure points for southern sea evacuation routes in Book II
