# King Colchebushev

> **Race:** [[Humans|Human]]
> **Nation:** [[Godalosvia]]
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
A Human king of [[Godalosvia]] during the [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]], widely regarded as one of the great rulers of his era.

## Role / Occupation
King of [[Godalosvia]]. Played a key role in facilitating [[Ghoulish Independence]]. Was notably spoken to directly by the [[Five Old Gods]], making him one of the few mortal leaders to receive divine communication. Authored the books [[Colchebushev I]] and [[Colchebushev II]], both of which were later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]].
