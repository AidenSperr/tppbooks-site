# Tavu Beidaka

> **Race:** [[Elves|Elf]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Status:** Alive (as of Book II)

## Description
A Moon Elf man living in the [[Tvaiale District]] of [[Lumen]]. Gentle-natured and steady in his demeanor, with the strained posture of a man who has worked long hours for many years. Seen in Book II trimming boards outside the family home, his hands still working smoothly despite the toll of long labor.

## Role / Occupation
Works at a place of business within walking distance of the family home. The sole earner in the household for most of his children's upbringing, working increasingly long hours as [[Othaala]]'s economy declined and costs rose.

## Connections
- [[Valaeria Beidaka]] — wife; married twenty years prior to the start of Book I
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — elder daughter
- [[Torra Beidaka]] — younger daughter
- [[Dakkai Eletha]] — knows him; receives Dakkai when he visits the household in Book II

## Notes
- His marriage to [[Valaeria Beidaka]] has always seemed mismatched; his calm and kindness are the opposite of her cruelty
