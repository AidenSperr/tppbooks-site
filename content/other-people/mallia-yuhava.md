# Mallia Yuhava

> **Race:** [[Elves|Elf]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Status:** Alive (as of Book II)

## Description
A woman living in [[Lumen]] during the events of Book I and II. Mother of [[Emaera Yuhava]].

## Role / Occupation
Civilian and grieving mother. Departs [[Lumen]] with [[Dakkai Eletha]] and a small group heading south to [[Yawebesa]], then by ship to [[Nolaykwe]], carrying the remains of her son [[Emaera Yuhava|Emaera's]] urn.

## Connections
- [[Emaera Yuhava]] — her child, deceased
- [[Dakkai Eletha]] — escorts her during the southern evacuation; digs up Emaera's urn alongside her

## Notes
- She stayed temporarily with a [[Aevatre Ajjael]] employee named Jvali's family in the western Tvaiale district of [[Lumen]] before departing
- Her landlord had already evicted her by the time she left
