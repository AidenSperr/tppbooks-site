# Nasati Aerthuma

> **Race:** [[Elves|Elf]]
> **Nation:** [[Otanlla]]
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
An [[Elves|Elven]] soldier and explorer active during the [[Timeline of the Wyvernal Era|Wyvernal Era]].

## Role / Occupation
Field officer in the army of [[Otanlla]] during the [[Wyvernal Wars]]. Following the wars, explored the southeastern reaches of the continent and discovered the [[Six Faunan Kings]] in their then-ancient and nearly perishing state. Authored *[[Ravantis]]*, which was later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]].
