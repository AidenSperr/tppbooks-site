# Herian of Atema

> **Race:** [[Angels|Angel]]
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
A scholar and historian from [[Atema]], active during the [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]].

## Role / Occupation
Historian. Completed *[[Delmervas]]* in 263 c.e., the first comprehensive written history of the [[Angels|Angelic]] people, from the founding of [[Tabatenn]] to the then-present day. It was publicly read in [[Atema Central Hall]] and distributed across [[Herat]] and neighboring regions, and is widely regarded as the earliest major history book produced on [[Alatrya]].

## Connections
- [[Delmervas]] — authored; later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]]
- [[Atema]] — city of origin and publication
