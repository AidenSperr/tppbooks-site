# Torra Beidaka

> **Race:** [[Elves|Elf]]
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Status:** Alive (as of Book II)

## Description
A Moon Elf girl, a few years younger than [[Anue Beidaka]], still attending Catalia (tertiary school) at the time of Book II's opening. Quiet and shy, with her hair typically pulled into a long ponytail. Resembles her mother [[Valaeria Beidaka]] closely, though without her freckles.

## Role / Occupation
Student and civilian.

## Connections
- [[Anue Beidaka]] — elder sister
- [[Tavu Beidaka]] — father
- [[Valaeria Beidaka]] — mother
- [[Dakkai Eletha]] — brings him a maroon scarf of Anue's when he visits the household, asking him to give it to her if he sees her

## Notes
- Steps quietly back when tension rises between her parents and Dakkai; observant but unassertive
- The maroon scarf she tries to pass along is a small but telling detail about her relationship with Anue
