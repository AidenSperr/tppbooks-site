# Savel Godarev of Lapentsk

> **Race:** [[Humans|Human]]
> **Nation:** [[Godalosvia]]
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
An early historian from [[Lapentsk]], active during the [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]].

## Role / Occupation
Historian and author. Completed *[[Frithranis]]* in 266 c.e., a history of the [[Humans|Human]] peoples, published quickly and widely distributed. The text was later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]].
