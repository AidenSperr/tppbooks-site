# Theraniel of Lumen

> **Race:** [[Elves|Elf]]
> **Nation:** [[Otanlla]]
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
An [[Elves|Elven]] historian and author from [[Lumen]], active during the [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]].

## Role / Occupation
Historian. Completed *[[Elemelfasa]]* in 265 c.e., a detailed history of the [[Elves|Elven]] peoples documenting the rise of [[Otanlla]], its cultural achievements, and its enduring political complexities. The work was later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]].
