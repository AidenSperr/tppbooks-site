# Khoren of Xeingang

> **Race:** [[Demons|Demon]]
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]] (pre-founding [[Xeingang]])
> **Status:** Deceased

## Description
A [[Bone-Singer|Bone-Singer]] elder from [[Xeingang]], active during the [[Timeline of the Conception Era|Conception Era]].

## Role / Occupation
Bone-Singer and historian. Compiled *[[Vichanera]]* in 264 c.e., documenting the scattered histories of the [[Demons|Demonic]] peoples from the [[Sharaczala Mountains]] to the northern plains. The work was shared cautiously beyond [[Demons|Demonic]] lands following the success of *[[Delmervas]]*.

## Connections
- [[Vichanera]] — authored; later included in the [[Book of Nasariane]]
- [[Bone-Singer]] — member of this oral historian tradition
- [[Xeingang]] — city of origin
