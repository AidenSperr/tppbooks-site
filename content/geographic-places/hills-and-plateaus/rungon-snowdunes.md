# Rungon Snowdunes

> **Type:** Snowdunes
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Sonueng]]

## Overview
A large area of snowdunes in northern [[Sonueng]], one of the harsher frozen landscapes within [[Huozhi]]'s northern territory. Contains the cities of [[Zovinu]], [[Vantung]], and [[Tengfung]].
