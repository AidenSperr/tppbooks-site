# Klovalmi Hills

> **Type:** Hills
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
An extremely hilly area between the [[Slovaera Mountains]] to the east and the [[Traoniel Cliffs]] to the west, in the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Contains the cities of [[Dajaerve]] at the western edge and [[Vahhlve]] at the eastern edge, the latter home to the largest [[Arveshian Shrine]] on the continent.
