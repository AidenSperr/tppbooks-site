# Taseman Plateau

> **Type:** Plateau
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Obbestra]]

## Overview
A towering rocky plateau in [[Obbestra]], [[Qauta]], with cliffs thousands of meters high. Not prominently marked on overhead maps but a significant geographic structure. Contains the [[Great Ice Crystal Forest]], [[Follicce Snowdunes]], and [[North Tunnoa Woods]] on its upper reaches.

## Connected Locations
- [[Obbestra]], [[Great Ice Crystal Forest]], [[Follicce Snowdunes]], [[North Tunnoa Woods]]

## Notes
- Despite its scale, it does not appear prominently on standard continental maps
