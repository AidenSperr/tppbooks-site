# Sholle Hills

> **Type:** Hills
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]] (south), [[Lovante Sector]] (north)

## Overview
A hilly area straddling the northwestern [[Yavathre Sector]] and northeastern [[Lovante Sector]]. The Birumiakese army descends through the Sholle Hills on their march from [[Suvrra]] toward the [[Sclove Mountains]], finding the terrain lower, more humid, and with taller, wilder grasses than the plateau above.

## Connected Locations
- [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Lovante Sector]], [[Suvrra]], [[Sclove Mountains]]

## Notes
- Described in Book II as rolling broad undulations catching morning sun like folds in a woven cloth; darker soil, taller grass, and ferns
