# Yavathre Plateau

> **Type:** Plateau
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
The dominant geographic feature of central [[Othaala]], forming the main landmass of the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Stretches from around the [[Kahletre Forest]] in the west to the [[Slovaera Mountains]] in the east, elevated well above the surrounding lowlands. The capital [[Lumen]] sits atop it, as do the cities of [[Suvrra]], [[Taebahe]], [[Akcolvathe]], [[Kaedoa]], [[Vahhlve]], [[Dajaerve]], and [[Thohvaer]]. The plateau's edge is defined by the [[Traoniel Cliffs]] to the east and drops sharply toward the western wetlands and [[Davla Lake]].
