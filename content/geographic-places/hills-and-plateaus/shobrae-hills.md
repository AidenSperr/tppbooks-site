# Shobrae Hills

> **Type:** Hills
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]

## Overview
A hilly area in the southern [[Lovante Sector]], through which the [[Shobrae River]] runs. Several coastal villages including [[Elmetcht]] and [[Mavaj]] are set within them.
