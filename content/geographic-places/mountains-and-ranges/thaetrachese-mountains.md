# Thaetrachese Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]], [[Kalavam]], [[Bovalna]], [[Centrem]]

## Overview
A tall mountain range running through [[Excemia]], [[Kalavam]], [[Bovalna]], and [[Centrem]] in [[Herat]]. Contains the [[Ateman River]] and the [[Great Ateman Waterfall]], and the [[Habet Canyon]] cuts through it near [[Centrem]].
