# Braelliae Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Kulavren Sector]], [[Columil Oblast]]

## Overview
A mountain range forming the divide between the [[Kulavren Sector]] of [[Othaala]] and [[Columil Oblast]] of [[Frithre]]. Contains the [[Saine Valley]], a large open valley on its southern side, and a significant portion of the [[Collavajaen River]]. The cities of [[Tuhve]] and [[Nahae]] sit near or within it.
