# Funiketa Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]] / [[A'Amaer]]

## Overview
A mountain chain marking the border between [[Otava Prefecture]] and [[Satsawa Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]] and [[Salsesa]] and [[Shikena Prefecture]] across the border in [[A'Amaer]].
