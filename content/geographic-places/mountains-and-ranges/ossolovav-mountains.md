# Ossolovav Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Almania]] / [[Frithre]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Koleva]], [[Heviava]], [[Katav]], [[Columil Oblast]], [[Zovamnev Oblast]], [[Doutoula Prefecture]]

## Overview
A long mountain range forming the border between [[Almania]] and [[Frithre]] and a smaller border section between [[Almania]] and [[Birumiaka]]. Bordering regions include [[Columil Oblast]], [[Zovamnev Oblast]], [[Doutoula Prefecture]], [[Koleva]], [[Heviava]], and [[Katav]]. The southern offshoot of the range forms the [[Valemvakia Mountains]]. Named in part for a companion of [[Ronaela Astarise]] who died in the [[Slovaera Mountains]] during her solo expedition. The mountains were named for them posthumously.
