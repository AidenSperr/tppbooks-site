# Sharaczala Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Minalsche Oblast]]

## Overview
A large mountain range spanning [[Lomensk Oblast]] and [[Minalsche Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. The ancient town of [[Bezharmav]] sits up against the range, which historically served as the site of the first Demonic encampments in [[Frithre]] and as the original homeland of the people of the former nation of [[Nabatia]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Minalsche Oblast]], [[Bezharmav]]

## Notes
- Historical site of first [[Demons|Demonic]] camps in [[Frithre]]
- Associated with the origins of the [[Nabati]] people
