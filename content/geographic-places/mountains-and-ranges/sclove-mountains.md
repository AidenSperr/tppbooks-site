# Sclove Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]

## Overview
A small mountain range against the [[Flokcek Mountains]] in the [[Amekia Sector]], to their southwest. The Birumiakese army traverses the lower foothills of the Sclove on their march toward the [[Aamarre Gap]] and [[Voemna Pass]] in Book II, finding them harder and less forgiving terrain than the Sholle Hills behind them.

## Connected Locations
- [[Flokcek Mountains]], [[Aamarre Gap]], [[Amekia Sector]]

## Notes
- Foothills described in Book II as exposed shale and speckled granite breaking through dark soil and brown clay; wind blows east to west from the Sclove against the direction of march
