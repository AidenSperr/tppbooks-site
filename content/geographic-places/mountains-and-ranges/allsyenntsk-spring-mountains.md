# Allsyenntsk Spring Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[A'Amaer]]
> **Region:** [[Soverteva Oblast]], [[E'elsavest]]

## Overview
An extremely large mountain range spanning eastern [[Soverteva Oblast]] in [[Frithre]] and the northern border of [[E'elsavest]] in [[A'Amaer]]. Contains the city of [[Soverteva]] and the extensive [[Allsyenntsk Springs]], a plateau of springs, geysers, and volcanic pools that forms one of the more distinctive geothermal landscapes on the continent.
