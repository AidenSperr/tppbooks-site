# Chomien Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Northern Flamelands]], [[Kashteni]], [[Vinzhin]]

## Overview
A large volcanic mountain range spanning the [[Northern Flamelands]], western [[Kashteni]], and northern [[Vinzhin]] in [[Huozhi]]. Contains [[Kashtet's Hearth]], an active volcano that erupts approximately every fifty years, and the holy village of [[Piaofuzhen]] in its southern foothills.
