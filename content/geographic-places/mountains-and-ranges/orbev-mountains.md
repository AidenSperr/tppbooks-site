# Orbev Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Dascenvl]]

## Overview
A mountain range marking the northeastern border between [[Frithre]] and [[Othaala]], mainly in [[Lomensk Oblast]] with the [[Dascenvl]] region of [[Qauta]] to the north. Contains the [[Hot Streams of Olbaerd]] and [[Orbev Lake]], a high-class vacation destination. Originally controlled by the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]] before consolidation into [[Frithre]].
