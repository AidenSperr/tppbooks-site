# Barumisu Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Namik Prefecture]], [[Polimeva Prefecture]], [[Tahevina Prefecture]]

## Overview
A mountain range spanning [[Namik Prefecture]], [[Polimeva Prefecture]], and [[Tahevina Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]], forming the northeastern interior of the nation. The [[Vane Gap]] cuts through the northeastern portion of the range.

## Connected Locations
- [[Namik Prefecture]], [[Polimeva Prefecture]], [[Tahevina Prefecture]], [[Vane Gap]]
