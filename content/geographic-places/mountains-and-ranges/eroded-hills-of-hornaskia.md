# Eroded Hills of Hornaskia

> **Type:** Eroded Hills / Old Mountains
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Kruntan Oblast]]

## Overview
Old and heavily eroded small mountains in north-central [[Kruntan Oblast]], along the [[Sea of Hornaskia]]. Their worn and flattened profile marks them as significantly older formations than the active mountain ranges elsewhere on the continent.

## Connected Locations
- [[Kruntan Oblast]], [[Sea of Hornaskia]]

## Notes
- Their erosion suggests considerable geological age relative to other ranges on [[Alatrya]]
