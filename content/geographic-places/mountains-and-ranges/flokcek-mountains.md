# Flokcek Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]

## Overview
A mountain chain in the [[Amekia Sector]], continuing northward from the [[Slovaera Mountains]]. The [[Voemna Pass]] cuts through the northern [[Flokcek Mountains]], and the [[Aamarre Gap]] lies between the Flokcek and [[Sclove Mountains]]. The eastern sun rises over the Flokcek peaks, casting the Aamarre Gap and its ridges into shadow during morning hours.

## Connected Locations

## Notes
- Continuation of the [[Slovaera Mountains]] northward
- The [[Voemna Pass]], the key defensive position of Book II, cuts through the northern portion of this range
