# Villeva Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Kalavam]], [[Divinava]]

## Overview
A short mountain range in [[Kalavam]] and [[Divinava]] in [[Herat]], believed to be part of the same formation as the [[Endall Mountains]] but interrupted by the [[Navalnitachia Flatlands]]. The [[Villeva Peninsula]] extends from this range.
