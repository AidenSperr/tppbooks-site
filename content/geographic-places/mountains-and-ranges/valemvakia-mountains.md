# Valemvakia Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Almania]]
> **Region:** [[Koleva]]

## Overview
A southern offshoot of the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] in the uninhabited [[Koleva]] region of [[Almania]]. Contains [[Mount Valemvakia]], the tallest mountain on the continent.
