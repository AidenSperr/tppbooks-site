# Bhahare Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]

## Overview
A mountain range in the west of the [[Lovante Sector]], historically significant as the site of the first [[Elves|Elven]] settlements on [[Alatrya]]. The [[Besa Gap]] cuts through the range, serving as the main overland passage between the western coast and the plateau interior. [[Nahavre]] sits on the eastern side of the mountains near the gap.

## Connected Locations
- [[Lovante Sector]], [[Besa Gap]], [[Nahavre]], [[Elves]]

## Notes
- The Elven people first settled the Bhahare Mountains before expanding into what became [[Otanlla]] and then [[Othaala]]
