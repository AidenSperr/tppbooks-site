# Mount Thuyaellfe

> **Type:** Mountain
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A mountain in the [[Slovaera Mountains]] notable for a rarely-visited [[Ulmanaean Shrine]] at or near its peak. Anue and Chuntian camp near the shrine area and meet [[Arveshia]] there during their trek in Book I.

## Connected Locations
- [[Slovaera Mountains]], [[Ulmanaean Shrine]], [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Notes
- [[Arveshia]] visits Anue and Chuntian near the Ulmanaean pavilion on this mountain in Book I
- The shrine is considered rarely visited, making it an unusual site for such a divine encounter
