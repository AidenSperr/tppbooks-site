# Slovaera Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A mountain range in the [[Yavathre Sector]] that separates the [[Yavathre Plateau]] from the [[Klovalmi Hills]] to the west. [[Dajaerve]] sits at its western base. The range contains [[Mount Slovaerathukalia]], its tallest peak, and [[Mount Thuyaellfe]], a mountain with a rarely-visited [[Ulmanaean Shrine]]. Anue and Chuntian traverse these mountains in Book I on their way to [[Dajaerve]]. The [[Umerr Forest]] runs through the terrain leading up to the range from the plateau side.
