# Shoneng Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Qauta]] / [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Ttuventk]], [[Rgrenzde]], [[Sonueng]], [[Jofiena]]

## Overview
A tall mountain range defining a significant stretch of the borders between [[Frithre]], [[Qauta]], and [[Huozhi]]. Contains the [[Alkta River]] and borders [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Ttuventk]], [[Rgrenzde]], [[Sonueng]], and [[Jofiena]] across the three nations.

## Connected Locations
- [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Ttuventk]], [[Rgrenzde]], [[Sonueng]], [[Jofiena]], [[Alkta River]]

## Notes
- A key continental border marker between the northwest and the Huozhese frontier
