# Endall Mountains

> **Type:** Mountain Range
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Mavisktev Oblast]], [[Novernia]], [[Colvescum]]

## Overview
A long mountain chain running from the [[Endall Peninsula]] in northeastern [[Mavisktev Oblast]] southward into [[Novernia]] and [[Colvescum]] in [[Herat]]. The [[Villeva Mountains]] are believed to be a continuation of the same range, interrupted by the [[Navalnitachia Flatlands]].
