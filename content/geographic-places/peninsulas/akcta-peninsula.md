# Akcta Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]

## Overview
A small peninsula in the south of the [[Warethoan Sector]] along the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]]. Home to the village of [[Llakte]] and the [[Akcta Islands]] off its tip. Referenced as a potential far-southern evacuation destination during Book II.
