# Endall Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Mavisktev Oblast]]

## Overview
A large peninsula in northeastern [[Mavisktev Oblast]] extending into the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] and the [[Sea of Hornaskia]]. The [[Endall Mountains]] run its length. Contains the tribal village of [[Tuithe'la'nat]] at its tip, home of the [[Tuithes]], as well as the city of [[Novaviscz]]. The [[Tuithe Islands]] extend from its tip as a chain connected by wood and rope bridges.
