# Juvawe Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Obemiva Prefecture]]

## Overview
A long, thin peninsula in [[Obemiva Prefecture]], marking the southern edge of the [[Sea of Samaiku]]. The city of [[Kavesana]] is situated on it with coastline on both the [[Sea of Samaiku]] and [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]], and the [[Eikalive Beaches]] span the width of its base. The [[Juvawe Islands]] extend from its tip.
