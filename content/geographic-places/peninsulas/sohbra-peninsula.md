# Sohbra Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Overview
A westward-facing peninsula on the southwestern corner of [[Alatrya]], extending into the [[West Takromavian Ocean]] from the [[Warethoan Sector]]. Home to the city of [[Dvaelemnev]].
