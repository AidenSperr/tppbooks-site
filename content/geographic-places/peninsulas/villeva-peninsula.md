# Villeva Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Kalavam]]

## Overview
A thin but long peninsula in northern [[Kalavam]] that divides the [[Excemian Sea]] to the west from the [[Tavirian Sea]] to the east. Defined by the [[Villeva Mountains]] running along its length.

## Connected Locations
- [[Kalavam]], [[Excemian Sea]], [[Tavirian Sea]], [[Villeva Mountains]]
