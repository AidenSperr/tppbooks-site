# Lovante Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]

## Overview
A medium-sized peninsula extending into the [[West Takromavian Ocean]] from the [[Lovante Sector]], on which most of the city of [[Knaema]] is built. The [[Knaeman Bay]] lies to its south, and the [[Lovante Sector]] takes its name from the peninsula.
