# Taseman Peninsula

> **Type:** Peninsula
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]]

## Overview
A large peninsula in northern [[Excemia]] extending northward into the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]]. Contains the [[Taseman Mountains]] and the city of [[Tallota]] at its southern tip. The [[Bay of Balina]] indents its coast near [[Balina]].
