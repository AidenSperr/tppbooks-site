# Dothesta Plains

> **Type:** Plains
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A flat, grassy plain encompassing most of the [[Yavathre Plateau]] in the [[Yavathre Sector]], containing the cities of [[Thohvaer]], [[Lumen]], and [[Akcolvathe]]. The [[Lake of the Twins]] sits within this plain outside [[Lumen]].
