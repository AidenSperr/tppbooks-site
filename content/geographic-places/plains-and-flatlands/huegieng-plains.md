# Huegieng Plains

> **Type:** Plains
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Jofiena]]

## Overview
A large plain encompassing most of [[Jofiena]] in western [[Huozhi]], containing the cities of [[Muyunfa]], [[Yufengu]], and [[Anjing]]. The Huozhese staging forces move through this plain toward the Othaalan border in Book II.
