# Chokke Grasslands

> **Type:** Grasslands
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]

## Overview
A large grassland area in the southeast of the [[Amekia Sector]], between the [[Flokcek Mountains]] and the [[Sclove Mountains]]. The village of [[Tuusalche]] sits north of these grasslands; Raomi evacuates the village's population across them toward [[Vahhlve]] in Book II.
