# Merrana Grasslands

> **Type:** Grasslands
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]

## Overview
A large low grassland in the [[Warethoan Sector]], through which the [[Sovithiena River]] runs. [[Yawebesa]] sits within it along the river, serving as a waypoint on the southern evacuation route in Book II.
