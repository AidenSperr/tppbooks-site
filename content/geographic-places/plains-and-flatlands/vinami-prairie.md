# Vinami Prairie

> **Type:** Prairie
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Doutoula Prefecture]], [[Satsawa Prefecture]], [[Zovamnev Oblast]]

## Overview
A large prairie mostly in [[Doutoula Prefecture]] and [[Satsawa Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]], with a small area extending into [[Zovamnev Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. The village of [[Vinami]] sits within it.
