# Navalnitachia Flatlands

> **Type:** Flatlands
> **Nation:** [[Herat]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Divinava]], [[Colvescum]], [[Novernia]], [[Minalsche Oblast]]

## Overview
A large flatland mostly in [[Divinava]], [[Colvescum]], and [[Novernia]] in [[Herat]], with a small portion in northern [[Minalsche Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. Believed to be the interruption that separates the [[Villeva Mountains]] from the [[Endall Mountains]] geologically.
