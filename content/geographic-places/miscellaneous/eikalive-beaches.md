# Eikalive Beaches

> **Type:** Beaches
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Obemiva Prefecture]]

## Overview
A group of scenic beaches spanning the width of the base of the [[Juvawe Peninsula]] in [[Obemiva Prefecture]], along the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]]. The city of [[Eikalive]] is situated directly on them.
