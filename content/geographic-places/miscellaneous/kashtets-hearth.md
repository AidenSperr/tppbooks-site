# Kashtet's Hearth

> **Type:** Volcano
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Northern Flamelands]]

## Overview
An extremely large and typically active volcano within the [[Chomien Mountains]] in the [[Northern Flamelands]] of [[Huozhi]]. Erupts approximately every fifty years. Considered a sacred marker of [[Kashtet]]'s presence in the land, and the most tangible sign of the Fire God's ongoing influence in the physical world. The [[Northern Flamelands]] take their character largely from its activity.

## Connected Locations
- [[Chomien Mountains]], [[Northern Flamelands]], [[Kashtet]], [[Huozhi]]

## Notes
- Its eruption cycle of roughly fifty years is well-documented by Huozhese scholars
- Lavastones mined nearby are refined into manatic catalysts under [[Huolong]]'s regime
