# Traoniel Cliffs

> **Type:** Cliffs
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
An iconic series of cliffs in the [[Yavathre Sector]], interrupted throughout by trees and brush and forming the eastern face of the elevated terrain between the [[Klovalmi Hills]] and the [[Yavathre Plateau]]. Home to both [[Kaedoa]], the City of Light, and the [[Grand Olbaerd Tree]]. The cliffs define the boundary between the Klovalmi Hills below and the plateau above, and the [[Vahhlve]] sits at their western edge.
