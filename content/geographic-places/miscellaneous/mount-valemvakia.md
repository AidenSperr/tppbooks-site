# Mount Valemvakia

> **Type:** Mountain
> **Nation:** [[Almania]]
> **Region:** [[Koleva]]

## Overview
The tallest mountain on the continent of [[Alatrya]], located in the [[Valemvakia Mountains]] in the uninhabited [[Koleva]] region of [[Almania]].
