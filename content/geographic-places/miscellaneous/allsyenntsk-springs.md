# Allsyenntsk Springs

> **Type:** Springs / Geothermal Feature
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[A'Amaer]]
> **Region:** [[Soverteva Oblast]], [[E'elsavest]]

## Overview
A large, elevated area of springs, geysers, and volcanic pools within the [[Allsyenntsk Spring Mountains]], spanning the border region between [[Soverteva Oblast]] in [[Frithre]] and [[E'elsavest]] in [[A'Amaer]].
