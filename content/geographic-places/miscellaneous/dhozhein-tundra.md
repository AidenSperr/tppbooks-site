# Dhozhein Tundra

> **Type:** Tundra
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Kashteni]], [[Vinzhin]], [[Northern Flamelands]]

## Overview
A large frozen tundra spanning the northern extent of [[Huozhi]], covering portions of [[Kashteni]], [[Vinzhin]], and the [[Northern Flamelands]]. Despite the extreme cold, several cities exist within it, including [[Penjung]], [[Songbein]], and [[Taningang]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Kashteni]], [[Vinzhin]], [[Northern Flamelands]], [[Penjung]], [[Songbein]], [[Taningang]]

## Notes
- The coexistence of the frozen tundra and the volcanic [[Northern Flamelands]] makes northern [[Huozhi]] one of the most geographically extreme regions on the continent
