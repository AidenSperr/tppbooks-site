# Grand Olbaerd Tree

> **Type:** Landmark Tree
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
The largest tree in the world, situated in the [[Traoniel Cliffs]] near [[Kaedoa]] in the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Closely associated with [[Lavi]], Goddess of Nature, whose connection to the tree gives [[Kaedoa]] its name as the City of Light. The tree's canopy is vast enough to shelter the city below it, and its roots form part of the natural terrain of the cliffs. A large floor mosaic depicting the Grand Olbaerd Tree is inlaid in the cathedral hall of the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]], testament to its continental religious significance.

## Connected Locations

## Notes
- The Grand Olbaerd Tree mosaic in the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]] is one of the most notable artistic depictions of it outside of [[Othaala]]
- Gilded pennants bearing the crests of every major Othaalan city fly from [[Basehharra Palace]], including one bearing the open circle of stars encasing Epala that represents [[Kaedoa]], a reference to the tree
