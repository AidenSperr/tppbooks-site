# Great Ice Crystal Forest

> **Type:** Ice Crystal Formation
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Rgrenzde]]

## Overview
A large forest of permanent ice crystals growing upward from the ground in [[Rgrenzde]], northern [[Qauta]]. The largest known crystal stands approximately fifty feet tall. Situated near the city of [[Qantua]] and the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], it is one of the most distinctive natural landmarks on the continent.

## Connected Locations
- [[Rgrenzde]], [[Qantua]], [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[Taseman Plateau]]

## Notes
- The crystals are permanent and do not appear to melt even in warmer seasons
- One of the more surreal natural landmarks on [[Alatrya]]
