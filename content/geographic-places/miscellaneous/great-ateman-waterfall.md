# Great Ateman Waterfall

> **Type:** Waterfall
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Centrem]]

## Overview
An extremely large waterfall in the [[Thaetrachese Mountains]] near the capital city of [[Atema]] in [[Centrem]]. One of the defining natural landmarks of [[Herat]] and a point of civic and cultural pride. The [[Ateman River]] originates here.
