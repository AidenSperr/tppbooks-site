# Marshes of Rising

> **Type:** Marsh
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]]

## Overview
A small, bioluminescent marsh in [[Sa'amas]], the central region of [[A'Amaer]], where the [[Risen]] process occurs. This is the site where souls deemed unworthy of entering [[Polaena]]'s realm upon death are reborn in [[Ghouls|Ghoulish]] form to begin their path toward [[Redemption]]. The bioluminescence of the marsh is understood to be a manifestation of the divine process occurring within it.

## Connected Locations
- [[Sa'amas]], [[A'Amaer]], [[Spola]], [[Ghouls]], [[Polaena]]

## Notes
- The theological and civic purpose of [[A'Amaer]] as a nation is organized around this site
- Raomi Allamaera references "the swamps of Spola" as a fate to be avoided. The deprivation of physical touch that comes with being a Ghoul is described as one of its curses
