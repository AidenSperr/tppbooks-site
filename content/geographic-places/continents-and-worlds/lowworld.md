# Lowworld

> **Type:** Underworld / Subterranean Realm
> **Nation:** N/A
> **Region:** Beneath [[Alatrya]]

## Overview
The realm beneath the surface of [[Alatrya]], also known as [[Prylkya]]. According to theology, those deemed unholy at death are sent here rather than to [[Polaena]]'s realm. It is the domain of [[The Low]] and his three sons, and is governed by them across its capital and surrounding territories. Its existence is considered myth by most surface mortals; those who know of it include the [[Polaenian Church]] and the [[Pyantyadha Eltyr]].
