# Alatrya

> **Type:** Continent
> **Nation:** N/A
> **Region:** N/A

## Overview
The primary continent of [[Nasariane]] and the setting of all surface events in The Polaenian Prophecies. Created by [[Polaena]] as the first continent on the planet. Home to the eight mortal races and the [[Eight Alatryan Nations]], as well as the [[Lowworld]] beneath it. The continent encompasses a vast range of geography from the frozen northern reaches of [[Qauta]] to the volcanic wastelands of [[Huozhi]] and the southern islands of [[Almania]].
