# Slobeska Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Herat]] / [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Fnolkischta]], [[Bovalna]]

## Overview
A large forest spanning northeastern [[Lomensk Oblast]] in [[Frithre]], [[Fnolkischta]] in [[Qauta]], and [[Bovalna]] in [[Herat]], named for the [[Slobeska River]] that runs nearby.
