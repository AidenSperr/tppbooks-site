# North Tunnoa Woods

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Rgrenzde]]

## Overview
A haunted forest on the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] in [[Rgrenzde]], the northern counterpart to the [[South Tunnoa Woods]]. Both are considered additionally haunted beyond the ordinary superstitions associated with remote northern forests.

## Connected Locations
- [[Rgrenzde]], [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[South Tunnoa Woods]]

## Notes
- Both Tunnoa Woods are considered haunted; the source of the haunting is not documented
