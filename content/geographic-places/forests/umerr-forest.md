# Umerr Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
An extremely dangerous and dark forest on the [[Yavathre Plateau]] between the [[Dothesta Plains]] and the [[Slovaera Mountains]], frequently rumored to contain a [[Church of the Low]]. Described as the near-opposite of [[Pfapohrrv Forest]], darkness seeps outward from its brush even at the treeline, and the dense tangle of bushes and vines impedes the trail like a claustrophobia-inducing mass. Screams and unidentifiable sounds can be heard emanating from deep within the forest at night.

Anue passes through the Umerr Forest in Book I traveling west from [[Thohvaer]] toward [[Dajaerve]], and the Gemwielders' convoy passes through it again in Book II on their route toward the [[Amekia Sector]].
