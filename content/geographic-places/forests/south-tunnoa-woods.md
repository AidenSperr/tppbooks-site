# South Tunnoa Woods

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]

## Overview
A haunted forest on the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] in [[Obbestra]], the southern counterpart to the [[North Tunnoa Woods]]. Both are considered additionally haunted beyond the ordinary superstitions associated with remote northern forests.
