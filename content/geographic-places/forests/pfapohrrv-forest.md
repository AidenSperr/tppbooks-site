# Pfapohrrv Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A glowing forest east of [[Lumen]] on the [[Yavathre Plateau]], named for the Grotaarre Trees that dominate its flora. Grotaarre Trees have tall trunks with sparse twig-like lower branches and thickening foliage toward the crown, whose flowers glow green-yellow year-round, reflecting light from the moons and mingling with the bioluminescent glowbugs that populate the forest floor. The result is a forest navigable at night by its own light.

The forest is most significant as the location of [[Arveshia]]'s Gem altar, a hidden meadow of plain white flowers deep within the trees, at whose far end a black mist created by Arveshia herself concealed a small stone altar bearing a Skrrove statue and the Gem beneath a sealed inscription. [[Vaethu]] and [[Aeliave]] of [[Cavant A-Meftua]] lived in a cabin just past the altar and guarded it for decades until [[Anue Beidaka]] arrived in Book I.
