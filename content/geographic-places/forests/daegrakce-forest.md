# Daegrakce Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A large forest in the southeastern [[Yavathre Sector]], bordering the [[Slovaera Mountains]] to the east and [[Suvrra]] to the north. The Birumiakese army passes near its edge on their march south from [[Suvrra]] in Book II.
