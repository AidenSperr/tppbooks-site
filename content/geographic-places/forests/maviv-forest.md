# Maviv Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Godascher Oblast]]

## Overview
A large forest in eastern [[Godascher Oblast]], through which part of the city of [[Godascher]] extends. The capital's edge reaches into the [[Maviv Forest]], giving it a distinctive forested character on its eastern side. The [[Maviv Sea]] and forest share their name.
