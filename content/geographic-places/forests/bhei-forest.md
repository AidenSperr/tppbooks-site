# Bhei Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Baodia]]

## Overview
An old and cold forest in [[Baodia]], the Demonic refugee sector of [[Othaala]]. One of the few forested areas in the eastern reaches of the nation. The original location of the [[Wind]] [[Gem]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Baodia]], [[Yumang River]]

## Notes
- Often visited on weekends by [[Chuntian Zhenfung]], she found the [[Gem]] of [[Vathem]] within the trees.
