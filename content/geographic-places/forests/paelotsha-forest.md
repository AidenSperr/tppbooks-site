# Paelotsha Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]], [[Lomensk Oblast]]

## Overview
A large forest on the western side of the [[Orbev Mountains]], spanning both the [[Amekia Sector]] in [[Othaala]] and [[Lomensk Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. The Gemwielders travel through the Paelotsha en route from [[Lomensk Oblast]] toward the [[Voemna Pass]] in Book II.
