# Lovyov Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Lomensk Oblast]]

## Overview
A holy forest in north-central [[Lomensk Oblast]], near [[Navakeve]]. The Gemwielders pass through the Lovyov after the fall of [[Navakeve]] in Book II, where a Navaki shepherd on the road tells them of the near-total destruction of the city and the fate of its people.

## Connected Locations
- [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Navakeve]]

## Notes
- The encounter with Arkady the shepherd takes place at the Lovyov's edge on the road into [[Aleski]] in Book II
- The forest's holy designation is not explained in surviving texts but is generally understood among the locals
