# Kahletre Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]], [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A large forest on the south and east of the [[Bhahare Mountains]] in the [[Lovante Sector]], marking the western edge of the [[Yavathre Sector]] and the [[Yavathre Plateau]]. Serves as a natural transitional boundary between the coastal lowlands and the plateau.
