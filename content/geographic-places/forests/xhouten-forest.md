# Xhouten Forest

> **Type:** Forest
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Jofiena]], [[Robosse Oblast]]

## Overview
A medium-sized forest mostly in [[Jofiena]] in [[Huozhi]] with a small portion extending into [[Robosse Oblast]] in [[Frithre]], along the [[Alkta River]]. Part of the terrain through which the Huozhese forces advance in Book II.
