# Nolaykian Bay

> **Type:** Bay
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]

## Overview
A small bay from the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]] in the south of the [[Warethoan Sector]], against which the city of [[Nolaykwe]] sits. Used as a departure point for sea evacuations during Book II.
