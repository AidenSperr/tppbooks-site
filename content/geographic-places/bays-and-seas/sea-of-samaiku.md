# Sea of Samaiku

> **Type:** Sea
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Obemiva Prefecture]], [[Namik Prefecture]], [[Polimeva Prefecture]]

## Overview
A large sea bordering the eastern coast of [[Birumiaka]], touching [[Obemiva Prefecture]], [[Namik Prefecture]], and [[Polimeva Prefecture]]. Named for the capital city of [[Samaiku]], which sits on its coast.
