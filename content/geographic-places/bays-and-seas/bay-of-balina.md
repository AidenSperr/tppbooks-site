# Bay of Balina

> **Type:** Bay
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]]

## Overview
A small bay on the [[Taseman Peninsula]] in [[Excemia]], surrounded by the ancient city of [[Balina]]. Opens from the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]].

## Connected Locations
- [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[Balina]], [[Taseman Peninsula]], [[Excemia]]

## Notes
- [[Balina]] is named for this bay, or vice versa; one of the oldest inhabited coastal features in [[Herat]]
