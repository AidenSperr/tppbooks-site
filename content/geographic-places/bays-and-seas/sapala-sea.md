# Sapala Sea

> **Type:** Sea
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]] / [[Almania]]
> **Region:** [[Doutoula Prefecture]], [[Heviava]], [[Bolavi]]

## Overview
A small sea from the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]] bordering [[Doutoula Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]] and the [[Heviava]] and [[Bolavi]] regions of [[Almania]]. The city of [[Sapala]] sits on its [[Birumiaka|Birumiakese]] shore.
