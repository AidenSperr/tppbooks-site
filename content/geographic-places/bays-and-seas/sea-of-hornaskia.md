# Sea of Hornaskia

> **Type:** Sea
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Mavisktev Oblast]], [[Godascher Oblast]], [[Soverteva Oblast]], [[Kruntan Oblast]]

## Overview
A large sea forming the northern coastline of [[Frithre]], bordering [[Mavisktev Oblast]], [[Godascher Oblast]], [[Soverteva Oblast]], and [[Kruntan Oblast]]. The capital [[Godascher]] sits at the confluence of the [[Tuskiev River|Tuskiev]] and [[Kroviv River|Kroviv]] rivers along its coast. Several major coastal cities front it including [[Lapentsk]], [[Valivia]], [[Porve Tviv]], [[Porve Yatasha]], and [[Sana Chovev]].
