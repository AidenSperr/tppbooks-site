# Bay of Amekia

> **Type:** Bay
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]], [[Amekia Sector]], [[Baodia]]

## Overview
A large bay from the [[West Takromavian Ocean]] cutting into the western coast of [[Othaala]], touching the [[Lovante Sector]], [[Amekia Sector]], and [[Baodia]]. The northern shore of [[Chaalne]] and [[Phaena]] face this bay, and [[Silvakca]] sits on the isthmus between it and the [[Baodian Sea]].
