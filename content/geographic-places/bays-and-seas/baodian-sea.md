# Baodian Sea

> **Type:** Sea
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]] / [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Sonueng]], [[Vinzhin]], [[Amekia Sector]], [[Baodia]]

## Overview
A large sea extending from the [[West Takromavian Ocean]], bounded by [[Baodia]] and the [[Amekia Sector]] of [[Othaala]] to the south and [[Huozhi]] to the north and east. Forms a significant stretch of the border between the two nations.
