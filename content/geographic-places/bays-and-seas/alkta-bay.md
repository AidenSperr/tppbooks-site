# Alkta Bay

> **Type:** Bay
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]] / [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Sonueng]] / [[Rgrenzde]]

## Overview
A small bay where the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] meets the northern coast, shared between [[Sonueng]] in [[Huozhi]] and [[Rgrenzde]] in [[Qauta]]. Also fed by the [[Alkta River]].

## Connected Locations
- [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[Alkta River]], [[Sonueng]], [[Rgrenzde]]
