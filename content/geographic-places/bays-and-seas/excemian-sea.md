# Excemian Sea

> **Type:** Sea
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]], [[Kalavam]]

## Overview
A large sea from the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] bordering the [[Excemia]] and [[Kalavam]] regions of [[Herat]]. The [[Ateman River]] flows into it near [[Duvaelar]], and [[Tavira]] sits against it within [[Kalavam]].
