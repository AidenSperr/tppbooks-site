# North Kalvenavian Ocean

> **Type:** Ocean
> **Nation:** N/A
> **Region:** North of [[Alatrya]]

## Overview
The ocean north of [[Alatrya]], bordering [[Qauta]], [[Herat]], and the northern edges of [[Frithre]]. Several seas and bays extend from it into the continent, including the [[Excemian Sea]], [[Bay of Zhouxe]], and [[Alkta Bay]].
