# South Kalvenavian Ocean

> **Type:** Ocean
> **Nation:** N/A
> **Region:** South of [[Alatrya]]

## Overview
The ocean region south of [[Alatrya]], bordering the southern coasts of [[Othaala]], [[Almania]], and [[Birumiaka]]. Several seas and bays open from it, including the [[Sea of Samaiku]], [[Sapala Sea]], [[Kulavren Bay]], [[Bay of Buhrrev]], and [[Nolaykian Bay]].
