# West Takromavian Ocean

> **Type:** Ocean
> **Nation:** N/A
> **Region:** West of [[Alatrya]]

## Overview
The major ocean to the west of [[Alatrya]], bordering [[Othaala]], [[Frithre]], and [[Huozhi]]. Several large bodies of water extend from it into the continent, including the [[Baodian Sea]], [[Bay of Amekia]], [[Sea of Hornaskia]], and others.
