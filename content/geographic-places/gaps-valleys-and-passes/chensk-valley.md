# Chensk Valley

> **Type:** Valley
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Lomensk Oblast]]

## Overview
A valley gap between the southern [[Orbev Mountains]] and the mountains north of the [[Slobeska River]] in [[Frithre]]. The midpoint of the valley marks the border between [[Robosse Oblast]] and [[Lomensk Oblast]].
