# Besa Gap

> **Type:** Mountain Gap
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]

## Overview
A large gap in the [[Bhahare Mountains]] in the [[Lovante Sector]], frequently used for travel between the western coast and the interior plateau cities, particularly between [[Nahavre]] and the capital region. The main road through the Besa Gap leads toward [[Lumen]] and is a heavily trafficked route.

## Connected Locations
- [[Bhahare Mountains]], [[Lovante Sector]], [[Nahavre]], [[Lumen]], [[Knaema]]

## Notes
- One of the primary overland routes between the coastal [[Lovante Sector]] and the [[Yavathre Plateau]]
