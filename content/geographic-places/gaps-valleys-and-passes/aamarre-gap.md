# Aamarre Gap

> **Type:** Mountain Gap
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]

## Overview
A wide, exposed gap between the [[Sclove Mountains]] and [[Flokcek Mountains]] in the [[Amekia Sector]], used for centuries as a transit route for armies moving south from the north. A flattened valley between the two ridgelines swept by a constant, sharp wind. The Birumiakese army camps here overnight before reaching the [[Voemna Pass]] on the 29th of Uyathirnd.

## Connected Locations
- [[Sclove Mountains]], [[Flokcek Mountains]], [[Voemna Pass]], [[Amekia Sector]]

- Wind howls constantly; fires must be shielded behind stone to survive the gusts
