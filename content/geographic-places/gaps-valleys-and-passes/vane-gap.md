# Vane Gap

> **Type:** Mountain Gap
> **Nation:** [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Polimeva Prefecture]], [[Tahevina Prefecture]]

## Overview
A gap in the northeastern [[Barumisu Mountains]] serving as the primary pass between [[Polimeva Prefecture]] and [[Tahevina Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]].
