# Lake Spola

> **Type:** Lake
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]]

## Overview
A large lake in [[Sa'amas]], the central region of [[A'Amaer]], on and around which the capital city of [[Spola]] is built. The lake feeds three rivers: the [[Assimisian River]], [[Dossaes River]], and [[Skosva River]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Spola]], [[Sa'amas]], [[Assimisian River]], [[Dossaes River]], [[Skosva River]]

- [[Spola]] is built directly on and around its shores and waters
