# Gaelime Lake

> **Type:** Lake
> **Nation:** [[Herat]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Centrem]], [[Minalsche Oblast]]

## Overview
A large lake feeding the [[Solavev River]], situated on the border between [[Centrem]] in [[Herat]] and [[Minalsche Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. The village of [[Gaelime]] sits on its southern shore.

## Connected Locations
- [[Solavev River]], [[Centrem]], [[Minalsche Oblast]], [[Gaelime]]
