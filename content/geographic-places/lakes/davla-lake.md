# Davla Lake

> **Type:** Lake
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]] / [[Lovante Sector]]

## Overview
A fairly large lake on the [[Yavathre Plateau]], against which the town of [[Duvaeri]] sits at the western edge of the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Its far edge is veiled in morning haze where the [[Shobrae River]] and lake meet. Shares a small stretch of the border between the [[Yavathre Sector]] and [[Lovante Sector]]. Notable as the arrival point where [[Queen Namerethe]] meets [[High Leader Gamerime]]'s fleet in Book II.

## Connected Locations
- [[Duvaeri]], [[Shobrae River]], [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Lovante Sector]]

- Described as glittering like a farewell as the Birumiakese army marches north from its shores
