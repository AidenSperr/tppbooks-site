# Lake of the Twins

> **Type:** Lake
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A holy and protected lake situated outside [[Lumen]] on the [[Dothesta Plains]] in the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Named for [[The Twins]], [[Ulmanae]] and [[Arveshia]], who are the patron deities of [[Othaala]]. Its sacred status means it is preserved from settlement or industrial use.

## Connected Locations
- [[Lumen]], [[Dothesta Plains]], [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Ulmanae]], [[Arveshia]]

## Notes
- Protected from any settlement or development owing to its religious significance
