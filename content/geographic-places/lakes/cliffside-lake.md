# Cliffside Lake

> **Type:** Lake
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]] / [[Kulavren Sector]]

## Overview
A lake against the cliffs of the southern [[Yavathre Plateau]], on the border between the [[Yavathre Sector]] and [[Kulavren Sector]]. The city of [[Plamauthe]] sits along the [[Plamauthe River]] that connects to it.
