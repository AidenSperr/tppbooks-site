# Tuithe Islands

> **Type:** Island Chain
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Mavisktev Oblast]]

## Overview
A small island chain off the tip of the [[Endall Peninsula]] in the [[Sea of Hornaskia]], connected to [[Tuithe'la'nat]] by a series of wood and rope bridges spanning the islands' spines. Home to the Grand Shrine of [[Seville]] on the outermost island, which is the destination of a traditional Tuithe pilgrimage rite of passage. The islands are four in number; pilgrims spend one day on each during the crossing. On the back cliffs of the shrine island, hidden between two stone gullies, is a small unmarked cave where [[Tahlii A'Trentao]] received [[Seville]]'s Gem directly from the goddess.

## Connected Locations
- [[Tuithe'la'nat]], [[Endall Peninsula]], [[Sea of Hornaskia]], [[Seville]]

## Notes
- The Grand Shrine of [[Seville]] sits on the outermost island
- The cave where Tahlii received the Gem is on the back cliffs of the shrine island, steep and rocky, hidden between two stone gullies
- The pilgrimage walk spans all four islands via wood and rope bridges — a rite of passage for the [[Tuithes]]
