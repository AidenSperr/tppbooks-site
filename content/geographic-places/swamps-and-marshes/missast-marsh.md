# Missast Marsh

> **Type:** Marsh
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]], [[E'elsavest]], [[Obemiva Prefecture]], [[Namik Prefecture]]

## Overview
A large marshland mostly in [[Sa'amas]] in [[A'Amaer]], with smaller portions in [[E'elsavest]], [[Obemiva Prefecture]], and [[Namik Prefecture]] in [[Birumiaka]]. The large city of [[Waveshe]] is situated within the marsh in [[E'elsavest]].
