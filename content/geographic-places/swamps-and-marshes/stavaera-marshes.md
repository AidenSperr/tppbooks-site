# Stavaera Marshes

> **Type:** Marshes
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Kulavren Sector]]

## Overview
A large marshland in the southern [[Kulavren Sector]] along the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]] and [[Kulavren Bay]]. The cities of [[Elataha]] and [[Halhhetha]] are situated within it. [[Elataha]] houses [[Othaala]]'s largest military port base.
