# Gohna Swamps

> **Type:** Swamp
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]

## Overview
A large swampy area in the northern [[Warethoan Sector]], through which the large city of [[Waretho]] is built. Fed by [[Lake Gohna]] and the [[Alavi River]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Warethoan Sector]], [[Waretho]], [[Lake Gohna]], [[Alavi River]]

## Notes
- [[Waretho]], one of the largest cities in [[Othaala]], is built directly within this swamp
