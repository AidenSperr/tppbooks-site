# Ateman River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]], [[Kalavam]], [[Centrem]]

## Overview
A river snaking through the [[Thaetrachese Mountains]], fed by [[Gaelime Lake]] and emptying into the [[Excemian Sea]] near [[Duvaelar]]. Contains the [[Great Ateman Waterfall]], from which it descends dramatically near [[Atema]].

- [[Gaelime Lake]], [[Excemian Sea]], [[Thaetrachese Mountains]], [[Great Ateman Waterfall]], [[Atema]], [[Duvaelar]]
