# Kroviv River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Godascher Oblast]]

## Overview
A medium-width river in [[Godascher Oblast]], fed by the [[Maviv Sea]] and emptying into the [[Sea of Hornaskia]]. Joins the [[Tuskiev River]] at the confluence on which [[Godascher]] is built.
