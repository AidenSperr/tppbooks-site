# Skosva River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]], [[E'elsavest]], [[Obemiva Prefecture]], [[Namik Prefecture]]

## Overview
A river fed by [[Lake Spola]] running on the border of [[Sa'amas]] and [[E'elsavest]], then continuing on the border of [[Obemiva Prefecture]] and [[Namik Prefecture]].
