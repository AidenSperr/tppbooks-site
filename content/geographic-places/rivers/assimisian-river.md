# Assimisian River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Salsesa]], [[Sa'amas]], [[Shikena Prefecture]]

## Overview
A river fed by [[Lake Spola]] emptying into the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]], mainly in [[Salsesa]] with small stretches bordering [[Sa'amas]] and [[Shikena Prefecture]]. The village of [[Whilssas]] sits along it.
