# Farilvana River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Almania]] / [[Frithre]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Koleva]], [[Heviava]], [[Katav]], [[Columil Oblast]], [[Zovamnev Oblast]], [[Soverteva Oblast]], [[Doutoula Prefecture]]

## Overview
A long river running through the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] and forming key stretches of the borders between [[Almania]], [[Frithre]], and [[Birumiaka]]. Bordering regions include [[Columil Oblast]], [[Zovamnev Oblast]], [[Soverteva Oblast]], [[Doutoula Prefecture]], [[Koleva]], [[Katav]], and [[Heviava]]. [[Ronaela Astarise]] crossed this river in 392 c.e. on her solo expedition into what would become [[Almania]].

## Connected Locations
