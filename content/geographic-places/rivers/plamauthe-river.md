# Plamauthe River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Kulavren Sector]]

## Overview
A long river from the [[Cliffside Lake]] feeding into the [[Sovithiena River]], marking much of the border between the [[Yavathre Sector]] and the [[Kulavren Sector]]. The [[Gollae River]] is a small offshoot. The large city of [[Plamauthe]] sits at the river's mouth.
