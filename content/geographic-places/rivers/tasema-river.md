# Tasema River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]] / [[Herat]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Drronnelina]], [[Bovalna]], [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Minalsche Oblast]]

## Overview
A river marking the eastern border of [[Drronnelina]] in [[Qauta]], the western border of [[Bovalna]] in [[Herat]], and the northeastern borders of both [[Lomensk Oblast]] and [[Minalsche Oblast]] in [[Frithre]]. Named for the ancient Faeric nation of [[Tasema]].
