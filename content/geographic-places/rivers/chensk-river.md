# Chensk River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Jofiena]]

## Overview
A small connective river marking the southern portion of the border between [[Robosse Oblast]] and [[Jofiena]], and the northern portion of the border between [[Lomensk Oblast]] and [[Jofiena]].
