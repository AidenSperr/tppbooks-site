# Hot Streams of Olbaerd

> **Type:** Hot Streams
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]], [[Lomensk Oblast]]

## Overview
A chain of naturally hot streams running through the [[Orbev Mountains]], straddling the border between [[Othaala]]'s [[Amekia Sector]] and [[Frithre]]'s [[Lomensk Oblast]]. The streams feed into [[Orbev Lake]].
