# Dossaes River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]] / [[Birumiaka]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]], [[Obemiva Prefecture]], [[Shikena Prefecture]]

## Overview
A river fed by [[Lake Spola]] emptying into the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]], mainly in [[Sa'amas]] with a small area bordering [[Obemiva Prefecture]] and [[Shikena Prefecture]].
