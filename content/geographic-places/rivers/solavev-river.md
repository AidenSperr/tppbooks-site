# Solavev River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Minalsche Oblast]], [[Mavisktev Oblast]], [[Godascher Oblast]], [[Bovalna]], [[Centrem]], [[Divinava]], [[Novernia]], [[Colvescum]]

## Overview
A long river forming a significant stretch of the border between [[Frithre]] and [[Herat]], marking the northern border of [[Minalsche Oblast]], the western borders of [[Mavisktev Oblast]] and [[Godascher Oblast]], and the southern and eastern borders of multiple [[Herat]] regions including [[Bovalna]], [[Centrem]], [[Divinava]], [[Novernia]], and [[Colvescum]]. Fed by [[Gaelime Lake]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Gaelime Lake]], [[Minalsche Oblast]], [[Mavisktev Oblast]], [[Godascher Oblast]], [[Bovalna]], [[Centrem]], [[Divinava]], [[Novernia]], [[Colvescum]]
