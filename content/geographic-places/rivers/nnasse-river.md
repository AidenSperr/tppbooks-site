# Nnasse River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Ttuventk]], [[Fnolkischta]]

## Overview
A small river system in [[Qauta]] marking the southeastern border between [[Ttuventk]] and [[Fnolkischta]], fed by [[Tevva Lake]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Ttuventk]], [[Fnolkischta]], [[Tevva Lake]]

## Notes
- Named for the ancient Faeric nation of [[Nnasse]]
