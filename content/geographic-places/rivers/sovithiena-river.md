# Sovithiena River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Warethoan Sector]], [[Kulavren Sector]]

## Overview
A long river leading to the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]], marking the borders of the [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Warethoan Sector]], and [[Kulavren Sector]]. The [[Bosiahha River]] is a small northern offshoot. [[Yawebesa]] sits along it in the [[Warethoan Sector]].
