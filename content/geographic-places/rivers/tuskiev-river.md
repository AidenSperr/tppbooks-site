# Tuskiev River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Godascher Oblast]]

## Overview
A wide river in [[Godascher Oblast]], fed by the [[Maviv Sea]] and emptying into the [[Sea of Hornaskia]]. One of the two rivers at whose confluence the capital [[Godascher]] is built, alongside the [[Kroviv River]].
