# Xiaen River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Vinzhin]], [[Sonueng]]

## Overview
A major river splitting much of [[Vinzhin]] and [[Sonueng]] in [[Huozhi]], fed by [[Lake Zien]] and emptying into the [[Bay of Zhouxe]]. The cities of [[Xeingang]] and [[Dongfeng]] sit along it.

## Connected Locations
- [[Lake Zien]], [[Bay of Zhouxe]], [[Vinzhin]], [[Sonueng]], [[Xeingang]], [[Dongfeng]]
