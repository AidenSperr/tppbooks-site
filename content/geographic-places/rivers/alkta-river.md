# Alkta River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Qauta]] / [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Ttuventk]], [[Rgrenzde]], [[Jofiena]], [[Sonueng]]

## Overview
A river running through the [[Shoneng Mountains]] and marking the western border between northern [[Robosse Oblast]] and [[Ttuventk]] and [[Rgrenzde]] in [[Qauta]], and the eastern border of [[Jofiena]] and [[Sonueng]] in [[Huozhi]]. Empties into the [[Alkta Bay]]. Named for the ancient Faeric nation of [[Alkta]].
