# Shobrae River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]], [[Lovante Sector]], [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A long river ending at the [[West Takromavian Ocean]], fed by [[Lake Gohna]], the [[Alavi River]], and [[Davla Lake]]. Its western portion marks the border of the [[Warethoan Sector]] and the [[Lovante Sector]]; its northern portion marks part of the border between the [[Yavathre Sector]] and the [[Lovante Sector]]. The [[Shobrae Hills]] are named for it.
