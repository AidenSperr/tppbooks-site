# Tovlekan River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Almania]]
> **Region:** [[Alve]], [[Viva]]

## Overview
A major river in [[Almania]] running through [[Alve]] and [[Viva]], emptying into the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]]. The capital city of [[Caimania]] and the large city of [[Lavitia]] both sit along it.

## Connected Locations
- [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[Alve]], [[Viva]], [[Caimania]], [[Lavitia]]
