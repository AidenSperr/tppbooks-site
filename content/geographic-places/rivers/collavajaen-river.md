# Collavajaen River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]], [[Kulavren Sector]], [[Columil Oblast]]

## Overview
A long river marking a large portion of the southern border between [[Othaala]] and [[Frithre]]. Originates from [[Lake Sovev]] and the [[Traoniel Cliffs]], runs through the [[Yavathre Sector]] and [[Kulavren Sector]], and feeds into the [[Sea of Novobriskia]]. The stretch near [[Lake Sovev]] is sometimes called the River Sovev. The [[Dobahv River]] is a small offshoot in the [[Yavathre Sector]].
