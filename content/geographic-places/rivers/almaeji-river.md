# Almaeji River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]] / [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]], [[Jofiena]]

## Overview
A river marking the border between northern [[Othaala]] and southern [[Huozhi]], running through the [[Amekia Sector]] near the city of [[Almaej]], from which it takes its name. The Almaeji River corridor is referenced by Raomi as the key strategic frontier in Book II — holding or losing the river crossings determines the pace of the Huozhese advance.

## Connected Locations
- [[Amekia Sector]], [[Jofiena]], [[Almaej]], [[Baodian Sea]]

## Notes
- Raomi describes the corridor as a line that, once broken, opens the road to [[Tuusalche]] and beyond
