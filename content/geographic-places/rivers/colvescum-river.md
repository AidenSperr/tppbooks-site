# Colvescum River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Herat]] / [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Colvescum]], [[Mavisktev Oblast]]

## Overview
A river marking the very northernmost border between [[Colvescum]] in [[Herat]] and [[Mavisktev Oblast]] in [[Frithre]], fed from [[Lake Dobesk]] and emptying into the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]].

## Connected Locations
- [[Lake Dobesk]], [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]], [[Colvescum]], [[Mavisktev Oblast]]
