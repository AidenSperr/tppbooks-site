# Slobeska River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Frithre]] / [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]], [[Lomensk Oblast]], [[Ttuventk]], [[Fnolkischta]], [[Dascenvl]], [[Drronnelina]]

## Overview
An extremely long river with many tributary systems, marking a large portion of the border between [[Frithre]] and [[Qauta]]. Borders [[Robosse Oblast]] against [[Ttuventk]], [[Fnolkischta]], and [[Dascenvl]], and marks the northern border of [[Lomensk Oblast]] against [[Dascenvl]], [[Fnolkischta]], and [[Drronnelina]].
