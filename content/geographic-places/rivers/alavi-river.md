# Alavi River

> **Type:** River
> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]], [[Yavathre Sector]]

## Overview
A small river feeding out of the [[Shobrae River]], on which the town of [[Sivrrakta]] sits. Runs along the northeastern edge of the [[Warethoan Sector]] and the southwestern edge of the [[Yavathre Sector]]. The [[Alavi Woods]] are named for it.
