# Gabo

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Shikenans]]

## Overview
The Fifth Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Shikenans]]. Resembled a leopard.

## Descendant Traits (Shikenans)
Small half-oval ears on top of the head. Hair with a leopard pattern — an orange-brown or yellowish base with black details. Light brown eyes. A long, thin tail carrying the same leopard patterning, attached at the tailbone.

## Notes
- Shikenans have a strong tradition of full-body tattoo art, with intricate designs covering much of the body
- The Shikenans reside in the Shikena Prefecture of [[Birumiaka]]
