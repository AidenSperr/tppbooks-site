# Shrova

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Gilimeans]]

## Overview
The First Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Gilimeans]]. A large fox-like creature with four wings upon its back, eight tails, and otherwise vulpine features. In sacred art, Shrova's body is long and graceful with a narrow face and a mane of raised fur running from the brow to the shoulders. One pair of wings is broad and feathered like a great bird's; the other is smaller, slightly lower, and often depicted folded.

## Descendant Traits (Gilimeans)
Two tails attached at the tailbone that split into two parts at the ends, commonly long with a tight pointed curl. Two fox-like ears atop the head. Hair is normally orange or light gray; eyes are usually dark brown or orange.

## Notes
- Also called Skrrove in Othaani
- Most often seen in carved stone or painted sacred art across Birumiaka and Othaala
