# Five Wyverns

> **Also Known As:** Five Great Dragons of the Wyvernal Era

## Overview
The five primordial dragons of the Wyvernal Era, two eras before the Bygone Era. The first element-controlling entities in [[Alatrya]], they resided in what is now the southern continent of [[Almania]]. Each controlled one of the five Old Elements. Their deaths gave rise to the five Dragonkinnian ethnicities, each descended from a different dragon's bloodline.

## Members
- [[Tyv]] — Old Dragon of Earth; bloodline originator of the [[Tyvnians]]
- [[Valevyr]] — Old Dragon of Flame; bloodline originator of the [[Valevyrians]]
- [[Kalvev]] — Old Dragon of Wind; bloodline originator of the [[Kalvevnians]]
- [[Thurva]] — Old Dragon of Water; bloodline originator of the [[Thurvanians]]
- [[Shvafni]] — Old Dragon of Electricity; bloodline originator of the [[Shvafnians]]

## Notes
- Referenced in the prophecy given by a Herati priest: *"Just as the Old Dragons and Faunan Kings' lives hath come and gone... from their corpses breathed new life"*
- The wings of Tyv and Valevyr are used as architectural motifs in the design of Othaala's palace
