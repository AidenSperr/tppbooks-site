# Tyv

> **Element:** [[Earth]]
> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Tyvnians]]

## Overview
The Old Dragon of Earth and one of the [[Five Wyverns]] of the Wyvernal Era. Bloodline originator of the [[Tyvnians]], the most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity.

## Appearance
Known from cultural reference. The wings of Tyv (alongside [[Valevyr]]) are used as an architectural motif in Othaala's royal palace.

## Descendant Traits (Tyvnians)
Green skin on the darker end of the spectrum, many pointed scales, a thin and long tail with a small barb on the end, and yellow-orange or orange eyes.

## Notes
- Pronunciation: TIHV
- Used as a casual expression of strength: *"I feel like I wrestled Tyv in my sleep"*
