# Six Faunan Kings

## Overview
The six mythical bloodline originators of the [[Beastfolk]] peoples of [[Birumiaka]]. Each was a great beast of mammalian descent, and each gave rise to one of the six Beastfolk ethnicities. Their legacy is celebrated across Birumiaka through a shared festival called [[Zamaleganaredoshinate]], or 'The Day of Ancestors,' featuring dancing, music, drumming, and wind instruments.

## Members
- [[Shrova]] — First Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Gilimeans]]
- [[Wotavi]] — Second Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Tahevinans]]
- [[Nuveriami]] — Third Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Namiks]]
- [[Junlundi]] — Fourth Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Obemevians]]
- [[Gabo]] — Fifth Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Shikenans]]
- [[Tagelndi]] — Sixth Faunan King; bloodline originator of the [[Satsawans]]

## Notes
- Referenced alongside the [[Five Wyverns]] in the prophecy given by a Herati priest: *"Just as the Old Dragons and Faunan Kings' lives hath come and gone... from their corpses breathed new life"*
- The Grand Olbaerd Tree in [[Birumiaka]] is said to be the origin point of all peoples, including the Faunan Kings
