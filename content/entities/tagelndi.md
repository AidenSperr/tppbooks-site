# Tagelndi

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Satsawans]]

## Overview
The Sixth Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Satsawans]]. Resembled a large rhinoceros.

## Descendant Traits (Satsawans)
Gray and somewhat rough skin. A long, thick horn growing from the forehead. Near-ovular ears on the top-side of the head. A small tail with a bushel of prickly black hair on its end. Black hair and dark brown eyes.

## Notes
- Satsawans are known for showing more skin than most other cultures, with both men and women commonly exposing the midriff
- Reside in the Satsawa and Otava Prefectures of [[Birumiaka]]
