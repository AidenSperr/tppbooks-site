# Junlundi

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Obemevians]]

## Overview
The Fourth Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Obemevians]]. An antelope whose horns are said to have grown to a meter in length.

## Descendant Traits (Obemevians)
Winding, multi-pronged horns. Ears similar to a deer's, positioned underneath the horns and more downturned. Eyes that are extremely dark brown. A straight tail with little fur and a common width of around one and a half inches.

## Notes
- The Obemevians reside in the Obemiva Prefecture of [[Birumiaka]]
