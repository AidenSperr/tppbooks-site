# Wotavi

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Tahevinans]]

## Overview
The Second Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Tahevinans]]. A large coyote with four ears.

## Descendant Traits (Tahevinans)
Four ears — two large and two small — positioned on top of the head. Sharp teeth lining the entirety of the mouth. A short, fluffed tail.

## Notes
- [[Mataki Naleva]] is Tahevinan Gemwielder encountered in Kaedoa is described as having four ears total and thick hair on them, reflective of Wotavi's heritage
