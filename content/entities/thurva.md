# Thurva

> **Element:** [[Water]]
> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Thurvanians]]

## Overview
The Old Dragon of Water and one of the [[Five Wyverns]] of the Wyvernal Era. Bloodline originator of the [[Thurvanians]], the fourth-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity.

## Descendant Traits (Thurvanians)
Light blue to light-turquoise skin, a tailfin on top of the tail, thin scales mostly concentrated on the tail and sides of the face, and a unique feature not shared by other ethnicities: small fin-like webs on the sides of the head in front of the ear hole.

## Notes
- Pronunciation: THUHR-vuh
