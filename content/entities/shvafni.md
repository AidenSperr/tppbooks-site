The Old Dragon of Electricity and one of the [[Five Wyverns]] of the Wyvernal Era. Bloodline originator of the [[Shvafnians]], the least populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity. Shvafnians are noted as craftspeople: blacksmiths, carpenters, and artisans, mainly

Light brown or reddish skin, long wavy horns that come to a sharp point (one of two horned Dragonkinnian ethnicities), yellow-orange or bright yellow eyes, a short and curled tail, and sparse rounded scales.
