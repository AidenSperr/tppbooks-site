# Kalvev

> **Element:** [[Wind]]
> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Kalvevnians]]

## Overview
The Old Dragon of Wind and one of the [[Five Wyverns]] of the Wyvernal Era. Bloodline originator of the [[Kalvevnians]], the third-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity.

## Descendant Traits (Kalvevnians)
Dark-brown skin with very little scales, short and rounded horns (one of only two horned Dragonkinnian ethnicities), a thin and short tail, and light-blue or light-gray eyes.

## Notes
- Pronunciation: KAHL-vehv
