# Valevyr

> **Element:** [[Fire]]
> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Valevyrians]]

## Overview
The Old Dragon of Flame and one of the [[Five Wyverns]] of the Wyvernal Era. Bloodline originator of the [[Valevyrians]], the second-most populous Dragonkinnian ethnicity.

## Descendant Traits (Valevyrians)
Desaturated red skin, a thick and medium-length tail, sharp triangular scales, and yellow-orange or reddish eyes.

## Notes
- Pronunciation: VAL-leh-vihr
- The wings of Valevyr and [[Tyv]] are used as an architectural motif in Othaala's royal palace
