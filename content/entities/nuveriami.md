# Nuveriami

> **Bloodline/Descendant Race:** [[Namiks]]

## Overview
The Third Faunan King and bloodline originator of the [[Namiks]]. A buck with long and complex antlers.

## Descendant Traits (Namiks)
Antlers growing from the sides of the head; a small tail around four inches long; and two ears placed just in front of the antlers, angled downward.

## Notes
- The Namiks are spread across the Polimeva and Namik Prefectures of [[Birumiaka]]
