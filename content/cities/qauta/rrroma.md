# Rrroma

> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Obbestra]]
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital of [[Qauta]] and one of the largest cities on the continent, spread like a fan across a wide plateau in [[Obbestra]]. Built of stacked stone and green-tiled roofs, with tall tiered plazas and shimmering canals running through its districts. At the city's center stands the King's Palace: seven clustered towers with faceted spires, pale blue tiled walls, and wide glass terraces trimmed in dark polished wood. The palace is older than most of the surrounding architecture. The city's streets are extremely dense and busy, having swelled further with refugees displaced by the wars near [[Huozhi]] and [[Frithre]].
