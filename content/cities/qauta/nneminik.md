# Nneminik

> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Drronnelina]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A large village in [[Drronnelina]], notable in Book II as the site of a trap laid against the Gemwielders by the corrupted [[Polaenian Church]] official [[Qirai Alvetavjh]]. Qirai holds the title of Marchionessa and files a false report of assault and political disruption against the group, which follows them to [[Rrroma]] and complicates their arrival at the King's Palace.

## Notable Residents
- [[Qirai Alvetavjh]] — Marchionessa; Church official secretly aligned with hostile forces; responsible for the ambush and subsequent false report
