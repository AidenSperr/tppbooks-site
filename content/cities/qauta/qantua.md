# Qantua

> **Nation:** [[Qauta]]
> **Region:** [[Rgrenzde]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
The only city in [[Rgrenzde]] and one of the northern cities of [[Qauta]], situated on the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]] near the [[Great Ice Crystal Forest]]. One of the Asyulani team cities. The Gemwielders pass through Qantua in Book II on their route east through [[Qauta]].
