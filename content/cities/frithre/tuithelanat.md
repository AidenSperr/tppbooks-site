# Tuithe'la'nat

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Mavisktev Oblast]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A small tribal village on the very tip of the [[Endall Peninsula]], with coastline on both the [[Sea of Hornaskia]] and the [[North Kalvenavian Ocean]]. Home of the [[Tuithes]], one of the five Human ethnicities of [[Frithre]].
