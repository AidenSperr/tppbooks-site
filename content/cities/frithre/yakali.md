# Ya'kali

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Zovamnev Oblast]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A large tribal village on the [[Farilvana River]] in the [[Ossolovav Mountains]] in [[Zovamnev Oblast]]. Home mainly to the [[Ya'kalians]], one of the five Human ethnicities of [[Frithre]], characterized by light skin, blue eyes, and brown or light brown hair.
