# Godascher

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Godascher Oblast]]
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital of [[Frithre]], a large city situated at the confluence of the [[Tuskiev River]] and [[Kroviv River]] along the [[Sea of Hornaskia]], extending into the edge of the [[Maviv Forest]]. Home to the [[Grand Central Church of Godascher]], the largest [[Polaenian Church]] on the continent, whose alabaster facade, wide red-orange dome, and central hand-shaped spire dominate the city's skyline. The palace of the Frithren monarch sits on the coast at the sea's edge.
