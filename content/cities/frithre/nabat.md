# Nabat

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Minalsche Oblast]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A small tribal town in the north of [[Minalsche Oblast]], home to the [[Nabati]] ethnicity. One of the oldest continuous Human settlements in [[Frithre]], predating the consolidation of [[Nabatia]] into the unified nation.
