# Robosse

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Robosse Oblast]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city in northern [[Robosse Oblast]] and the only city in the region. One of the early targets of the Huozhese military campaign in Book II, referenced by Raomi as part of the advancing Demonic forces' initial push from [[Ttuventk]] through the northern rivers.
