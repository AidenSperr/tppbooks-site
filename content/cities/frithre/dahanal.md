> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]

## Overview
A small village in southern [[Zovamnev Oblast]], historically one of the settlements liberated from Almanian Empire control in the third year of the Bygone Era, commemorated in the Frithren national holiday on the 18th of Inyutevr.
