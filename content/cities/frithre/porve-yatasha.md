# Porve Yatasha

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Kruntan Oblast]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city on the [[Sea of Hornaskia]] in the [[Yatasha Plains]] of [[Kruntan Oblast]]. Seat of House Szakuvya, one of the city-leading noble houses of [[Frithre]]. Home city of [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]].

## Notable Residents
- [[Tyraskir Szakuvya]] — a Baron of Porve Yatasha; head of House Szakuvya
- [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]] — son of Tyraskir; a Prince of Porve Yatasha; former resident; Water Gemwielder

## Notable Structures
- Szakuvya Estate — manor house outside the city proper, with grounds including wooded land

## Geographic Features
[[Sea of Hornaskia]], [[Yatasha Plains]]

## Notes
- Dhathrir describes himself to Huolong as "son of Tyraskir Szakuvya, Prince of Porve Yatasha"
- House Szakuvya leads the city's governance
