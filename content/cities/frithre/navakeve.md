# Navakeve

> **Nation:** [[Frithre]]
> **Region:** [[Lomensk Oblast]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A fairly large city in [[Lomensk Oblast]], formerly the capital of the [[Kingdom of Navakeveia]]. Home to the majority of the [[Navaki]] ethnicity. By the time of Book II, Navakeve is reported as nearly destroyed in the Huozhese advance — the Gemwielders pass through [[Lovyov Forest]] nearby and are told most of the Navaki have perished.
