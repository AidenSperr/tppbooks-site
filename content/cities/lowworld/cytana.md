# Cytana

> **Nation:** [[Prylkya]] (the Lowworld)
> **Region:** Unknown
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital city of the [[Prylkya|Lowworld]], where [[The Low]] is said to reside. Ruled by [[Vyrmyr]], the Third Son of [[The Low]] and the weakest of his three sons. Its existence is largely unknown to the surface world and is considered rumor or myth by most mortals.

## Notable Residents
- [[The Low]] — said to reside here
- [[Vyrmyr]] — Third Son of [[The Low]]; Ruler of Cytana
