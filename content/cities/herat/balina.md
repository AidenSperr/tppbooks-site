# Balina

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Excemia]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large and ancient city on the [[Bay of Balina]] in [[Excemia]], one of the oldest settlements in [[Herat]]. Home to the [[Balinians]], an Angelic ethnicity with feathered wings, plus-shaped pupils, and characteristically white hair.
