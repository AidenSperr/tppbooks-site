# Tavira

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Kalavam]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city on the [[Excemian Sea]] in [[Kalavam]], set against the [[Villeva Mountains]]. Home city of [[Lycheseia Athalnus]], the Light Gemwielder, who maintained a personal workshop outside the city that also served as her residence.

## Notable Residents
- [[Lycheseia Athalnus]] — Light Gemwielder; born and raised in Tavira; her workshop is just outside the city
