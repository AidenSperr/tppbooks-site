# Covana

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Bovalna]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city in southern [[Bovalna]] with a notable [[Polaenian Church]]. The Gemwielders visit the church here in Book I, where an elderly Angelic priest remarks that many followers stop reading the [[Book of Nasariane]] before reaching its later books.
