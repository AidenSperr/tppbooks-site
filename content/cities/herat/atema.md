# Atema

> **Nation:** [[Herat]]
> **Region:** [[Centrem]]
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital of [[Herat]], situated in [[Centrem]] near the [[Great Ateman Waterfall]]. Home of the Angelic Council and once the seat of [[President Stacit Chatanus]]. [[Herat]]'s first comprehensive history, *[[Delmervas]]*, was publicly read in Atema Central Hall and distributed across the nation from here. Atema is notably the most academically and culturally mixed city in [[Herat]] given the nation's open-borders policy.

## Notable Residents
- [[President Stacit Chatanus]] — A President of [[Herat]]
- [[Herian of Atema]] — historian; author of *[[Delmervas]]*

## Notable Structures
