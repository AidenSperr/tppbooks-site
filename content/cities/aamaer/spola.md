# Spola

> **Nation:** [[A'Amaer]]
> **Region:** [[Sa'amas]]
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital of [[A'Amaer]], situated on and around [[Lake Spola]] in the central [[Sa'amas]] region. As the seat of the nation ordained to house those undergoing [[Redemption]], Spola functions as both a civic and a deeply theological center.
