# Taebahe

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** City

## Overview
A city west of [[Lumen]] on the [[Yavathre Plateau]], modest in size. It is a flat-spread city of many flowing courtyards and slate-colored roofs, laid out in circular and curved neighborhoods linked by efficient arterial roads. The main government building sits just off the center square. Positioned on the main highway between [[Lumen]] and the western plateau cities, and one of the four cities referenced in the main road network alongside [[Lumen]], [[Suvrra]], and [[Kaedoa]].
