# Nolaykwe

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city on the [[Nolaykian Bay]] in the south of the [[Warethoan Sector]]. Serves as a key southern port and sea evacuation point during Book II, with [[Mallia Yuhava]] and [[Dakkai Eletha]] departing from here by ship.
