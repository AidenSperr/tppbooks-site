# Yawebesa

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]
> **Type:** Town

## Overview
A small town in the [[Merrana Grasslands]] along the [[Sovithiena River]] in the [[Warethoan Sector]]. A waypoint on the southern evacuation route in Book II. [[Mallia Yuhava]] and [[Dakkai Eletha]] pass through here before continuing to [[Nolaykwe]] to board a ship.
