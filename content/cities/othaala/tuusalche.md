# Tuusalche

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A medium-sized village north of the [[Chokke Grasslands]] in the central-east [[Amekia Sector]]. A small, organized settlement with a central market square, a large grain storage building, and a fountain. The mayor at the time of Book II is [[Eshro Saone]], a broad and steady man who responds to Raomi's warning without panic and orders immediate evacuation.

## Notable Residents
- [[Eshro Saone]] — mayor; directs the evacuation in Book II

## Notable Structures
- Central market square with fountain and grain storage
- Local inn/accommodations
