> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]

## Overview
A large city on the [[South Kalvenavian Ocean]] and [[Kulavren Bay]] in the [[Kulavren Sector]], within the [[Stavaera Marshes]]. Home to the largest military port base in [[Othaala]], larger in scale than the station at [[Akcolvathe]], though the latter is considered a full military station.
