# Duvaeri

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Town

## Overview
A small town at the western edge of the [[Yavathre Sector]] on [[Davla Lake]] where the [[Shobrae River]] meets the lake. Its walls are low stone and wood, mostly to deter wildlife rather than fortify. Buildings are a mix of central and western Othaalan styles; fully wooden roofs from starlia timber alongside stone-and-wood housing with taller roofs and dark stone chimneys. Othaalan flags fly from nearly every rooftop. Notable as the arrival point for the Birumiakese fleet in Book II, where [[Queen Namerethe]] personally meets [[High Leader Gamerime]] at the docks.

## Notable Residents
- [[Raomi Allamaera]] — was born in Duvaeri before leaving for [[Akcolvathe]]

## Notable Structures
- Docks — dark stone platforms with rope-tethered moorings and narrow watchtowers at either end

## Geographic Features
