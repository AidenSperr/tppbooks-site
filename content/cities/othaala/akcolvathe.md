# Akcolvathe

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A somewhat large city south of [[Lumen]] against the [[Pfapohrrv Forest]] on the [[Dothesta Plains]], in the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Home to the largest or second-largest military station in [[Othaala]] — the largest being the port base at [[Elataha]]. The city where [[Raomi Allamaera]] joined the military after leaving his family in Duvaeri.

## Notable Residents
- [[Raomi Allamaera]] — joined the military here; his career began at the station

## Notable Structures
- Military station — one of the largest in [[Othaala]]
