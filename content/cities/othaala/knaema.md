# Knaema

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]
> **Type:** Major City

## Overview
An extremely large city occupying most of the [[Lovante Peninsula]], built against the [[Knaeman Bay]]. One of [[Othaala]]'s most prominent centers of law and scholarship. [[Ronaela Astarise]] returned here after her southern expedition and was received by King Marien I, who commissioned her to publish her findings. Capital of the former nation of [[Otanlla]].
