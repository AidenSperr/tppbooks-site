# Suvrra

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A sizeable city north of [[Lumen]] on the [[Yavathre Plateau]], nestled between two dark forests. Its pale, thick-walled buildings rise to dozens of spires capped in oxidized copper. The city is famed for its perfume producers, whose scent drifts outward on the wind along with smoke and grain. A key waypoint on the main plateau highway connecting [[Lumen]], [[Taebahe]], and [[Kaedoa]]. Raomi passes through on his way to [[Voemna Pass]] in Book II, and the Birumiakese army camps north of the city before their march.
