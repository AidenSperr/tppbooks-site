# Almaej

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city near the [[Almaeji River]] in the [[Amekia Sector]], whose main industry is the mining and export of materials. The first major city to fall in the Huozhese invasion of Book II. Raomi Allamaera witnesses the line break and the walls consumed by fire before retreating south to warn [[Tuusalche]].
