# Dajaerve

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Town

## Overview
A small, dilapidated town at the base of the [[Slovaera Mountains]] where the [[Klovalmi Hills]] begin. Outer buildings are thin wood siding on stilts with rotted, sunken roofs; the grass is sparse and weedy; the dirt is brown-gray and muddy. The town has been economically drained, with crops failing and money siphoned off by a corrupt local figure living in notable comfort just outside of town. Arveshia describes it as a troubled place, possibly cursed, though she attributes its misery primarily to this individual. Has a central well, several inns (none of them particularly nice), and a recommended one: the Woodpecker Inn, one street west of Main. Anue and Chuntian spend time here in Book I seeking the person Arveshia sent them to find.
