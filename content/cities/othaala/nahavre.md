# Nahavre

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A large village on the eastern side of the [[Bhahare Mountains]] near the [[Besa Gap]] in the [[Lovante Sector]]. The first inland waypoint for evacuees coming from the eastern sectors through the Besa Gap. Refugees from [[Macaraalo]] pass through Nahavre before continuing to the coast.
