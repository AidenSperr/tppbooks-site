# Macaraalo

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Amekia Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large city in the northwest of the [[Amekia Sector]]. Used as a recuperation point for retreating Othaalan forces during the Huozhese invasion in Book II; Raomi sends the bulk of his retreating troops here while he continues south to [[Tuusalche]].
