# Thohvaer

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A small and lively village on a lake in the northern portion of the [[Yavathre Plateau]], in the [[Yavathre Sector]]. Home to Torrv Estata, Linia Estata, and their daughter Taenala during the events of Book I. Anue stays here for a period and receives a magical grimoire from Torrv before departing for [[Dajaerve]].

## Notable Residents
- Torrv Estata — resident; gives Anue a magical grimoire
- Linia Estata — resident; Torrv's partner
- Taenala Estata — their daughter
