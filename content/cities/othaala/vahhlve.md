# Vahhlve

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** Town

## Overview
A town in the [[Yavathre Sector]] at the western edge of the [[Traoniel Cliffs]] and eastern edge of the [[Klovalmi Hills]]. Built across a valley between four large hills, with nicer houses on the hillsides and the main settlement below. At its center stands the largest [[Arveshian Shrine]] on the continent, a towering structure easily four times taller than the largest surrounding building, open-air and grand in construction. The town is generally bustling and has a market, public well near the shrine square, bakeries, and inns. Visited and used as a resupply point by Raomi's convoy in Book I.

## Notable Residents
- [[Sa Paelnathe]] — caretaker of the Grand Shrine; sole regular attendant; lives in a log cabin on the first hill into town

## Notable Structures
- [[Arveshian Shrine]] — the largest in [[Othaala]]; towers over surrounding buildings; open-air construction; maintained by a single caretaker
