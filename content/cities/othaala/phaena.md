# Phaena

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Lovante Sector]]
> **Type:** Large City

## Overview
A large and old religious and naval city on the northern coast of the [[Lovante Sector]] near the [[Bay of Amekia]]. Notable in Book II as the origin of a scorched emergency letter delivered by a golden talvi messenger bird to Raomi at the Voemna camp, bearing urgent news of unknown content.
