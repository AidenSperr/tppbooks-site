# Kaedoa

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Yavathre Sector]]
> **Type:** City

## Overview
Commonly known as the City of Light, or *Halefel Lunan* in Othaani, situated within the [[Traoniel Cliffs]] in the [[Yavathre Sector]] under the [[Grand Olbaerd Tree]]. The [[Grand Olbaerd Tree]] is closely associated with [[Lavi]], the Goddess of Nature. The city is integrated with the natural structure of the cliffs and the tree's enormous canopy, making it one of the more visually distinct cities in [[Othaala]]. One of the few places on the continent known for an integrated, multiracial society. Used as a gathering point for the Gemwielders prior to their journey in Book I.
