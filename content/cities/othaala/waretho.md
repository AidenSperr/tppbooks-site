# Waretho

> **Nation:** [[Othaala]]
> **Region:** [[Warethoan Sector]]
> **Type:** Major City

## Overview
The largest city in the [[Warethoan Sector]], situated in the [[Gohna Swamps]]. One of the largest cities in [[Othaala]], used as a point of reference for scale when the Birumiakese army compares the size of [[Taebahe]] to it. Named as an evacuation destination during the Huozhese invasion.
