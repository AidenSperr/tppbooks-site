# Piaofuzhen

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Kashteni]]
> **Type:** Village

## Overview
A small village nestled in the southern [[Chomien Mountains]] in eastern [[Kashteni]]. Considered the holiest site of [[Kashtet]] worship on the continent and home to the largest [[Kashteti Shrine]] in [[Alatrya]]. Birthplace of [[Huolong]].

## Notable Residents
- [[Huolong]] — born here

## Notable Structures
- [[Kashteti Shrine]] — the largest shrine to [[Kashtet]] on the continent
