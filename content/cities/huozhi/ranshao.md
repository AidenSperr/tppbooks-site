# Ranshao

> **Nation:** [[Huozhi]]
> **Region:** [[Kashteni]]
> **Type:** Capital City

## Overview
The capital of [[Huozhi]] and seat of [[Huolong]]'s dictatorship. Situated in northern [[Kashteni]], with the port city of [[Lansecheng]] directly connected to its outskirts. Ranshao is a dark, working city, its walls blackened by fire decades past and worn like armor since. It does not glitter like [[Atema]] or [[Godascher]], but it does burn efficiently and constantly. The streets are organized into named districts, with the metal-focused Tunhao District in the south and the Xhatao District further north leading to [[Castle Tianshaobieng]] via Leina Street.

## Notable Residents
- [[Huolong]] — dictator of [[Huozhi]]; bearer of [[Kashtet]]'s Gem; resides in [[Castle Tianshaobieng]]
- [[Dhathrir Szakuvya]] — stayed temporarily as a guest and proposed ally to Huolong

## Notable Structures
- [[Castle Tianshaobieng]] — Huolong's palace; gigantic; carved basalt and ashy marble with gilded details; sixteen towers with bonfire-lit roofs; entered via a long staircase flanked by marble loviria statues

## Geographic Features
[[West Takromavian Ocean]] (via [[Lansecheng]]), [[Chomien Mountains]] (nearby)

## Notes
- Entry to the city from the south is via Luezhing Street through the Tunhao District, then Foanai Road right and Leina Street north to the palace
- The populace is visibly oppressed; crowds of protesting citizens have been noted at the palace steps
- Described as comparable to other capitals in scale but fundamentally different in character: industrial, dark, and militarized
